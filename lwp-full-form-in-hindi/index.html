LWP Full Form in Hindi: Leave Without Pay Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of LWP is Leave Without Pay, which translates into Hindi as अवैतनिक अवकाश (Avaitanik Avkash) or बिना वेतन की छुट्टी (Bina Vetan Ki Chhutti). In human resources management, corporate employment contracts, and government service regulations, LWP represents an authorized leave of absence granted to an employee during which they do not receive basic pay, allowances, or wages. It is typically sanctioned when an employee has exhausted all paid leave balances—such as casual leave, sick leave, and earned leave—but requires extended time off due to medical emergencies, higher education, sabbatical research, or critical personal circumstances.

LWP Full Form in Hindi: Meaning and Core HR Definition

LWP का फुल फॉर्म हिंदी में Leave Without Pay होता है, जिसे आधिकारिक रूप से 'अवैतनिक अवकाश' कहा जाता है। इसका सरल अर्थ है कि कर्मचारी को कार्यालय या सेवा से अनुपस्थित रहने की अनुमति तो दी जाती है, परंतु उस अवकाश अवधि के दौरान उसे कोई वेतन, महंगाई भत्ता (DA) या अन्य वित्तीय परिलब्धियां प्राप्त नहीं होती हैं। यह व्यवस्था सरकारी विभागों, सार्वजनिक उपक्रमों (PSUs) तथा निजी कॉर्पोरेट कंपनियों में मानव संसाधन नीतियों (HR Policies) का एक अनिवार्य अंग है।

While an employee on LWP does not draw a monthly paycheck, their legal employment relationship with the organization remains fully intact. Unlike unauthorized absence or job abandonment (absconding), which triggers disciplinary action and termination of service, LWP is a formally approved leave category. The employee retains their designation, employee code, health insurance benefits in select organizations, and right to resume active duty upon the expiration of the sanctioned leave duration.

Impact of LWP on Monthly Salary, Provident Fund, and Gratuity

Taking Leave Without Pay has immediate financial ramifications on monthly compensation and long-term terminal benefits. Under the Payment of Wages Act and standard payroll accounting systems, daily salary is calculated by dividing monthly cost-to-company or gross pay by the total calendar days or designated working days in that pay cycle. For every day spent on LWP, a proportionate deduction is applied across basic salary, house rent allowance (HRA), and special allowances.

Furthermore, because Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employee Pension Scheme (EPS) contributions are calculated as a statutory percentage of actual basic wages paid, months with significant LWP days see corresponding drops in retirement savings. Regarding gratuity entitlements under the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, taking substantial uninterrupted LWP may affect continuous service calculations if the employee fails to complete the statutory minimum of 240 working days within that financial year.

Legal Framework and Government Service Rules on Extraordinary Leave

In the Government of India civil service, Leave Without Pay is codified under Rule 32 of the Central Civil Services (CCS) Leave Rules 1972 as Extraordinary Leave (EOL). EOL is granted under two specific conditions: when no other leave is admissible under the rules, or when other leave is admissible but the government servant applies specifically in writing for EOL.

Under service rules, continuous periods of Extraordinary Leave taken on private affairs do not count towards qualifying service for annual salary increments, unless certified by the competent authority that the leave was compelled by illness beyond the employee's control or pursued for specialized technical education beneficial to government service. Understanding these legal nuances allows public and private sector employees to plan extended leaves without jeopardizing long-term career progression.

How to Apply for Leave Without Pay (LWP) Professionally

  1. Verify Existing Leave Balances

    Check your employee self-service portal to confirm that all paid casual, earned, and sick leave quotas have been fully utilized.

  2. Assemble Supporting Documentation

    Gather medical records, university admission letters, or caregiving certificates justifying the requirement for extended unpaid leave.

  3. Hold Prior Discussion with Reporting Manager

    Meet with your department head to discuss workload handovers, interim coverage, and anticipated return timelines before submitting the application.

  4. Submit Formal LWP Application via HRMS

    Log into your company HRMS system, select Leave Without Pay (LWP), attach your justification documents, and submit for executive approval.

  5. Obtain Written Confirmation and Resume Duty

    Secure formal approval documentation stating leave start and end dates, and complete joining formalities upon returning to office.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of LWP in Hindi?

LWP का फुल फॉर्म हिंदी में Leave Without Pay होता है, जिसे 'अवैतनिक अवकाश' या 'बिना वेतन की छुट्टी' कहते हैं।

Q2: Does taking LWP result in termination of employment?

No, LWP is an officially sanctioned leave where your job remains secure, but you do not receive salary for the days taken.

Q3: How is salary calculated during LWP?

For every day an employee is on approved LWP, one day's proportionate gross salary is deducted from their monthly pay cycle.

Q4: Does LWP affect Provident Fund (PF) contributions?

Yes, because PF is deducted as a percentage of actual basic wages earned, lower earnings during LWP lead to lower monthly PF deposits.

Q5: What is LWP called in Central Government service?

In Indian Central Civil Services (CCS), LWP is officially designated as Extraordinary Leave (EOL) under Rule 32.

Q6: Can an employee take LWP while paid leaves are still available?

Generally, company HR policies require exhausting all casual, earned, and medical leaves before an employee can request LWP.

Q7: Does LWP break continuous service for Gratuity?

If an employee works at least 240 days in a 12-month calendar cycle, continuous service for Gratuity remains intact despite occasional LWP.
