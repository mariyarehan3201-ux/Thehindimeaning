Is There a Full XL Mattress?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

Yes, there is absolutely a Full XL mattress. While not as universally stocked in traditional brick-and-mortar furniture retail showrooms as standard Twin, Full, Queen, or King sizes, the Full XL is an official, widely manufactured mattress size available through most major online mattress brands. Measuring 54 inches wide by 80 inches long, a Full XL mattress provides the exact same width as a standard Full bed, but adds five crucial inches of extra length to match the 80-inch legroom of a Queen or King mattress, making it the ultimate space-saving solution for tall solo sleepers.

Dimensional Comparison: Full XL vs. Standard Full and Queen

To understand why the Full XL mattress was engineered, one must examine the dimensional gaps in standard American bedding sizes. A traditional Standard Full mattress (historically known as a 'Double Bed') measures 54 inches wide by 75 inches long. While 54 inches provides ample elbow room for an individual sleeper, the 75-inch length is problematic for anyone taller than 5 feet 9 inches.

When you account for a standard pillow taking up the top 10 to 12 inches of the mattress, a six-foot-tall sleeper on a standard Full bed finds their feet dangling uncomfortably off the foot of the bed. Historically, the only alternative was upgrading to a Queen mattress (60 inches wide by 80 inches long). However, in compact urban apartments, studio lofts, guest rooms, or college dorms, adding six inches of width can block closet doors, obstruct nightstands, and overcrowd walking paths.

Who Is the Full XL Mattress Best Suited For?

The Full XL mattress occupies a unique ergonomic sweet spot, making it the perfect bedding solution for specific lifestyle and demographic profiles. First and foremost, it is the holy grail for tall solo sleepers. College students, young professionals, and single adults between 5 feet 10 inches and 6 feet 4 inches enjoy full Queen-length legroom while retaining the spatial efficiency of a Full-width mattress.

Second, Full XL mattresses are ideal for narrow or awkwardly configured bedrooms. In long, narrow urban apartment bedrooms or historic homes with tight doorways, saving six inches of floor width compared to a Queen creates vital walking clearance for dressers, desks, and bedroom doors. Third, Full XLs make outstanding guest beds, accommodating both tall solo visitors and occasional budget-conscious couples comfortably without overwhelming smaller spare bedrooms.

The Challenges: Finding Accessories, Bed Frames, and Bedding

While the physical dimensions of a Full XL are fantastic, prospective buyers must anticipate practical shopping challenges. Because Full XL is considered a specialized size, walking into a local department store to purchase fitted sheets, mattress protectors, or platform bed frames can be frustrating; physical retailers rarely stock Full XL bedding on retail shelves.

Fortunately, the explosion of direct-to-consumer e-commerce has made sourcing Full XL bedding effortless online. Leading mattress manufacturers—including Saatva, Helix, Brooklyn Bedding, Nectar, and Texas Pocket Springs—regularly manufacture Full XL mattresses in memory foam, hybrid, and innerspring models. Furthermore, online bedding retailers and Amazon stock thousands of Full XL deep-pocket cotton sheets, comforters, and adjustable bed bases, allowing you to furnish your bedroom seamlessly.

How to Shop for and Outfit a Full XL Mattress

Step-by-step guidance on sourcing, purchasing, and accessorizing a Full XL bed.

  1. Measure Your Bedroom Space and Door Clearances

    Measure your floor layout with a tape measure. Ensure you have at least 24 to 30 inches of walking clearance around the bed perimeter, verifying that 54x80 inches fits better than a 60x80 Queen.

  2. Select an Online Mattress Brand Offering Full XL

    Search reputable direct-to-consumer mattress brands (such as Brooklyn Bedding, Saatva, or Helix). Confirm that 'Full XL' is selectable in the size dropdown menu before ordering.

  3. Purchase an Adjustable or Dedicated Full XL Bed Frame

    Order a platform bed frame or adjustable power base specifically rated for Full XL (54x80 inches). Many universal metal bed frames feature sliding width pins and extendable length bars.

  4. Source Deep-Pocket Full XL Fitted Sheets Online

    Never use standard Full fitted sheets, as they will tear trying to stretch the extra 5 inches. Buy dedicated 'Full XL' sheets online, or purchase Queen flat sheets and comforters for generous drape.

  5. Install a Waterproof Full XL Mattress Protector

    Protect your mattress investment by encasing it in a breathable, waterproof Full XL mattress protector to shield against spills, dust mites, and maintain manufacturer warranty validity.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What are the exact dimensions of a Full XL mattress?

A Full XL mattress measures 54 inches wide by 80 inches long (137 cm x 203 cm), offering the width of a Full bed with the length of a Queen.

Q2: What is the difference between a Full and a Full XL mattress?

A Full XL mattress is exactly 5 inches longer than a standard Full mattress (80 inches vs. 75 inches), providing necessary legroom for taller individuals.

Q3: Can two people sleep on a Full XL mattress?

Two adults can sleep on a Full XL in guest settings, but each person only gets 27 inches of personal width (the width of a crib), making a Queen (60 inches) preferable for couples.

Q4: Do standard Full sheets fit a Full XL mattress?

No. Standard Full fitted sheets are 75 inches long and will pop off the corners of an 80-inch Full XL mattress. You must purchase dedicated Full XL fitted sheets.

Q5: Can you put a Full XL mattress on a Queen frame?

A Full XL will fit on a Queen frame length-wise, but there will be a 3-inch gap on each side where the frame is wider than the mattress. A dedicated or adjustable frame is recommended.

Q6: Why do college dorms use Twin XL instead of Full XL?

College dormitories use Twin XL (38x80 inches) because rooms are compact, maximizing floor space while still accommodating tall students with 80-inch lengths.

Q7: Are Full XL mattresses more expensive than standard Fulls?

Full XL mattresses typically cost about $50 to $150 more than a standard Full due to extra manufacturing materials and specialized dimensions.

Q8: Can you buy a box spring for a Full XL mattress?

Yes. Many mattress manufacturers produce matching 54x80 inch Full XL box springs and foundations, which are readily available through online retailers.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

In summary, the Full XL mattress is a real, highly practical, and versatile bedding size that bridges the gap between a standard Full and a Queen bed. Measuring 54 inches by 80 inches, it offers 80 inches of generous legroom for tall individuals while conserving valuable bedroom floor space. For tall teenagers, college students, and apartment dwellers seeking comfortable sleep without sacrificing walking clearance, a Full XL mattress represents the ultimate bedroom space-saving upgrade.
