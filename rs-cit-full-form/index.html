RS CIT Full Form: RKCL Computer Diploma Admission

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of RS CIT (or RSCIT) is Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology. Conceptualized and administered by Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL) in academic partnership with Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota, RS-CIT is the official state-approved public computer education course in Rajasthan. Designed to empower citizens, students, and professionals with fundamental computing literacy, the RS CIT certificate serves as the mandatory minimum computer qualification required for recruitment in numerous Rajasthan state civil services, clerical cadre positions, police administration, and revenue departments.

The integration of digital technology into public governance has transformed how governments interact with citizens. In Rajasthan, the state administration pioneered widespread digital service delivery through the e-Mitra citizen portal, Jan Aadhaar digital identity cards, and the Bhamashah biometric financial inclusion platform. To ensure that grassroots civil servants and public sector clerks operate these digital systems effectively, the Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology was instituted as a prerequisite for state employment.

RS CIT is administered by Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL), a public-private partnership promoted by the Government of Rajasthan. Through an extensive network of thousands of authorized IT Gyan Kendras (ITGKs) operating in every district, tehsil, and block, RKCL delivers standardized, high-quality IT training even in remote desert villages.

Evaluation in RS CIT follows a transparent two-tier assessment framework. Internal assessments conducted through online lab modules evaluate continuous attendance and daily homework exercises, while the final summative evaluation is managed centrally by Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota.

Understanding the grading system and score distribution ensures candidates focus on clearing both internal and external passing thresholds. The following table illustrates the VMOU RS CIT assessment and grading framework.

Completing the RS CIT course provides young graduates with an essential credential for state government careers. The practical digital skills acquired continue to provide lasting value across everyday administrative and commercial life.

How to Apply for RS CIT Course and Clear the VMOU Exam

  1. Select an Authorized RKCL Gyan Kendra

    Visit the official RKCL website (rkcl.in) to locate an accredited private or government IT Gyan Kendra (ITGK) training center in your locality.

  2. Submit Required KYC and Academic Documents

    Provide your 10th-grade mark sheet, recent passport photographs, Aadhaar card copy, and pay the state-standardized course fee.

  3. Attend Daily Theory and Lab Training

    Complete one hundred thirty-two hours of computer training spread across three months, covering Windows navigation, Microsoft Office, and cyber safety.

  4. Appear for the VMOU Statewide Exam

    Take the official objective multiple-choice examination conducted by VMOU Kota, scoring at least twenty-eight marks to earn your state diploma.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of RS CIT?

RS CIT stands for Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology, a foundational computer literacy course.

Q2: What is the fee for the RS CIT course in Rajasthan?

The standardized government course fee typically ranges around ₹3,350 to ₹3,500, with fee waivers available for government female empowerment schemes.

Q3: What is the passing mark in the RS CIT final exam?

Students must score at least 12 marks out of 30 in internal lab assignments, and 28 marks out of 70 in the VMOU final written examination.

Q4: Which government jobs require the RS CIT diploma?

Rajasthan Patwari, Police Constable, LDC (Lower Division Clerk), Gram Sevak, High Court Clerk, and Women Supervisor recruitments mandate RS CIT.

Q5: Can RS CIT be completed through online home study?

While study materials and digital apps are accessible online, students must complete practical lab hours at an authorized ITGK center.

Q6: What is the duration of the RS CIT training program?

The program spans 3 months, equivalent to 132 hours of combined classroom lectures and practical computer lab sessions.

Q7: Is there any negative marking in the RS CIT VMOU test?

No, the written test comprises thirty-five multiple-choice questions (two marks each) with zero negative marking.

Q8: How can candidates download the RS CIT digital certificate?

Once VMOU declares results, candidates can download verified digital e-certificates directly into their national DigiLocker accounts.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

RS CIT stands for Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology, the state premier computer literacy certification. Backed by the Government of Rajasthan, RKCL, and VMOU Kota, it equips students with practical office automation, internet navigation, and e-governance skills required for modern civil service posts. Enrolling in an authorized IT Gyan Kendra opens valuable pathways to public employment and digital empowerment.
