The Foundational Mission and Scope of the Global Healthy Living Foundation

In healthcare advocacy, clinical medical policy, and patient education, the acronym GHLF universally designates the Global Healthy Living Foundation. Founded in 1999 by arthritis patient advocate Seth Ginsberg and social entrepreneur Louis Tharp, GHLF is an international non-profit organization (501(c)(3)) headquartered in Upper Nyack, New York, dedicated to improving the quality of life for millions of individuals living with chronic, autoimmune, and musculoskeletal illnesses.

Unlike traditional charitable medical organizations that focus predominantly on funding laboratory bench research, GHLF concentrates heavily on patient advocacy, education, clinical trial accessibility, and healthcare public policy. GHLF empowers patients diagnosed with complex chronic conditions—such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, migraine, inflammatory bowel disease, and osteoporosis—to become informed self-advocates in clinical decision-making with their healthcare providers.

Operating internationally across North America, Latin America, Europe, and Australia, GHLF bridges the critical gap between complex medical research, clinical drug access, and the daily lived experiences of patients.

Public Policy and Legislative Advocacy: Fighting for Patient Access

A defining hallmark of GHLF is its active involvement in healthcare policy. Through its grassroots '50-State Network', GHLF mobilizes chronic disease patients to engage directly with state and federal lawmakers, insurance commissioners, and regulatory bodies.

GHLF advocates vigorously against restrictive health insurance practices that impede patient recovery. Key policy priorities include combating step therapy ('fail first' protocols)—where insurance companies force patients to try and fail cheaper, older medications before approving the doctor-prescribed biologic—and capping predatory copay accumulator programs that prevent manufacturer assistance cards from counting toward patient deductibles.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does GHLF stand for?

GHLF stands for the Global Healthy Living Foundation, an international non-profit organization advocating for chronic disease patients.

Q2: What is CreakyJoints?

CreakyJoints is GHLF's flagship digital patient community, providing education, support, and advocacy for people with arthritis and rheumatic diseases.

Q3: What is ArthritisPower?

ArthritisPower is a patient-powered research registry app created by GHLF and the University of Alabama at Birmingham to study real-world autoimmune symptoms.

Q4: Who founded GHLF?

GHLF was co-founded in 1999 by Seth Ginsberg (who was diagnosed with spondyloarthritis at age 17) and social entrepreneur Louis Tharp.

Q5: Is GHLF a registered charity?

Yes, GHLF is an official 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in Upper Nyack, New York.

Q6: What conditions does GHLF support?

GHLF supports patients with rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, lupus, migraines, ankylosing spondylitis, Crohn's, and cardiovascular diseases.

Q7: What is step therapy that GHLF fights against?

Step therapy (or 'fail first') is an insurance rule forcing patients to fail on cheaper drugs before the insurer agrees to cover the doctor's prescribed medicine.

Q8: Does GHLF charge patients for membership?

No, all patient education, community forums, tracking apps, and support webinars provided by GHLF and CreakyJoints are 100% free.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Global Healthy Living Foundation (GHLF) is an inspiring beacon of patient-centered healthcare advocacy. By uniting millions of patients through platforms like CreakyJoints, collecting real-world patient research through ArthritisPower, and defending medication access in state legislatures, GHLF ensures that the patient voice remains at the center of modern medicine.
