How Many Cubic Yards Is a Bag of Mulch?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how many cubic yards is a bag of mulch requires converting between cubic feet (the standard measurement on retail bagged mulch) and cubic yards (the standard unit for bulk landscaping). A standard 2-cubic-foot bag of mulch contains approximately 0.074 cubic yards, meaning it takes exactly 13.5 bags to equal one full cubic yard. For larger 3-cubic-foot commercial bags, one bag equals approximately 0.111 cubic yards, requiring 9 bags to make one cubic yard.

The Mathematical Conversion: Cubic Feet to Cubic Yards

To understand the mathematical relationship between bagged mulch and bulk deliveries, one must start with foundational volume geometry. One cubic yard is a three-dimensional cube measuring 3 feet long, 3 feet wide, and 3 feet high (3 ft x 3 ft x 3 ft). Multiplying these dimensions yields exactly 27 cubic feet in one cubic yard.

Retail garden centers—such as Home Depot, Lowe's, and local nurseries—sell bagged organic mulch (cedar, shredded hardwood, cypress, black dyed, or pine bark nuggets) almost exclusively in 2-cubic-foot bags. To convert a 2-cubic-foot bag into cubic yards, divide 2 by 27, which equals 0.07407 cubic yards. To determine how many bags make a full yard, divide 27 by 2, which gives exactly 13.5 bags.

Bagged vs. Bulk Mulch: Cost, Convenience, and Labor Comparison

Homeowners landscaping their garden beds frequently debate whether to purchase individual bags or order a dump-truck delivery of bulk mulch. The deciding factor usually comes down to total volume and physical project logistics. For small garden refreshing projects requiring under 2 to 3 cubic yards (under 27 to 40 bags), buying bags is often significantly more convenient. Bags can be tossed into an SUV trunk, transported clean, and carried directly to individual plant beds without shovel work.

However, for larger landscaping overhauls requiring 5, 10, or 20 cubic yards, purchasing in bulk from a local landscape supply yard is dramatically more cost-effective. Bulk mulch typically costs $30 to $45 per cubic yard, whereas purchasing 13.5 two-cubic-foot bags at $3.50 to $4.50 per bag costs between $47 and $60 per cubic yard—a 30% to 50% price markup for the plastic packaging.

How to Calculate How Many Bags of Mulch You Need

To calculate exactly how many bags or cubic yards of mulch your property requires, use the standard landscape volume formula: Area (Square Feet) multiplied by Mulch Depth (Feet). Horticultural experts universally recommend maintaining a mulch layer 2 to 3 inches deep across garden beds. A 3-inch depth provides ideal weed suppression, soil moisture retention, and root insulation without suffocating plant crowns.

One cubic yard of mulch spread at a 3-inch depth covers precisely 108 square feet of garden area (at a 2-inch depth, it covers 162 square feet). Therefore, if your front planting beds total 500 square feet and you desire a 3-inch blanket, divide 500 by 108, which equals 4.63 cubic yards. Multiplying 4.63 by 13.5 bags indicates you will need approximately 63 standard two-cubic-foot bags.

How to Calculate and Order Mulch for Your Garden

Follow these five simple steps to calculate your exact mulch needs and avoid over-purchasing.

  1. Measure Length and Width of Each Garden Bed

    Multiply length by width in feet to calculate the square footage of all beds, then add them together.

  2. Choose Your Desired Mulch Depth (Typically 2 to 3 Inches)

    Select 3 inches for fresh weed-prone beds or 1 to 2 inches for an annual top-dress freshening.

  3. Divide Total Square Footage by 108 (For 3-Inch Depth)

    Calculate cubic yards needed (e.g., 600 sq ft divided by 108 = 5.5 cubic yards).

  4. Multiply Cubic Yards by 13.5 for 2 Cu Ft Bags

    Convert yards to bags (e.g., 5.5 cubic yards x 13.5 = 74 standard 2-cubic-foot bags).

  5. Decide Between Buying Bags or Ordering Bulk Delivery

    If needing more than 40 bags (3 yards), compare bulk delivery costs against loading retail bags.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How many cubic yards is a 2 cubic foot bag of mulch?

A 2-cubic-foot bag of mulch is equal to 0.074 cubic yards (2 divided by 27).

Q2: How many 2 cu ft bags of mulch make a cubic yard?

It takes exactly 13.5 two-cubic-foot bags of mulch to make one full cubic yard.

Q3: How many 3 cu ft bags of mulch make a cubic yard?

It takes exactly 9 three-cubic-foot bags of mulch to equal one cubic yard (27 divided by 3).

Q4: How much square footage does one bag of mulch cover?

A standard 2-cubic-foot bag covers 8 square feet at a 3-inch depth, or 12 square feet at a 2-inch depth.

Q5: How many bags of mulch fit in an SUV?

A typical midsize to large SUV can comfortably fit 12 to 18 two-cubic-foot bags with rear seats folded down.

Q6: How many bags of mulch fit in a pickup truck bed?

A standard 6.5-foot pickup truck bed can carry 35 to 45 bags of mulch (approx. 2.5 to 3 cubic yards).

Q7: Is bulk mulch cheaper than bagged mulch?

Yes, bulk mulch is usually 30% to 50% cheaper per cubic yard than bagged mulch, though delivery fees apply for small orders.

Q8: How heavy is a 2 cubic foot bag of mulch?

A dry bag weighs 30 to 35 pounds, while a rain-soaked bag can weigh 45 to 50 pounds.
