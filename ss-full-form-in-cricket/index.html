SS Full Form in Cricket: Sareen Sports Bat Brands

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of SS in cricket stands for Sareen Sports. Recognized across the sporting world under the globally famous brand name SS Sunridges, Sareen Sports is one of the premier and most historic manufacturers of world-class cricket equipment based in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India. Founded in 1969 by master craftsman N.K. Sareen, the company earned universal acclaim for precision hand-crafted cricket bats sculpted from premium unbleached English and Kashmir willow clefts. SS bats have been wielded by legendary international cricketing icons including Sourav Ganguly, VVS Laxman, MS Dhoni, Yuvraj Singh, Shikhar Dhawan, and Kieron Pollard. Renowned for substantial bat profiles, enlarged sweet spots, balanced pick-ups, and distinctive Sunridges embossing, the SS monogram represents elite craftsmanship, robust durability, and traditional bat-making heritage.

The Heritage and Legacy of Sareen Sports in World Cricket

Cricket is a sport where equipment craftsmanship directly influences athletic performance. The origin of Sareen Sports in Meerut, India, dates back to 1969 when founder N.K. Sareen laid the groundwork for a family-run athletic equipment workshop. Over decades of dedicated refinement, Meerut emerged as a global capital for sports manufacturing, with Sareen Sports leading the charge in engineering cricket bats capable of withstanding the punishing power of contemporary international cricket.

The SS monogram and the Sunridges insignia have become ubiquitous fixtures in international test matches, one-day internationals, and domestic T20 franchise leagues across India, England, Australia, and the Caribbean. Today, the company pairs state-of-the-art computer-controlled profiling machinery with veteran master podshavers who shape each cleft by hand.

Engineering Anatomy of an SS Cricket Bat

What differentiates a world-class SS cricket bat from ordinary alternatives is the calculated distribution of wood across the blade. Master bat-makers at Sareen Sports sculpt bats with oversized edges ranging between 38 and 42 millimeters, without compromising the overall pickup or making the bat feel bottom-heavy.

The iconic duckbill toe design tapers excess weight from the lower corners while redistributing mass into the mid-to-low hitting swell. Furthermore, SS incorporates multi-piece imported cane handles with specialized rubber and cork dampening inserts. This handle construction absorbs vibrational shock on mis-timed shots while maximizing the mechanical transfer of torque during explosive boundary strikes.

Global Recognition and Legendary International Endorsements

The international pedigree of Sareen Sports is cemented by the stellar roster of legendary batters who have entrusted their careers to SS bats. In historic World Cup triumphs and high-stakes test series, batsmen have favored the generous sweet spots and unmatched balance of Ton and SS profiles.

Even when global apparel multinationals signed multi-million dollar promotional deals with superstar cricketers, numerous players famously insisted on continuing to play with their custom-crafted SS willow blades beneath corporate sticker wraps. This quiet professional preference serves as the greatest testament to the unmatched engineering standards maintained at the Meerut factory.

How to Properly Knock-In and Prepare an SS Cricket Bat

  1. Apply a Light Layer of Raw Linseed Oil

    Pour a 10-cent coin sized amount of raw linseed oil onto the face and edges of the willow blade, buffing it evenly with a soft cloth while strictly avoiding the bat splice and handle.

  2. Let the Willow Cure Horizontally Overnight

    Allow the oiled bat to rest horizontally face-up in a cool, dry room for at least twenty-four hours so the wood fibers absorb the moisture and regain elasticity.

  3. Gradually Compact Wood Fibers with a Wooden Mallet

    Gently strike the face and toe of the bat with a specialized wooden cricket mallet, gradually increasing impact velocity over four to six hours to compress the willow cells.

  4. Conduct Controlled Net Sessions with Seasoned Leather Balls

    Perform short defensive batting drills against aged, soft leather cricket balls before subjecting the new bat to high-speed match bowling conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of SS in cricket bats?

In cricket, the full form of SS stands for Sareen Sports, the prestigious sports goods manufacturing company headquartered in Meerut, India.

Q2: When was Sareen Sports established?

Sareen Sports was founded in the year 1969 by entrepreneur and master sports goods craftsman N.K. Sareen in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh.

Q3: What is the difference between SS and SS Sunridges?

They refer to the identical brand; Sunridges is the primary registered trademark brand name under which Sareen Sports markets its equipment.

Q4: What wood is used to manufacture top-tier SS bats?

Top-tier professional SS cricket bats are carved from Grade 1 and Grade 1+ English willow (Salix alba caerulea) imported directly from the United Kingdom.

Q5: Which famous cricketers have used SS cricket bats?

Prominent cricketers who have used SS bats include MS Dhoni, Sourav Ganguly, Rohit Sharma, Yuvraj Singh, Kieron Pollard, and Kumar Sangakkara.

Q6: What is the Ton brand name related to SS?

TON is another premium marquee sub-brand manufactured by Sareen Sports, widely visible on bat edges and stickers used by international batters.

Q7: Does Sareen Sports produce equipment other than bats?

Yes, SS produces batting gloves, leg guards, helmets, kit bags, leather balls, wicketkeeping gear, and technical cricket apparel.

Q8: How can one verify the authenticity of an original SS bat?

Authentic SS bats feature laser-engraved serial codes, 3D holographic verification stickers, and QR validation tags linked to the official Sareen Sports database.

The SS full form represents Sareen Sports, an enduring icon of Indian sporting craftsmanship that has conquered cricket grounds across the globe. Through relentless dedication to uncompromised willow selection, traditional hand-shaping, and modern protective design, SS Sunridges continues to equip international superstars and aspiring young cricketers with championship-winning gear.
