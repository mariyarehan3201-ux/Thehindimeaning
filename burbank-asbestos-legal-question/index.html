Aerospace Manufacturing and Studio Facilities in Burbank

Burbank's industrial history during the twentieth century was dominated by the Lockheed Aircraft Corporation. Lockheed's massive manufacturing complex at Burbank Airport produced military aircraft, commercial airliners, and reconnaissance planes. Tens of thousands of workers assembled aircraft electrical systems, engines, braking mechanisms, and structural heat shields. Friction brake assemblies, electrical wire insulation, engine firewalls, and cockpit ductwork utilized heavy concentrations of chrysotile and amosite asbestos.

In addition to aerospace production, Burbank hosts major motion picture and television studios—such as Warner Bros., Disney Studios, and NBC Studios. The construction and maintenance of massive soundstages, mechanical equipment rooms, and central refrigeration plants relied extensively on spray-applied acoustic plaster, structural fireproofing, pipe lagging, and stage electrical cable insulation. Carpenters, studio electricians, and HVAC technicians working in unventilated soundstages were routinely exposed to toxic dust clouds during stage remodeling.

California Asbestos Law and Los Angeles County Superior Court Procedures

Asbestos personal injury and wrongful death claims in Burbank are filed in the Los Angeles County Superior Court, which maintains a specialized, highly experienced asbestos litigation docket. Under California Code of Civil Procedure (CCP) Section 340.2, the statute of limitations for personal injury asbestos claims is one year from the date the claimant first suffered disability and either knew, or through reasonable diligence should have known, that the disability was caused by asbestos exposure.

For retirees or individuals not actively employed, California courts apply an exceptionally generous interpretation that protects claimants whose illness manifests decades after exposure. For wrongful death actions, surviving heirs must file within one year of the date of death. Crucially, under California CCP Section 36, elderly plaintiffs (age 70 or older) or terminally ill claimants diagnosed with mesothelioma can petition for trial preference, which legally compels the court to commence trial within 120 days.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is California's statute of limitations for an asbestos lawsuit in Burbank?

Under California CCP § 340.2, plaintiffs have one year from the date of disability and diagnosis, or one year from death for wrongful death claims.

Q2: What is trial preference under California CCP Section 36?

Trial preference allows terminally ill or elderly asbestos plaintiffs to have their cases brought to trial within 120 days of petition approval.

Q3: Can Lockheed Burbank aerospace workers file asbestos claims?

Yes, machinists, assemblers, and mechanics exposed to asbestos friction parts and thermal insulation at Lockheed can recover compensation.

Q4: Where do Burbank residents go for specialized mesothelioma treatment?

Patients frequently receive care at UCLA Medical Center, Keck Medicine of USC, or City of Hope Comprehensive Cancer Center.

Q5: Can entertainment studio workers in Burbank sue for asbestos exposure?

Yes, studio electricians, grips, and stage carpenters exposed to asbestos soundstage insulation can pursue claims.

Q6: Can family members in Burbank sue for take-home asbestos exposure?

Yes, California courts permit family members who inhaled fibers from laundering a worker's contaminated clothing to file claims.

Q7: Do I have to appear in a courtroom if I file an asbestos lawsuit?

Rarely; most cases settle out of court, and video depositions are routinely conducted in the comfort of your Burbank home.

Q8: How much does it cost to hire an asbestos attorney in Burbank?

Attorneys work on contingency, meaning clients pay no upfront fees and legal costs are deducted only from successful recoveries.
