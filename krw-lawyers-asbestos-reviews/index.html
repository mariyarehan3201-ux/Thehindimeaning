Firm Background and Asbestos Practice Profile

Based in San Antonio, Texas, with a nationwide toxic tort practice, KRW Lawyers has spent decades focusing on complex mass torts, catastrophic injury claims, and asbestos-related disease compensation. The firm maintains specialized litigation teams dedicated exclusively to investigating occupational exposure histories, filing claims with established asbestos bankruptcy trust funds, and pursuing civil lawsuits against solvent equipment manufacturers, chemical refineries, and commercial contractors.

A defining operational feature highlighted in client evaluations is the firm's comprehensive investigative infrastructure. KRW employs dedicated staff investigators, occupational historians, and medical record specialists who reconstruct detailed employment chronologies for tradespeople across petrochemical plants, military shipyards, and construction projects.

Navigating Contingency Fees and Retaining Counsel

Like other leading national asbestos litigation firms, KRW Lawyers operates on a contingency fee model. Under this structure, clients pay zero out-of-pocket costs or hourly legal fees. The firm advances all case expenses—including hiring medical expert witnesses, retrieving medical records, taking depositions, and filing court motions. Legal fees are calculated as a pre-agreed percentage of the total gross settlement or jury verdict, and fees are collected only if the firm successfully recovers money for the client.

Prospective clients evaluating reviews should also consider the importance of scheduling an initial case consultation to ask specific questions regarding their unique exposure background. Asking about past settlement results for your specific trade, estimated case timelines, and how the firm handles healthcare subrogation liens will give you total confidence before signing a retainer agreement, ensuring your family's legal interests are protected by dedicated professionals.

How to Evaluate and Retain an Asbestos Law Firm

Follow these strategic steps to review, evaluate, and partner with top-tier asbestos legal representation.

  1. Research Independent Client Reviews

    Examine verified client reviews on Google, Better Business Bureau (BBB), and legal directories like Martindale-Hubbell and Avvo.

  2. Schedule a Free Consultation

    Contact the firm to schedule a no-obligation consultation, requesting a home or virtual visit if health issues prevent travel.

  3. Inquire About Job Site Experience

    Ask whether the firm has documented historical exposure evidence and past successful claims for your specific employers and trade unions.

  4. Review the Contingency Fee Agreement

    Examine the representation contract carefully, confirming that fees and advanced litigation expenses are deducted only upon a successful financial recovery.

  5. Authorize Direct Exposure Investigation

    Execute formal medical authorizations and collaborate with firm investigators to reconstruct an unassailable record of toxic product contact.
