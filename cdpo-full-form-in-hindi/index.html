CDPO Full Form in Hindi: Child Development Officer

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of CDPO in Hindi is चाइल्ड डेवलपमेंट प्रोजेक्ट ऑफिसर (बाल विकास परियोजना अधिकारी). In English, CDPO stands for Child Development Project Officer. In state public administration and the Department of Women and Child Development, the CDPO is a gazetted administrative civil service officer (Class II) charged with the executive management, field monitoring, and logistical execution of the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) scheme across an administrative block or sub-district. The CDPO oversees hundreds of rural and urban Anganwadi centers, directing supplementary nutritional programs, maternal health tracking, childhood immunization drives, and early childhood preschool education to eradicate malnutrition and promote maternal wellness.

The Strategic Mandate of Child Development Project Officers

A nation socioeconomic vitality depends fundamentally on the physical health, cognitive development, and nutritional well-being of its youngest citizens. Children who suffer from chronic stunting, micro-nutrient deficiencies, or anemia during their formative years face permanent cognitive setbacks. Recognizing this existential priority, the Government of India launched the landmark Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) program in 1975, establishing the world largest grassroots community wellness initiative.

At the operational heart of ICDS stands the Child Development Project Officer. Operating as the executive head of an administrative block project, the CDPO translates government social policies into measurable field outcomes, ensuring that pregnant women, nursing mothers, and infants receive life-saving nutrition and primary healthcare.

Administrative Roles and Field Responsibilities of a CDPO

The daily administrative portfolio of a CDPO balances high-level inter-departmental coordination with rigorous surprise inspections at village Anganwadi centers.

Administrative Focus Domain Core Operational Tasks Executed Key Socioeconomic Deliverables
Anganwadi Oversight Surprise inspections, auditing attendance registers, sanitation checks Eliminating leakages; ensuring accountable grassroots public service
Child Malnutrition Eradication Monitoring height/weight charts; classifying SAM and MAM children Referring severely malnourished infants to Nutrition Rehabilitation Centers
Maternal Health Support Facilitating PMMVY benefits, distributing iron-folic acid supplements Drastic reduction in Maternal Mortality Ratios and low birth-weights
Early Childhood Education Supervising play-way non-formal preschool modules for ages 3 to 6 Accelerating cognitive readiness for primary school admissions
Adolescent Girls Empowerment Implementing Scheme for Adolescent Girls (SAG) and anemia screenings Elevating health literacy and curbing child marriage practices

Administrative Hierarchy in Women and Child Development Departments

Understanding the vertical administrative hierarchy demonstrates how the CDPO interfaces with district magistrates and ground-level Anganwadi workers.

Administrative Cadre Jurisdictional Governance Level Primary Functional Authority
District Programme Officer (DPO) District Level (Entire Revenue District) District-wide budget allocation, policy direction, inter-agency liaison
Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) Block / Project Level (100-250 Centers) Project executive command, field inspections, personnel management
Female Supervisor (Mukhya Sevika) Circle Sector Level (20-25 Centers) Direct ground supervision, spot checks, training Anganwadi workers
Anganwadi Worker (AWW) Village / Ward Level (~1,000 Population) Daily nutrition distribution, early education, home surveys
Anganwadi Helper (AWH) Center Level (Local Anganwadi Hub) Cooking hot meals, maintaining center cleanliness, gathering toddlers

Recruitment Pathways, PSC Examinations, and Career Growth

The post of CDPO is highly sought after by civil service aspirants due to its gazetted authority, competitive remuneration, and immense societal impact. State Public Service Commissions conduct multi-tier selection processes comprising preliminary screening tests, descriptive mains examinations, and comprehensive personal interviews.

Following years of meritorious field administration, high-performing CDPOs are promoted to District Programme Officers (DPO), Deputy Directors, and Joint Directors within the Directorate of Women and Child Development, actively shaping state-level child protection and welfare policies.

How to Clear State PSC Exams and Become a CDPO

  1. Attain a Recognized Bachelor Degree

    Graduate with a bachelor degree in any discipline; degrees in Sociology, Home Science, Social Work, or Psychology offer distinct advantages.

  2. Apply for State Public Service Commission CDPO Notifications

    Register online when state commissions (such as BPSC, UPPSC, or JPSC) publish official gazetted recruitment notices for CDPO vacancies.

  3. Clear Preliminary and Main Written Examinations

    Excel in competitive papers covering General Studies, Current Affairs, Child Welfare Policies, and chosen optional specialized subjects.

  4. Clear Personal Interview and Document Verification

    Demonstrate administrative aptitude and social sensitivity during the final interview board to receive formal appointment as a Block CDPO.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the complete full form of CDPO in Hindi and English?

In Hindi, it is बाल विकास परियोजना अधिकारी; in English, it is Child Development Project Officer.

Q2: What administrative rank does a CDPO hold in government service?

A CDPO is a Class-II Gazetted Administrative Officer under the state Department of Women and Child Development.

Q3: What is the minimum educational eligibility required to become a CDPO?

Candidates must hold an undergraduate bachelor degree from a recognized university in any academic stream.

Q4: Which public agency conducts the recruitment for CDPO posts?

State Public Service Commissions (such as BPSC, UPPSC, MPPSC, and HPPSC) conduct official competitive examinations for CDPO posts.

Q5: How many Anganwadi centers fall under a single CDPO jurisdiction?

Depending on geographic block size and population density, a CDPO typically supervises between 100 to 250 Anganwadi centers.

Q6: What are the core administrative duties of a CDPO?

Duties include inspecting Anganwadi centers, monitoring take-home rations, tracking child growth charts, and executing POSHAN Abhiyaan.

Q7: What is the starting salary of a CDPO in India?

Under 7th Pay Commission Pay Matrix Level 9 (Grade Pay 5400), initial monthly remuneration spans between ₹65,000 to ₹85,000.

Q8: Can both male and female candidates apply for the post of CDPO?

Yes, in most states both male and female candidates are eligible, though certain states offer specific gender reservation quotas.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The CDPO full form in Hindi represents बाल विकास परियोजना अधिकारी (Child Development Project Officer), an esteemed administrative role dedicated to uplifting vulnerable women and children. By directing nutrition campaigns, supervising Anganwadi centers, and championing maternal health rights, CDPOs build the foundation for a healthier, more prosperous nation.
