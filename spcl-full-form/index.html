SPCL Full Form: Shapoorji Pallonji and Shivalic Power

Full Form

The full form of SPCL in civil infrastructure, construction engineering, and corporate enterprise is Shapoorji Pallonji and Company Limited (frequently abbreviated as SPCPL or SPCL). Founded in 1865, it is a world-renowned 160-year-old engineering and construction conglomerate. What Is SPCL and What Does It Stand For in Infrastructure?

In global civil engineering, architectural mega-structures, and construction contracting, SPCL stands for Shapoorji Pallonji and Company Limited. Established in 1865 in Mumbai, the Shapoorji Pallonji Group is one of India's oldest, largest, and most respected multinational business conglomerates. Operating across more than forty countries in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and Asia, SPCL has shaped urban skylines through iconic, historic civil engineering projects.

From constructing the historic Reserve Bank of India heritage building, the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Phiroze Jeejeebhoy Towers, and the majestic Qasr Al Alam Palace for the Sultan of Oman in Muscat, to erecting the world's tallest residential twin towers (The Imperial in Mumbai), SPCL delivers turnkey engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions for super-tall skyscrapers, deep-sea ports, metro rail systems, and hydro-electric mega-dams.

SPCL in Electrical Engineering: Shivalic Power Control Limited

In industrial electrical engineering and power distribution, SPCL also stands for Shivalic Power Control Limited. Headquartered in Faridabad, Haryana, and publicly listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE: SPCL), Shivalic Power Control is an ISO-certified manufacturer specializing in custom-engineered low-voltage (LV) and medium-voltage (MV) electrical switchboards and automation panels.

Operating a modernized manufacturing facility spanning over 100,000 square feet, the company manufactures power control center (PCC) panels, motor control center (MCC) panels, intelligent smart panels, synchronization panels, and solar AC/DC combiner boxes certified under international IEC standards. The table below illustrates the primary electrical equipment manufactured by Shivalic Power Control Limited.

Engineering Excellence and Quality Standards

Whether executing complex civil works on the world's highest railway arch bridge (the Chenab Bridge executed by Afcons / SPCL) or manufacturing precision type-tested switchgear panels compliant with IEC 61439-1/2, engineering excellence is the core hallmark of SPCL organizations.

Both enterprises implement rigorous quality management frameworks certified under ISO 9001, ISO 14001 (environmental sustainability), and ISO 45001 (occupational health and workplace safety), ensuring projects withstand extreme physical demands and deliver decades of continuous service.

How EPC Contractors Procure Custom Switchgear Panels from SPCL

  1. Prepare Electrical Single Line Diagram (SLD)

    Formulate the project's single line electrical diagram detailing main transformer ratings, prospective short-circuit fault levels, and feeder capacities.

  2. Submit Technical Specifications for Panel Quotation

    Send engineering requirements to Shivalic Power Control Limited (SPCL), specifying ingress protection (IP) ratings and internal separation forms (Form 4b).

  3. Review and Approve General Arrangement (GA) Drawings

    Scrutinize engineering CAD drawings, panel dimension envelopes, busbar sizing calculations, and feeder layouts before production release.

  4. Conduct Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

    Visit the SPCL manufacturing facility to conduct high-voltage insulation tests, primary current injection, and operational sequence tests.

  5. Authorize Dispatch and Coordinate Site Commissioning

    Approve transport dispatch, install panels on base channels, connect cable terminations, and complete final site acceptance testing (SAT).

Q2: What is SPCL in electrical manufacturing?
