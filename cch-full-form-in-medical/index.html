CCH Full Form in Medical: Homoeopathy Council

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

In Indian medical administration and clinical education, the full form of CCH is Central Council of Homoeopathy. Constituted as a statutory body by the Government of India under the Homoeopathy Central Council Act of 1973, the CCH was established to formulate uniform educational standards, prescribe minimum curricular requirements, inspect homoeopathic medical colleges, and maintain the central register of qualified homoeopathic practitioners nationwide. While the CCH served as the principal regulatory watchdog for decades, it was officially dissolved in 2021 and superseded by the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH) to modernize alternative healthcare governance.

Homoeopathy occupies an integral position within the Indian healthcare framework, boasting one of the world largest institutional networks of homoeopathic hospitals, dispensaries, and research institutes. Before the enactment of the Homoeopathy Central Council Act in 1973, homoeopathic education across various states lacked uniform standards. Institutions offered varying course durations, diverse syllabi, and conflicting qualification titles, creating widespread confusion among the public and the medical fraternity.

The establishment of the Central Council of Homoeopathy introduced a unified national standard. The CCH standardized the five-and-a-half-year BHMS curriculum, mandated minimum hospital bed-to-student ratios, established laboratory requirements, and instituted ethical codes of professional conduct. Under the modern National Commission for Homoeopathy, the regulatory machinery is divided into four autonomous autonomous boards: the Homoeopathy Education Board, the Medical Assessment and Rating Board, the Board of Ethics and Registration, and the Advisory Council. This structural division prevents conflicts of interest by separating the bodies that set academic curricula from those that inspect college facilities and enforce ethical standards.

Understanding the differences between the legacy CCH model and the contemporary NCH framework clarifies how regulatory oversight has been strengthened. The table below outlines key institutional differences between the two governance systems.

The legacy of the Central Council of Homoeopathy lives on in the standardized academic foundation it established across Indian medical colleges. By establishing robust minimum educational requirements, it helped homoeopathy evolve into a respected, institutionalized clinical science that serves millions of patients every day.

How to Verify Homoeopathic Medical Degrees and Practitioner Credentials

  1. Access the National Commission for Homoeopathy Portal

    Navigate to the official NCH portal (formerly CCH) to access the centralized National Register of Homoeopathic Practitioners.

  2. Search by Practitioner Registration Number

    Enter the doctor state or central council registration number to verify active clinical licensing status and verified medical qualifications.

  3. Verify College Affiliation and Recognition Status

    Cross-reference the issuing homoeopathic institution against the list of NCH-approved colleges to confirm degree authenticity.

  4. Report Fraudulent or Unregistered Practitioners

    Submit formal complaints regarding unregistered individuals or unaccredited institutions to state medical councils or regional AYUSH directorates.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of CCH in the medical sector?

In the medical field, CCH stands for Central Council of Homoeopathy, the historic statutory regulator for homeopathic education in India.

Q2: Under which legislative act was the CCH constituted?

The CCH was constituted under the Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973 enacted by the Parliament of India.

Q3: Is the Central Council of Homoeopathy still functioning today?

The CCH was dissolved in 2021 and replaced by a modern statutory body called the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH).

Q4: What was the primary statutory role of CCH?

The CCH regulated the curriculum, college infrastructure, faculty standards, and national professional registration for homoeopathy.

Q5: Which academic degrees were regulated under CCH guidelines?

The CCH regulated the Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS) and postgraduate MD (Homoeopathy) degrees.

Q6: Can an institution offer BHMS degrees without council approval?

No, offering homoeopathic medical education without council accreditation is illegal and degrees granted are null and void.

Q7: What ministry supervised the Central Council of Homoeopathy?

The CCH functioned under the administrative oversight of the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India.

Q8: What alternate meanings does CCH have in international medicine?

In clinical diagnostics, CCH can stand for Congenital Cortical Hyperostosis or Certified Clinical Homeopath in the United States.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

In medical education, CCH stands for Central Council of Homoeopathy, the historic statutory watchdog that standardized homeopathic curricula, college infrastructure, and professional registration in India for nearly five decades. Though succeeded by the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH) in 2021, understanding the regulatory foundation established by CCH provides critical context for evaluating legitimate alternative medical credentials.
