How Long Is a VW Tiguan?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how long is a vw tiguan requires looking at its specific generation, wheelbase configuration, and regional market specifications. In the North American market, the second-generation Volkswagen Tiguan (the long-wheelbase Tiguan Allspace variant) measures precisely 186.1 inches (15.5 feet or 4,728 millimeters) in overall exterior bumper-to-bumper length. This elongated platform makes the North American Tiguan one of the largest compact crossover SUVs in its class, even allowing Volkswagen to offer an optional third-row 7-passenger seating configuration.

North American Long-Wheelbase (LWB) vs. European Short-Wheelbase (SWB)

A frequent point of confusion for global automotive shoppers is that Volkswagen manufactures the Tiguan in two distinct body lengths. In Europe and global markets, the standard Tiguan is a shorter two-row compact SUV measuring approximately 177.5 inches (4,509 mm) long, with a 105.5-inch wheelbase. In contrast, for the North American market (United States and Canada), Volkswagen exclusively sells the extended Long-Wheelbase (LWB) model, known in Europe as the Tiguan Allspace.

The North American Tiguan rides on an elongated 109.8-inch wheelbase and measures 186.1 inches from the front grille to the rear bumper. This extra 8.6 inches of vehicle length translates into substantially larger rear-seat legroom (36.6 inches) and cavernous cargo capacity. In front-wheel-drive (FWD) models, this extra length allows for a folding 50/50-split third-row bench seat, a rare feature in the compact crossover SUV segment.

How the VW Tiguan Measures Up Against Compact SUV Competitors

To understand how the VW Tiguan fits into the broader automotive landscape, it is helpful to compare its 186.1-inch length against its primary compact SUV competitors. Traditional compact crossovers—such as the Toyota RAV4 (180.9 inches), Honda CR-V (184.8 inches), Mazda CX-5 (180.1 inches), and Subaru Forester (182.7 inches)—are noticeably shorter bumper-to-bumper.

Measuring more than 5 inches longer than a Toyota RAV4, the Tiguan blurs the boundary between compact and midsize SUVs. This generous length provides limousine-like second-row legroom and expansive cargo loading length, allowing owners to slide 6-foot flat-pack furniture or sports gear directly into the back with the second-row seats folded flat.

Garage Fitment, Turning Radius, and Parking Practicality

For homeowners wondering whether a Volkswagen Tiguan will comfortably fit into their residential garage, the vehicle's 186.1-inch length (15.5 feet) fits easily into standard residential garages. A typical single-car suburban garage measures 20 feet deep, leaving roughly 4.5 feet of total buffer space to open the rear power liftgate and walk past the front bumper with the garage door closed.

Despite its elongated wheelbase, the Tiguan remains exceptionally maneuverable in urban environments. It features an electric power-assisted steering rack delivering a relatively tight turning circle of 37.7 feet (curb-to-curb). Available features such as front and rear Park Distance Control parking sensors and the 360-degree Area View camera system make parallel parking and tight parking garage maneuvers effortless.

How to Verify If a VW Tiguan Fits in Your Garage

Follow these five straightforward steps to measure your garage and parking space before buying.

  1. Measure Garage Depth from Wall to Closed Door

    Ensure your garage has at least 18.5 to 19 feet of interior depth to accommodate the Tiguan's 15.5-foot body comfortably.

  2. Account for Bumper Wall Clearance and Storage Shelves

    Deduct 1 to 2 feet if you have storage workbenches, bicycles, or water heaters positioned along the back garage wall.

  3. Measure Garage Door Opening Width with Side Mirrors

    Ensure the garage entrance door opening is at least 8 feet wide; the Tiguan measures 82.6 inches wide including exterior mirrors.

  4. Check Ceiling Height Clearance for Rear Liftgate

    The Tiguan's power liftgate reaches 82 inches high when fully open; program the tailgate opening memory stop if you have low ceiling tracks.

  5. Test Parallel Parking and Driveway Turnarounds

    Take a test drive to practice parking and verify that the 37.7-foot turning circle navigates your driveway smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How long is a VW Tiguan in feet?

The North American Volkswagen Tiguan is approximately 15.5 feet long (specifically 15.51 feet or 186.1 inches).

Q2: How long is a VW Tiguan in centimeters / millimeters?

It measures 472.8 centimeters or 4,728 millimeters in bumper-to-bumper exterior length.

Q3: Does a VW Tiguan have a third row of seats?

Yes, North American front-wheel-drive (FWD) Tiguan models come standard with an emergency 3rd-row bench for 7-passenger seating.

Q4: Is the VW Tiguan bigger than a Toyota RAV4?

Yes, the Tiguan is 186.1 inches long, making it more than 5 inches longer than the Toyota RAV4 (180.9 inches).

Q5: Is the VW Tiguan considered a compact or midsize SUV?

It is classified as a compact crossover SUV, but its long wheelbase sits near the upper size limit of the compact class.

Q6: Will a VW Tiguan fit in a standard 20-foot garage?

Yes, easily; a 20-foot garage provides roughly 4.5 feet of clearance beyond the Tiguan's 15.5-foot length.

Q7: What is the turning circle of a VW Tiguan?

The turning circle curb-to-curb is approximately 37.7 feet, providing agile handling in tight city parking.

Q8: Did the Tiguan get bigger in 2018?

Yes, when redesigned for 2018, the North American Tiguan grew nearly 11 inches in length compared to the 1st-generation model.
