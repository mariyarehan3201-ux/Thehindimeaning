Is Stella and Chewy a Good Dog Food?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

Yes, Stella & Chewy's is widely regarded by veterinarians, canine nutritionists, and pet parents as one of the best, most premium, and nutrient-dense dog food brands on the market. Founded in 2003 and based in Oak Creek, Wisconsin, Stella & Chewy's specializes in biologically appropriate raw, freeze-dried raw, and raw-coated dry kibble formulated with 85% to 95% meat, organ, and bone content. By utilizing High-Pressure Processing (HPP) to eliminate dangerous pathogens like Salmonella and Listeria without cooking away vital enzymes, the brand delivers exceptional nutrition for dogs of all life stages.

The Nutritional Philosophy: Biologically Appropriate Raw Canine Nutrition

In an industry long dominated by ultra-processed commercial kibble filled with corn, wheat, soy, and low-grade animal by-products, Stella & Chewy's was built on the core biological principle of the ancestral canine diet. Domestic dogs are evolutionary carnivores whose digestive tracts, stomach acids, and dentition are engineered to metabolize animal proteins, raw bone minerals, and organ nutrients rather than heavily refined carbohydrates.

Every Stella & Chewy's freeze-dried dinner patty and raw-blend formulation prioritizes responsibly sourced proteins. The brand exclusively utilizes grass-fed beef, cage-free poultry, wild-caught fish, and pasture-raised lamb sourced without added growth hormones or antibiotics. Furthermore, 100% of the fruits and vegetables included in their recipes (such as organic blueberries, cranberries, spinach, and squash) are certified organic, providing natural bio-available vitamins and antioxidants.

Safety and Manufacturing: High-Pressure Processing (HPP) Technology

The primary concern veterinarians traditionally voice regarding raw pet food diets is the risk of microbial contamination. Feeding raw meat can potentially expose pets and human family members to dangerous foodborne pathogens such as Salmonella, Listeria monocytogenes, and E. coli. Stella & Chewy's systematically eliminates this risk through state-of-the-art High-Pressure Processing (HPP).

HPP is a cold-pasteurization food safety method. Packaged raw food patties are placed inside an industrial chamber and subjected to an extraordinary 87,000 pounds per square inch of natural hydraulic water pressure—six times greater than the pressure found at the bottom of the Mariana Trench. This extreme hydrostatic force instantly shatters the cellular membranes of harmful bacteria, effectively sterilizing the food while generating zero heat, preserving delicate vitamins, vital living enzymes, and natural amino acids intact. Every single batch is tested by third-party laboratories before leaving the facility.

Clinical and Health Benefits Observed by Pet Parents

Transitioning a dog to Stella & Chewy's typically produces dramatic physical improvements within three to six weeks. Because the food is free from cheap grain fillers, artificial colors, and chemical preservatives, dogs suffering from chronic food allergies, yeasty itchy paws, and recurrent ear infections frequently experience total relief. The high concentration of natural omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids produces a lustrous, shiny coat and eliminates dandruff.

Furthermore, because the canine digestive tract can absorb nearly 90% of the nutrient content of biologically appropriate raw meat and organs, dogs produce significantly smaller, firmer, and practically odorless stools. While the food represents a significant financial investment—costing $150 to $300+ per month to feed a large breed exclusively on freeze-dried patties—many owners utilize the brand's 'Meal Mixers' as an affordable topper over high-quality kibble to provide raw nutritional benefits within budget.

How to Safely Transition Your Dog to Stella & Chewy's

Step-by-step guidance on safely introducing raw and freeze-dried dog food without digestive upset.

  1. Plan a Gradual 10-Day Dietary Transition Schedule

    Never switch dog foods abruptly. Mix 25% Stella & Chewy's with 75% old food for days 1-3, transition to a 50/50 split for days 4-6, advance to 75% new food for days 7-9, and feed 100% on day 10.

  2. Rehydrate Freeze-Dried Patties with Warm Water

    Crumble freeze-dried patties into your dog's bowl and add warm water, bone broth, or goat's milk (approximately 2 tablespoons of liquid per patty). Allow 3 to 5 minutes for moisture to rehydrate the raw meat.

  3. Maintain Safe Kitchen and Food Handling Hygiene

    Wash your hands with warm water and soap after handling raw food. Wash your dog's stainless steel or ceramic food bowls with hot soapy water after every meal to maintain strict bacterial cleanliness.

  4. Adjust Portion Sizing Based on Canine Weight and Energy

    Consult the feeding chart on the bag based on your dog's ideal adult weight. Because freeze-dried raw is extremely calorie-dense and filler-free, smaller portions satisfy nutritional needs.

  5. Store Unused Food in a Cool, Dry Pantry Location

    Keep unopened and opened freeze-dried bags sealed tightly in their original moisture-barrier packaging. Rehydrated food that is uneaten after 30 minutes should be discarded or refrigerated.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Is Stella & Chewy's safe for dogs?

Yes. Stella & Chewy's uses patented High-Pressure Processing (HPP) to destroy harmful pathogens like Salmonella and Listeria without cooking the meat, followed by strict third-party safety testing.

Q2: Do you have to add water to Stella & Chewy's freeze-dried food?

While dogs can eat freeze-dried patties dry as treats, rehydrating with warm water or bone broth is strongly recommended to aid digestion and maintain vital kidney hydration.

Q3: Why is Stella & Chewy's so expensive?

It is expensive because it contains 85% to 95% responsibly sourced real meat, organs, and bone with certified organic produce, rather than cheap corn, wheat, or soy fillers.

Q4: Has Stella & Chewy's ever had a recall?

Stella & Chewy's had a voluntary recall in 2015 for potential Listeria. In response, they upgraded to advanced patented HPP technology and strict lot-by-lot testing protocols.

Q5: Can puppies eat Stella & Chewy's?

Yes. Stella & Chewy's offers specialized puppy formulas tailored with optimal calcium-to-phosphorus ratios to support healthy bone growth in developing puppies.

Q6: Can I use Stella & Chewy's as a kibble topper?

Yes. Many dog owners use Stella & Chewy's Meal Mixers or crumbled patties as a nutritious, tasty topper over standard kibble, providing raw benefits at an affordable cost.

Q7: Where is Stella & Chewy's manufactured?

All Stella & Chewy's products are manufactured in their own state-of-the-art, SQF-certified human-grade food facility in Oak Creek, Wisconsin.

Q8: Does Stella & Chewy's help dogs with allergies?

Yes. By eliminating grains, fillers, and artificial additives and offering single-source proteins like venison, duck, and lamb, it frequently relieves chronic food allergies and itching.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

To conclude, Stella &amp; Chewy's is an outstanding, ultra-premium dog food brand that sets the industry standard for safe, biologically appropriate canine nutrition. Backed by responsibly sourced pasture-raised meats, organic produce, and patented High-Pressure Processing pathogen sterilization, the brand provides exceptional digestibility and allergy relief. Whether fed as an exclusive freeze-dried raw diet or utilized as a nutritious kibble topper, Stella &amp; Chewy's delivers tremendous health and vitality for beloved dogs.
