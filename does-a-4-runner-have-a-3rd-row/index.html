Does a 4 Runner Have a 3rd Row?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

The Toyota 4Runner has earned legendary status among sport utility vehicle enthusiasts for its rugged body-on-frame truck platform, legendary off-road reliability, and strong resale value. For growing families and outdoor adventure groups, a frequent question when shopping for this mid-size SUV is whether the 4Runner features a third-row seat. The answer is yes, but only on specific trim levels and as an optional configuration: while standard 4Runners are two-row five-passenger vehicles, select trims offer a 50/50 split-folding third row expanding seating to seven passengers.

Trim Availability: Which 4Runner Trims Offer Third-Row Seating?

Toyota does not equip every 4Runner with three rows of seating; in fact, hardcore off-road trims strictly omit the third row. Throughout the enduring fifth generation (2010 to 2024), third-row seating is available primarily as an optional package on the entry-level SR5, the SR5 Premium, and the luxury-focused Limited trim. The upscale Limited trim offers luxurious leather-trimmed third-row seating with power-folding capabilities, while the SR5 trims utilize manual folding mechanisms.

Conversely, if you are purchasing a performance off-road trim—such as the TRD Off-Road, TRD Off-Road Premium, TRD Pro, or TRD Sport—a third row is entirely unavailable from the factory. These rugged models prioritize rear cargo organization, heavy-duty suspension clearance, sliding rear cargo decks, and onboard equipment storage over extra passenger capacity. Buyers needing third-row seating must focus their search strictly on SR5 or Limited grades.

Passenger Comfort, Legroom, and Child Seat Practicality

While the availability of seven seats provides immense versatility for carpooling and family emergencies, prospective buyers must maintain realistic expectations regarding third-row passenger comfort. The 4Runner's third row offers approximately 29.3 inches of legroom, which is notably tighter than dedicated three-row crossovers like the Toyota Highlander or Grand Highlander. Because the rear floor is elevated to accommodate the rear live axle and spare tire, adults sit with their knees raised awkwardly.

Consequently, automotive reviewers and owners agree that the 4Runner's third row is best suited for children, young teenagers, or short around-town trips for adults. Accessing the third row requires tumbling the second-row 40/20/40 seat forward, which is straightforward for agile passengers. For child safety seats, the third row lacks lower LATCH anchors, meaning child restraint seats must be installed using standard vehicle seatbelts and top tethers.

Impact on Cargo Space and Payload Dynamics

Opting for third-row seating impacts the 4Runner's renowned rear cargo capacity. In standard two-row configurations, the 4Runner provides a massive 47.2 cubic feet of cargo volume behind the second row, expanding to 89.7 cubic feet with rear seats folded flat. When the third row is upright, cargo room behind the rear bench drops to roughly 9.0 cubic feet—enough for a few grocery bags or slim backpacks.

Fortunately, the 50/50 split third-row bench folds down into the cargo floor when not in use. However, because the folded seats sit atop the structural floor pan, third-row equipped models feature a cargo floor that is approximately two to three inches higher than standard two-row models, creating a slight cargo lip at the tailgate rather than a completely flat load floor.

How to Decide If You Need a Third-Row 4Runner in 5 Steps

Follow these five evaluative steps to determine whether a third-row Toyota 4Runner matches your family lifestyle.

  1. Determine Your Daily Passenger Count

    Evaluate whether you regularly transport more than five individuals, or if extra seating is needed only for occasional weekend carpooling.

  2. Assess the Ages and Heights of Passengers

    Recognize that the 29.3 inches of third-row legroom is ideal for young children but tight for full-grown adults.

  3. Filter Your Shopping to SR5 and Limited Trims

    Eliminate TRD Off-Road and TRD Pro trims from your search, focusing exclusively on SR5, SR5 Premium, or Limited models.

  4. Test-Fold the Third Row in Person

    Visit a dealership to fold the 50/50 split bench up and down to evaluate cargo floor height and rear visibility.

  5. Compare Against the Toyota Grand Highlander

    If you need daily adult seating in all three rows, test drive a unibody crossover like the Grand Highlander alongside the truck-based 4Runner.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can adults sit in the third row of a 4Runner?

Adults can sit in the third row for short, emergency trips, but tight legroom (29.3 inches) and low seat cushions make it uncomfortable for long journeys.

Q2: Can you add an aftermarket third row to a 4Runner?

Adding a third row to a two-row model is not recommended; factory third-row models have reinforced floor brackets, rear seatbelts, and curtain airbags.

Q3: Does the 2025 sixth-generation 4Runner have a third row?

Yes, the redesigned sixth-generation 4Runner continues to offer an optional third-row seat on non-hybrid SR5 and Limited trims.

Q4: Do TRD Pro 4Runners have a third row?

No, the TRD Pro, TRD Off-Road, and TRD Sport are configured strictly as two-row, five-passenger vehicles.

Q5: Does the third row fold completely flat?

The seats fold flat into the floor, but the cargo floor sits about two inches higher than in models without the third-row option.

Q6: Does the 4Runner have third-row air conditioning vents?

Fifth-generation 4Runners feature ceiling-mounted air vents in the second row, but lack dedicated independent third-row climate controls.

Q7: How much does the third-row option cost on a 4Runner?

The factory third-row seating option typically adds between 1,000 and 1,500 dollars to the vehicle MSRP on SR5 and Limited trims.

Q8: Are there child car seat anchors in the 4Runner's third row?

The third row has top tether anchors, but lacks lower LATCH anchors, requiring car seats to be installed using vehicle lap-shoulder belts.
