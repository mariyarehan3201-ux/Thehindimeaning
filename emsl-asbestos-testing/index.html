EMSL Asbestos Testing

EMSL Analytical Laboratory Infrastructure and Accreditations

EMSL Analytical, Inc. stands as one of the largest and most prestigious commercial environmental testing laboratory networks in North America, operating over fifty state-of-the-art testing facilities across the United States and Canada. Established in 1981, EMSL provides comprehensive analytical testing for industrial hygienists, environmental consultants, commercial engineering firms, regulatory agencies, and private homeowners seeking definitive identification of hazardous asbestos materials.

A distinguishing hallmark of EMSL asbestos testing is its unwavering commitment to third-party quality assurance and formal regulatory accreditations. EMSL testing facilities maintain active accreditation under the National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program (NVLAP), administered by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) under Lab Code 101048 and associated regional codes. Furthermore, EMSL laboratories hold certifications through the American Industrial Hygiene Association Laboratory Accreditation Programs (AIHA-LAP, LLC), state departments of environmental protection, and international standard ISO/IEC 17025.

Advanced Microscopy Methodologies: PLM, PCM, and TEM

To evaluate diverse environmental matrices, EMSL deploys an advanced suite of optical and electron microscopy methodologies tailored to specific regulatory requirements. For standard bulk building materials—such as textured drywall coatings, acoustic plaster, pipe insulation, and vinyl flooring—EMSL utilizes Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) coupled with dispersion staining techniques in accordance with EPA Method 600/R-93/116. PLM allows certified petrographers to identify optical crystal properties, determining the exact mineralogical fiber type and volumetric percentage.

When analyzing non-friable organically bound (NOB) materials like asphaltic roofing felts, floor tile mastics, and vinyl composite tiles where fibers are tightly bound in complex petroleum matrices, standard optical microscopy can yield false negatives. In these scenarios, EMSL performs gravimetric matrix reduction followed by high-resolution Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM). TEM utilizes high-energy electron beams operating at magnifications exceeding twenty-thousand times, paired with Energy Dispersive X-Ray Spectroscopy (EDX) and Selected Area Electron Diffraction (SAED) to positively identify sub-microscopic fibers that escape optical detection.

EMSL Test Kits, Chain of Custody, and Laboratory Certificates

For homeowners and small contractors seeking direct access to certified laboratory testing, EMSL offers consumer-friendly DIY asbestos test kits. These kits supply sterile collection bags, disposable sampling tools, nitrile gloves, prepaid shipping mailers, and detailed field sampling instructions. Clients collect suspect material samples, complete the required chain of custody form detailing sample locations and turnaround requirements, and dispatch the package directly to their nearest EMSL regional laboratory.

Upon completion of testing, EMSL issues a comprehensive, legally defensible Certificate of Analysis (COA) accessible through their secure online LabConnect portal. The final report explicitly documents sample condition upon receipt, analytical preparation methods, percentage and classification of asbestos detected (e.g., 5% Chrysotile, None Detected), non-asbestos matrix components (such as cellulose or fiberglass), and analyst quality control verification signatures, empowering clients to make informed, code-compliant remediation decisions.

How to Submit Samples for EMSL Asbestos Testing

Standard operational procedure for properly sampling, packaging, documenting, and submitting suspect materials to EMSL Analytical.

  1. Obtain an EMSL Test Kit or Standard Chain of Custody Form

    Purchase an official EMSL asbestos test kit online or download the standard chain of custody document from the EMSL Analytical website.

  2. Collect Material Specimens Following Safe Sampling Protocols

    Wear a P100 respirator, mist the sample location with amended water, and cut a small one-inch square piece of suspect material down to the substrate.

  3. Package Samples in Double Zip-Seal Polyethylene Bags

    Place each distinct sample into an individual zip-seal bag, mark it with a unique identifier, and enclose all samples inside a second sealed bag.

  4. Complete the Chain of Custody Document with Analysis Details

    Fill out the form with your contact info, sample IDs, material descriptions, desired analysis type (PLM or TEM), and requested turnaround time.

  5. Ship to the Nearest Accredited EMSL Laboratory Location

    Place the samples and chain of custody form inside a sturdy mailer and dispatch it via tracked courier to your designated regional EMSL facility.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is EMSL Analytical known for in asbestos testing?

EMSL is the leading commercial environmental laboratory network in North America, renowned for NVLAP accreditation and advanced PLM and TEM analysis.

Q2: How long does EMSL take to report asbestos test results?

Standard turnaround is two to three business days, but emergency rush analysis is available with results delivered in as little as 2 to 6 hours.

Q3: What laboratory accreditations does EMSL maintain for asbestos?

EMSL maintains accreditation through NVLAP (NIST Lab Code 101048), AIHA-LAP, LLC, ISO/IEC 17025, and state environmental health departments.

Q4: What is the difference between PLM and TEM testing at EMSL?

PLM uses optical microscopy for standard bulk materials, while TEM uses electron beams to resolve sub-micron fibers in complex matrices like floor mastics.

Q5: Can homeowners buy and use EMSL asbestos test kits?

Yes, EMSL sells consumer test kits containing sterile collection bags, protective gloves, prepaid shipping mailers, and step-by-step instructions.

Q6: How do I access my EMSL laboratory test reports?

Reports and legally defensible Certificates of Analysis are securely accessed through EMSL's proprietary online customer portal, LabConnect.

Q7: Does EMSL perform air clearance sample testing for schools?

Yes, EMSL is heavily utilized for mandatory AHERA Transmission Electron Microscopy air clearance analysis following school abatement projects.

Q8: How much material should I send to EMSL for a bulk test?

A piece measuring one to two square inches, or one to two tablespoons of loose material, is sufficient for full laboratory preparation and testing.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

EMSL asbestos testing provides unmatched scientific precision, regulatory credibility, and nationwide accessibility for anyone navigating potential asbestos contamination. By combining NVLAP-accredited laboratory rigor with advanced PLM, PCM, and TEM instrumentation, EMSL eliminates guesswork and ensures that property owners, environmental consultants, and public agencies receive accurate, legally defensible analytical findings.
