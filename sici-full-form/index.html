SICI Full Form: Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of SICI is Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute. Established in 1968 and named in honor of India's second Prime Minister, Lal Bahadur Shastri, SICI is an elite bi-national educational organization that promotes academic research, university partnerships, student fellowships, and cross-cultural scholarly exchange between India and Canada. Jointly funded by the Government of India and Canadian university networks, SICI connects over 150 premier universities across both nations.

What Is SICI and What Does It Stand For?

In international higher education diplomacy, cross-border academic research, and bilateral institutional partnerships, SICI stands for Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute. Founded in 1968 under a visionary bilateral agreement between the governments of India and Canada, the institute was named to commemorate the memory of Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri, who championed peaceful international solidarity, agricultural self-reliance, and scientific education.

SICI operates as an independent, bi-national educational consortium comprising over 150 premier member universities and research institutions across India and Canada (including IITs, IIMs, central universities, the University of Toronto, and McGill University). Headquartered binationality with executive secretariats in New Delhi, India, and Calgary, Canada, SICI serves as the premier academic bridge fostering interdisciplinary scientific research, social science dialogues, and bilateral student-faculty mobility.

Bilateral Mission and Institutional Funding Structure

The core mission of the Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute is to advance mutual understanding between the peoples of India and Canada through academic research and intellectual collaboration. The institute is supported financially and programmatically by the Ministry of Education (Government of India) through the Department of Higher Education, alongside membership dues and grants from Canadian universities and funding councils.

SICI funds joint research initiatives addressing pressing global challenges, including climate change mitigation, sustainable agriculture, public health epidemiology, clean energy transition, artificial intelligence governance, and indigenous community studies. Core Grant Programs and Fellowships Offered by SICI

The Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute awards prestigious competitive research fellowships, institutional development grants, and student exchange mobility funds annually. These grants enable Indian scholars to conduct post-doctoral and doctoral research in Canadian laboratories, while Canadian academicians deliver lecture series and collaborate with Indian university faculties.

Applications undergo rigorous peer review by independent bi-national selection committees based on scholarly merit, academic rigor, and bilateral relevance. The table below outlines prominent grant programs administered by SICI.

Shastri Conference & Lecture Series Grants Member universities Organizing international bilateral webinars, seminars, and academic workshops

Impact on Bilateral Relations and Cultural Diplomacy

Over more than five decades of academic diplomacy, SICI has nurtured thousands of distinguished scholars, environmental scientists, diplomats, and cultural ambassadors. By establishing Indian Studies chairs in major Canadian universities and Canadian Studies research centers across Indian central universities, the institute built a lasting foundation of intercultural understanding.

In times of shifting political climates, SICI serves as a resilient, independent track-two educational bridge, preserving people-to-people ties, protecting international scholarly collaborations, and demonstrating that intellectual exploration transcends geopolitical boundaries.

How University Researchers Apply for a SICI Research Grant

  1. Confirm Institutional Membership of Your University

    Verify that your home university or institute in India or Canada is an active, fee-paying member of the Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute consortium.

  2. Identify a Collaborative Counterpart in the Partner Country

    Establish an academic partnership with a co-principal investigator (Co-PI) at an accredited Canadian or Indian member institution.

  3. Draft a Bi-National Joint Research Proposal

    Formulate a detailed research plan outlining mutual methodology, timeline, budget allocation, and measurable bilateral scholarly outputs.

  4. Submit Online Application with Institutional Endorsement

    Upload the proposal, investigator CVs, and formal endorsement letters from both university Registrars/Deans via the SICI grant portal.

  5. Undergo Peer Review and Execute the Grant Agreement

    Upon selection by the bi-national peer evaluation committee, sign the grant agreement, receive research funds, and commence collaborative research.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does SICI stand for?

SICI stands for Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute, a bi-national educational organization promoting academic research between India and Canada.

Q2: When was SICI founded and after whom is it named?

SICI was founded in 1968 and named in honor of Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri, the second Prime Minister of India.

Q3: Which government ministry supports SICI in India?

In India, SICI is funded and supported by the Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education).

Q4: Where are the headquarters of SICI located?

SICI has twin secretariats: one in New Delhi (India) and one in Calgary, Alberta (Canada).

Q5: Who can apply for SICI research grants and fellowships?

Faculty members, post-doctoral scholars, doctoral candidates, and students enrolled in SICI member universities in India and Canada can apply.

Q6: What disciplines are covered under SICI funding?

Funding covers STEM sciences, environmental sustainability, humanities, social sciences, agricultural technology, and public health.

Q7: How many universities are members of SICI?

Over 150 leading universities and premier research institutes across India and Canada are institutional members of the consortium.

Q8: Does SICI support undergraduate student internships?

Yes, programs like the Shastri Student Internship Project (SSIP) provide funding for undergraduate and graduate student research internships.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute (SICI) stands as a monument to bi-national educational partnership. By connecting scholars, funding groundbreaking cross-border research, and honoring the legacy of Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri, SICI enriches higher education, deepens mutual respect between India and Canada, and produces knowledge that addresses humanity's greatest shared challenges.
