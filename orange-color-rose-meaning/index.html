How to Select and Care for Fresh Orange Roses

Follow these florist-approved steps to maximize the vase life and vibrancy of orange roses.

  1. Step 1

    Examine the rose guard petals: gently squeeze the base of the flower bud to confirm it feels firm and healthy.

  2. Step 2

    Choose roses that are slightly open rather than tight bullet buds, ensuring they will open fully in the vase.

  3. Step 3

    Prepare a clean glass vase filled with room-temperature water mixed with floral nutrient powder.

  4. Step 4

    Trim two inches off each stem at a sharp 45-degree angle using sharp shears under cold running water.

  5. Step 5

    Strip away all green leaves that would sit below the water line to prevent foul bacterial growth in the vase.

  6. Step 6

    Place the arrangement in a cool room away from direct afternoon sun, heaters, and ripening fruit bowls.

  7. Step 7

    Change the vase water and re-cut stem tips every two to three days to ensure uninterrupted water hydration.
