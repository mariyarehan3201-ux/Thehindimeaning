ACP 5 Asbestos

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 30, 2026

An ACP 5 asbestos form, officially designated as the Asbestos Assessment Report by the New York City Department of Environmental Protection (NYC DEP), is a mandatory regulatory filing required before the NYC Department of Buildings will issue construction, alteration, or demolition permits. Certified NYC Asbestos Investigators inspect job sites to formally verify whether a proposed project involves hazardous materials or legally qualifies as not an asbestos project.

The Purpose and Legal Function of an ACP-5 Form

The ACP-5 form serves as official legal certification that an independent NYC-certified investigator surveyed the work zone and concluded that no asbestos-containing materials will be disturbed during construction. This determination applies when materials test negative, have already been fully abated, or when the scope of work involves less than minor quantities (under 10 square feet or 25 linear feet).

If an investigator discovers regulated materials that will be disturbed, the project cannot proceed under an ACP-5. Instead, a licensed contractor must execute certified abatement under an ACP-7 project notification before secondary work permits can be approved.

Investigation Protocols for Certified NYC Investigators

Only an investigator holding a valid certification card issued by the NYC DEP Asbestos Technical Review Unit is legally authorized to execute an ACP-5 survey. The investigator reviews historic building plans, identifies homogeneous sampling areas, and extracts core specimens for laboratory analysis under strict chain-of-custody protocols.

Filing Through the NYC DEP ARTS Online Portal

Modern ACP-5 filings are conducted exclusively online through the Asbestos Reporting and Tracking System (ARTS). Once the certified investigator enters bulk sampling results and uploads accredited laboratory reports, the system generates an official electronic barcoded certificate that links directly to the NYC DOB NOW permit database, allowing building inspectors to approve construction permits seamlessly.

How to Obtain and File an ACP-5 Form for NYC Building Permits

Step-by-step regulatory walkthrough for property owners and contractors securing an NYC DEP ACP-5 Asbestos Assessment Report.

  1. Retain a Certified NYC Asbestos Investigator

    Hire an environmental consultant possessing an active investigator certification card issued by the NYC Department of Environmental Protection.

  2. Conduct On-Site Visual and Bulk Sampling Survey

    Facilitate a full walk-through of the proposed construction area to extract bulk material samples from walls, ceilings, and flooring.

  3. Analyze Specimens at a NYS ELAP Accredited Lab

    Send collected samples to an accredited laboratory for Polarized Light Microscopy testing to verify asbestos percentages.

  4. Upload Survey Data to the NYC DEP ARTS Portal

    Have the certified investigator log into the DEP Asbestos Reporting and Tracking System to enter sample findings and certify the project as non-hazardous.

  5. Submit Barcoded ACP-5 to NYC Department of Buildings

    Download the officially barcoded ACP-5 document and attach it directly to your DOB NOW work permit application for municipal approval.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is an ACP-5 form in New York City?

An ACP-5 is an Asbestos Assessment Report filed with the NYC DEP certifying that a proposed construction project will not disturb regulated asbestos materials.

Q2: Can an architect or engineer sign an ACP-5 form?

No, under NYC administrative law, only a certified NYC Asbestos Investigator licensed by the DEP is legally authorized to sign and file an ACP-5 form.

Q3: What is the difference between an ACP-5 and an ACP-7?

An ACP-5 is filed when no asbestos will be disturbed (or under minor quantities), whereas an ACP-7 is an official project notification required before conducting active abatement.

Q4: Do all NYC Department of Buildings permits require an ACP-5?

Almost all alteration and demolition permits require an ACP-5 or an ACP-7, with only limited plumbing and electrical work qualifying for specific exemptions.

Q5: How long is an approved ACP-5 form valid in NYC?

An ACP-5 form is valid for one year from the date of submission through the NYC DEP ARTS portal.

Q6: How much does it cost to get an ACP-5 in NYC?

Professional investigator fees typically range from $400 to $750 for residential apartments and $1,200 to $2,500+ for commercial spaces, including lab testing.

Q7: What happens if an investigator finds asbestos during an ACP-5 survey?

If suspect materials test positive and will be disturbed by the proposed renovation, the project must undergo licensed abatement under an ACP-7 filing before permits can be granted.

Q8: What is the NYC DEP ARTS system?

The Asbestos Reporting and Tracking System (ARTS) is the centralized web portal used by investigators, contractors, and inspectors to file and track all asbestos projects in NYC.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Securing an ACP 5 asbestos form represents a critical milestone in navigating New York City's demanding construction permitting process. By engaging a certified NYC Asbestos Investigator early in design planning, conducting thorough bulk sampling, and submitting verified reports through the DEP ARTS system, developers and property owners ensure legal compliance, protect construction crews, and prevent costly stop-work delays.
