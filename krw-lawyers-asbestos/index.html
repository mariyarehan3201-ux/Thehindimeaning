Firm Background and Asbestos Practice Focus

Ketterman Rowland & Westlund, operating widely under the banner of KRW Lawyers, established a dedicated toxic tort and asbestos practice group to represent workers injured by industrial hazards. Headquartered in San Antonio, Texas, with regional offices and national litigation affiliations, the firm handles product liability claims stemming from military service, oil refining, heavy petrochemical manufacturing, commercial construction, and power generation.

The firm's asbestos practice focuses primarily on representing individuals diagnosed with malignant pleural mesothelioma, peritoneal mesothelioma, and asbestos-induced bronchogenic lung cancer. KRW Lawyers deploys dedicated intake investigators, paralegals, and forensic document researchers who reconstruct decades-old workplace exposure timelines, identifying the specific corporate entities, equipment manufacturers, and bankruptcy trust funds responsible for each client's illness.

Investigative Methodologies and Asbestos Trust Fund Processing

A core operational strength of asbestos litigation firms like KRW Lawyers is their ability to execute rapid, forensic investigations into historical occupational exposures. Because asbestos illnesses possess latency periods spanning thirty to fifty years, claimants rarely possess physical product labels or invoices from jobsites worked in the 1960s or 1970s. Asbestos legal teams maintain extensive databases of industrial worksites—including oil refineries, naval shipyards, chemical processing plants, and power stations—cross-referencing client job titles with known asbestos-containing equipment.

Once exposure sites and culpable products are identified, the firm's administrative teams process claims across the more than sixty active asbestos bankruptcy settlement trusts. Created under Section 524(g) of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code by reorganized building material manufacturers, these trusts hold over thirty billion dollars in dedicated assets. By filing complete, well-documented administrative packages that satisfy standardized medical and occupational criteria, lawyers secure significant financial distributions within months without subjecting clients to courtroom trials.

Contingency Fee Structures and Selecting Asbestos Counsel

Like all reputable toxic tort litigation firms, KRW Lawyers handles asbestos and mesothelioma cases on a strict contingency fee basis. Under this arrangement, clients pay zero upfront retainers, zero hourly consultation charges, and zero out-of-pocket litigation costs. The law firm advances all expenses necessary to investigate historical exposure, procure medical records, consult oncology experts, and file legal pleadings. Legal fees and reimbursed expenses are deducted as an agreed-upon percentage of the financial recovery only after compensation is successfully secured.

When deciding whether to retain KRW Lawyers or another national asbestos litigation practice, prospective clients should evaluate the firm's documented history of successful asbestos recoveries, their familiarity with regional industrial exposure sites, and their commitment to responsive, compassionate communication. Retaining a dedicated firm ensures that terminally ill individuals can focus entirely on medical care and family, while experienced advocates pursue maximum financial restitution.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is KRW Lawyers' asbestos practice?

The toxic tort litigation practice of Ketterman Rowland & Westlund, representing victims of mesothelioma and asbestos exposure.

Q2: How much does it cost to retain an asbestos lawyer?

Asbestos attorneys work on a contingency fee basis; clients pay zero upfront fees and legal fees are collected only if financial compensation is won.

Q3: What types of asbestos claims can be filed?

Claims include administrative bankruptcy trust filings, civil personal injury lawsuits, wrongful death claims, and VA disability benefits.

Q4: How long does it take to receive compensation from an asbestos claim?

Bankruptcy trust claims are often paid within three to six months, while civil court lawsuits generally resolve in twelve to eighteen months.

Q5: Can family members file a claim if a loved one passed away from mesothelioma?

Yes. Surviving spouses, dependent children, or estate executors can file wrongful death lawsuits and bankruptcy trust claims.

Q6: Do I have to travel to the law firm's office?

No. Mesothelioma litigators travel to visit clients at home, hospital, or hospice, and handle all legal filings remotely.

Q7: What compensation is available for mesothelioma victims?

Compensation covers medical treatments, chemotherapy, travel to specialized cancer centers, lost income, and pain and suffering.

Q8: Can military veterans file lawsuits in addition to VA benefits?

Yes. Veterans can receive 100% disability benefits from the VA while concurrently recovering settlements from civilian product manufacturers.
