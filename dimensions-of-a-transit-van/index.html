Dimensions of a Transit Van

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

The Ford Transit is America's best-selling full-size commercial cargo van, celebrated by trade contractors, logistics delivery fleets, and campervan conversion enthusiasts alike. Engineered with exceptional modularity, the Ford Transit is manufactured across three body lengths (Regular, Long, and Extended-Length), three roof heights (Low, Medium, and High Roof), and two wheelbases. Understanding exact exterior dimensions, interior standing heights, cargo floor lengths, and doorway clearances is essential for fleet planning and DIY camper layouts.

The Three Roof Heights: Standing Room and Exterior Clearance

The most defining architectural dimension of the Ford Transit is its choice of three distinct factory roof heights: Low Roof, Medium Roof, and High Roof. The Low Roof model features an exterior height of approximately 82 to 83 inches (around 6.9 feet), allowing it to clear standard seven-foot residential garage doors and commercial parking garages, but limits interior cargo height to 56.9 inches (requiring adults to crouch).

For van lifers, camper conversions, and delivery drivers who need to stand upright, the Medium and High Roof configurations are game changers. The Medium Roof provides an exterior height of roughly 100 to 101 inches (8.4 feet) and an interior cargo standing height of 72.0 inches (exactly 6.0 feet). The High Roof model reaches an exterior height of 109 to 110 inches (9.1 feet), delivering a cavernous 81.5 inches (6 feet 9 inches) of interior ceiling height, allowing even tall adults to walk upright effortlessly.

The Three Body Lengths: Regular, Long, and Extended (EL)

Ford manufactures the Transit full-size chassis across three overall body lengths paired with two wheelbase configurations: a 130-inch standard wheelbase and a 148-inch long wheelbase. The Regular Length model (130" wheelbase) measures 219.9 inches in overall bumper-to-bumper length (18.3 feet), offering a cargo floor length of 126.3 inches (10.5 feet) behind the front seats.

The Long Length model (148" wheelbase) measures 237.6 inches overall (19.8 feet) with a cargo floor length of 143.7 inches (nearly 12 feet), fitting into standard twenty-foot parking spaces. The flagship Extended Length (EL) model utilizes the 148-inch wheelbase with an extended rear body overhang, stretching to 263.9 inches overall (22.0 feet) and providing an immense 172.2 inches (14.35 feet) of flat cargo floor length, yielding up to 487.3 cubic feet of total cargo volume.

Interior Width, Wheel Wells, and Door Opening Clearances

Cargo loading efficiency depends heavily on doorway openings and interior width between the rear wheel wells. Across all Transit van models, the maximum interior cargo width (measured wall-to-wall at beltline height) is 70.2 inches. The critical width between the rear wheel wells is 54.8 inches on single-rear-wheel (SRW) models, which easily allows standard four-by-eight-foot sheets of plywood or loaded standard GMA pallets (40x48 inches) to slide flat between the wheel arches.

Door dimensions provide outstanding forklift and delivery access. The rear 50/50 hinged doors open up to 180 degrees (or 253 degrees with optional swing hinges), providing a rear door opening width of 61.7 inches and an opening height matching the roof tier (up to 72.2 inches on High Roof models). The passenger side sliding door offers a massive 51.2-inch opening width, allowing forklift operators to load pallets directly through the side of the vehicle.

Evaluating these dimensions ensures your commercial fleet or camper build functions with maximum ergonomic efficiency.

How to Measure and Choose a Ford Transit Van in 5 Steps

Follow these practical steps to choose the ideal roof height and body length for your Transit van application.

  1. Determine Standing Height Needs

    If you want to walk upright without crouching (camper van or mobile workshop), choose the Medium (72" ceiling) or High Roof (81.5" ceiling).

  2. Evaluate Residential Garage and Parking Limits

    If you must park inside a standard 7-foot home garage, you must strictly select the Low Roof model (82" exterior height).

  3. Calculate Cargo Floor Length Requirements

    Select 130" wheelbase (10.5 ft floor) for nimble city delivery, or 148" Extended (14.35 ft floor) for queen bed and bathroom camper layouts.

  4. Verify Pallet and Plywood Wheel Well Clearance

    Confirm that single-rear-wheel models provide 54.8 inches of flat width between wheel arches to easily load 48-inch wide materials.

  5. Check Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR)

    Choose between Transit-150 (half-ton), Transit-250 (three-quarter ton), or Transit-350 Heavy Duty depending on your payload requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can a 6-foot person stand up inside a Ford Transit van?

Yes, a 6-foot person can stand completely upright in a Medium Roof Transit (72.0 inches of interior height) and has ample clearance in a High Roof Transit (81.5 inches).

Q2: What is the exterior height of a High Roof Ford Transit?

The exterior height of a High Roof Ford Transit ranges from 109.4 to 110.4 inches (approximately 9.1 to 9.2 feet), requiring ten-foot clearance bays.

Q3: How long is a Ford Transit van bumper to bumper?

Overall bumper-to-bumper length is 219.9 inches (18.3 ft) for Regular, 237.6 inches (19.8 ft) for Long, and 263.9 inches (22.0 ft) for Extended models.

Q4: Will a 4x8 sheet of plywood fit in a Ford Transit?

Yes, all single-rear-wheel Transit vans have 54.8 inches of width between the wheel wells, allowing 4x8 sheets of plywood to lie completely flat on the floor.

Q5: Can a Low Roof Ford Transit fit in a standard garage?

Yes, the Low Roof Transit is 82.2 to 83.6 inches tall, allowing it to squeeze into standard seven-foot (84-inch) residential garage doors with careful driving.

Q6: What is the difference between Transit 148 and 148 Extended?

Both share a 148-inch wheelbase, but the 148 Extended features a 26-inch longer rear body overhang, extending cargo floor length from 143.7 inches to 172.2 inches.

Q7: How wide is the interior of a Ford Transit cargo van?

The maximum interior width wall-to-wall is 70.2 inches, narrowing to 54.8 inches between the rear wheel wells.

Q8: How much cargo volume does the largest Ford Transit have?

The High Roof Extended Length (EL) Transit van offers a massive 487.3 cubic feet of interior cargo volume, the largest in its class.
