Does Bmw X5 Have a Third Row?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

The BMW X5 has long been recognized as the pioneer of the luxury 'Sports Activity Vehicle' (SAV) segment, combining athletic Bavarian handling with sophisticated cabin technology. For families seeking a balance between high-performance driving dynamics and family practicality, an essential question arises: does the BMW X5 have a third row? The answer is yes, but with major caveats: while earlier models offered an optional third row, BMW discontinued the third-row option on US-market X5 models after the 2023 model year, directing three-row buyers to the larger BMW X7.

Generation and Model Year Availability: When Was the Third Row Offered?

Throughout its multi-generation history, BMW offered an optional third-row seat on select X5 models. In the second generation (E70, 2007-2013), third generation (F15, 2014-2018), and early fourth generation (G05, 2019-2023), buyers could option a compact, power-folding 50/50 split third-row seat on non-hybrid six-cylinder models like the X5 sDrive40i and xDrive40i.

However, starting with the mid-cycle refresh (Life Cycle Impulse, or LCI) for the 2024 model year, BMW officially eliminated the third-row seating option from the US-market X5 order guide entirely. High-performance M models (like the X5 M60i or X5 M Competition) and plug-in hybrid models (like the xDrive45e and xDrive50e) never offered the third row due to underfloor battery pack packaging constraints.

Passenger Space and Legroom: What Was the X5 Third Row Like?

For shoppers browsing the pre-owned market for a three-row BMW X5 (2019 to 2023), it is vital to understand that the X5's third row was designed strictly as an occasional '+2' jump seat. With a tight 27.4 inches of legroom, a low cushion position, and limited footwell depth, the X5 third row is unsuitable for adults or tall teenagers.

The third row was designed primarily for young children, carpool emergencies, or short trips across town. Furthermore, optioning the third row included electric second-row slide-and-tilt seat mechanisms and rear two-axle air suspension to level the chassis under passenger weight. However, the third row lacked dedicated lower LATCH anchors, requiring child safety seats to be fastened via seatbelts.

The BMW X7 Alternative: True Luxury for Seven Passengers

The primary reason BMW discontinued the third row on the X5 is that the brand introduced the flagship BMW X7 in 2019. The X7 was conceived specifically to compete against the Mercedes-Benz GLS and Cadillac Escalade, offering a full-size luxury SAV platform where three rows of adult seating come standard.

Riding on an extended chassis that is nearly ten inches longer than the X5, the BMW X7 provides true three-row comfort with 33.3 inches of third-row legroom, standard five-zone climate control, power-sliding captain's chairs, and a dedicated third-row panoramic glass moonroof. For luxury buyers who require genuine seven-passenger accommodation, stepping up to the X7 is the logical choice.

How to Shop for a Three-Row BMW in 5 Steps

Follow these five steps to find a BMW that provides the passenger seating and luxury your family requires.

  1. Determine Your Daily Passenger Frequency

    Decide whether you need third-row seating on a daily basis (X7 required) or for occasional carpool use (pre-owned X5).

  2. Search Pre-Owned X5 Models (2019-2023)

    If seeking an X5, filter used inventories for 2019-2023 models with the optional 'Third-Row Seating' package (Code 4UB).

  3. Eliminate Hybrid and V8 Trims from X5 Search

    Do not waste time searching X5 45e, 50e, or X5 M models, as third rows were never built on electrified or V8 trims.

  4. Test Drive the BMW X7 Alongside the X5

    Visit a BMW center to experience the electric third-row folding and adult legroom in an X7 xDrive40i.

  5. Evaluate Garage Dimensions for X7 Fitment

    Measure your home garage to ensure it can comfortably park the X7's 204-inch overall length.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Does the 2024 or 2025 BMW X5 have a third row?

No, BMW officially discontinued the third-row seating option on the BMW X5 in North America starting with the 2024 model year refresh.

Q2: Can adults sit in the third row of a BMW X5?

The third row in pre-2024 X5 models is very cramped (27.4 inches of legroom) and is suitable only for young children or emergency trips.

Q3: Can you retrofit a third row into a two-row BMW X5?

No, retrofitting is unsafe and impractical; third-row models feature unique structural floor pans, air suspension, and side curtain airbags.

Q4: Does the BMW X5 plug-in hybrid have a third row?

No, the high-voltage lithium-ion battery pack in the X5 xDrive45e and xDrive50e occupies the underfloor cargo space, preventing third-row installation.

Q5: What BMW SUV has 3 rows of seats?

The full-size BMW X7 comes standard with three rows of seating for six or seven passengers.

Q6: Does the third row in a BMW X5 fold flat?

Yes, the 50/50 split third-row seats in 2019-2023 models fold completely flat into the cargo floor when not in use.

Q7: Does the BMW X5 have third-row air vents?

Models equipped with the factory third row included auxiliary rear air conditioning vents and a dedicated third-row blower control.

Q8: How much did the third-row option cost on an X5?

When available from 2019 to 2023, the factory third-row seating package cost approximately 2,300 dollars and included two-axle air suspension.
