The Origins of 'Have a Day': Bob Does Sports and Social Golf Culture

Traditional golf has historically been steeped in rigid dress codes, country club etiquette, and silent reverence. However, the explosion of digital golf entertainment—led by channels like Bob Does Sports, Good Good Golf, and Barstool Fore Play—shattered these stuffy conventions. Robby Berger's signature catchphrase, 'Have a Day,' began as an enthusiastic, full-throated toast delivered after a miraculously sunk putt, an ice-cold beverage on the fairway, or a hilarious triple-bogey disaster.

The phrase immediately captured the hearts of everyday recreational golfers who play the game for laughs, friendship, and weekend escapism rather than chasing PGA Tour perfection. When Berger and his crew launched official 'Have a Day' branded t-shirts, hoodies, and performance polos, they tapped into an underserved market of golfers who desired comfortable, contemporary streetwear that celebrated the social joy of being outside with friends.

Fabric Engineering: Ring-Spun Cotton vs. Performance Poly Blends

The enduring popularity of the have a day shirt stems not just from its catchy slogan, but from premium garment blank selection. Early merchandise creators often used stiff, itchy promotional cotton tees that shrank and distorted after a single wash. In contrast, official 'Have a Day' lifestyle shirts utilize high-grade combed ring-spun cotton and tri-blends that deliver butter-soft tactile comfort against the skin.

For golfers wearing the slogan directly onto the links, the brand expanded into four-way stretch technical fabrics. Performance polos incorporating polyester-spandex blends provide moisture-wicking capillary action, moving perspiration away from the torso while providing unrestricted rotational mobility through the golf backswing. Wrinkle-resistant stay-flat collars guarantee the shirt looks tailored enough for golf clubhouse dining rooms.

Styling the Shirt: From the Fairway to the Taproom

The versatility of the have a day shirt makes it an effortless wardrobe staple. On the golf course, a performance Have a Day polo or t-shirt pairs seamlessly with four-way stretch golf shorts, a vintage rope snapback hat, and athletic spikeless golf shoes. Because the slogan carries universal, positive optimism, it serves as an instant conversation starter on executive putting greens and public municipal fairways alike.

Off the course, styling the classic graphic cotton tee is effortless. Layer it beneath an open flannel shirt or utility canvas overshirt with relaxed-fit chinos or denim for casual brewery outings, college tailgates, or weekend travel. Embracing the 'Have a Day' mindset means approaching life with gratitude, good humor, and a refusal to take yourself too seriously—a philosophy proudly broadcast on your chest.

How to Style and Care for a Have a Day Graphic Shirt

Follow these five practical fashion and laundry steps to keep your graphic t-shirt looking crisp and feeling soft.

  1. Check Sizing Charts for Desired Fit

    Choose your true size for a standard athletic fit, or size up one size for a relaxed, trendy oversized streetwear aesthetic.

  2. Pair with Versatile Casual Bottoms

    Match with stretch performance golf shorts on the links, or slim-straight dark denim and retro sneakers for weekend casual wear.

  3. Invert Shirt Inside Out Prior to Washing

    Turn the shirt inside out before placing it in the laundry to protect the screen-printed typography from abrasion against washer agitators.

  4. Wash in Cold Water on Gentle Cycle

    Use cold water (below 85°F) with a mild, bleach-free detergent to prevent graphic cracking and fabric color fading.

  5. Air Dry or Tumble Dry on Low Heat

    Tumble dry on the lowest heat setting or hang on a drying rack to preserve collar shape and prevent cotton shrinkage.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Where does the phrase 'Have a Day' come from?

The phrase was popularized by Robby Berger of the hit YouTube golf entertainment channel 'Bob Does Sports' as a celebratory catchphrase.

Q2: What does 'Have a Day' mean in golf culture?

It means enjoying the day to the fullest, celebrating good shots, laughing off bad holes, and prioritizing fun and camaraderie on the course.

Q3: Can you wear a Have a Day t-shirt on a golf course?

Many public and municipal golf courses welcome casual t-shirts, while private country clubs require the collared 'Have a Day' performance polo.

Q4: What material are Have a Day shirts made from?

Official shirts are crafted from premium ring-spun combed cotton, soft cotton-poly blends, or four-way stretch athletic performance fabrics.

Q5: Do Have a Day shirts shrink in the wash?

Official shirts are pre-shrunk, but washing in cold water and drying on low heat ensures minimal to zero shrinkage over time.

Q6: Are Have a Day shirts unisex?

Yes, the classic crewneck t-shirts and hoodies feature a relaxed unisex cut that flatters both men and women.

Q7: What other merchandise does Have a Day make?

The brand produces performance golf polos, hoodies, vintage rope hats, golf towels, drink koozies, and golf headcovers.

Q8: Why is YouTube golf merchandise so popular right now?

Channels like Bob Does Sports brought accessible, relatable humor to a sport historically seen as elitist, creating passionate fan communities.
