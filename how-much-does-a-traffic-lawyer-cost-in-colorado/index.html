How Much Does a Traffic Lawyer Cost in Colorado?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how much does a traffic lawyer cost in Colorado requires analyzing the infraction severity, the driver's license status (commercial CDL vs. standard operator), and county municipal court jurisdiction. On average, hiring a traffic lawyer in Colorado costs between $250 and $750 for basic speeding and point-reduction tickets on a flat-fee basis. Severe traffic misdemeanors—such as reckless driving, careless driving involving injury, or driving under suspension (DUR)—typically cost $1,000 to $2,500.

Colorado DMV Point System and Flat-Fee Legal Defense

In Colorado, the Department of Revenue Division of Motor Vehicles (DMV) enforces an aggressive driver point assessment system under C.R.S. § 42-2-127. An adult driver who accumulates 12 points in 12 consecutive months or 18 points in 24 months faces an automatic driver's license suspension. Minor speeding infractions carry 1 to 4 points, while speeding 20 to 39 MPH over the limit carries 6 points, and reckless driving carries 8 points.

Most Colorado traffic ticket attorneys charge transparent flat fees to handle ticket negotiations, typically ranging from $250 to $600 for standard speeding citations. In the vast majority of cases, the attorney can enter a special appearance on your behalf, meaning you do not have to take time off work or travel to courthouses in Denver, Colorado Springs, Boulder, or rural mountain counties like Eagle or Summit.

Misdemeanor Traffic Offenses vs. Civil Infractions

A critical legal distinction in Colorado is between civil traffic infractions (Class A and B infractions) and criminal traffic misdemeanors (Class 1 and 2 misdemeanors). Civil infractions cannot result in jail time and are resolved through administrative fines. However, speeding 25 MPH or more over the posted speed limit, reckless driving, or leaving the scene of an accident are criminal misdemeanor offenses under Colorado law.

A criminal traffic misdemeanor carries statutory penalties of 10 to 90 days in county jail, fines up to $1,000, and creates a permanent, non-expungeable criminal record. Because these charges carry potential incarceration, defense attorneys bill $1,000 to $2,500. Skilled counsel can routinely negotiate deferred judgments or plea down criminal misdemeanors to civil non-moving infractions.

The True Financial Value: Auto Insurance Savings

The primary economic justification for hiring a Colorado traffic attorney is preventing catastrophic auto insurance rate increases. Simply mailing in payment for a Colorado traffic citation constitutes an automatic guilty plea, triggering immediate point transmission to the DMV. When auto insurance carriers conduct routine annual motor vehicle record (MVR) underwriting audits, a 4 to 6-point moving violation typically triggers a 25% to 45% premium surcharge.

Over the three consecutive years that an infraction remains on an insurance driving record, premium hikes typically cost motorists $1,200 to $2,700 in cumulative extra premiums. A traffic attorney who negotiates your ticket down to a 0-point 'defective vehicle' or non-moving violation prevents insurance surcharges completely, delivering a massive return on a $350 legal fee.

How to Handle a Traffic Ticket in Colorado

Follow these five strategic steps after receiving a traffic citation in Colorado.

  1. Do Not Mail in Payment Immediately

    Paying the fine online or by mail is a formal guilty plea that adds points to your driving record and raises insurance rates.

  2. Check the Appearance Date and Court Location

    Identify whether your ticket is filed in municipal court or county court, and note the mandatory arraignment date.

  3. Consult with a Local Colorado Traffic Defense Lawyer

    Contact an attorney who routinely appears in that specific courthouse to evaluate plea reduction odds.

  4. Execute a Representation Agreement and Waiver

    Sign a legal waiver authorizing your attorney to appear in court on your behalf so you do not have to attend.

  5. Pay Reduced Fines or Complete Traffic School Promptly

    Pay any negotiated court court costs online or complete court-ordered defensive driving school within the assigned deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How much does a traffic lawyer cost in Colorado?

Hiring a traffic lawyer in Colorado typically costs between $250 and $750 for standard speeding tickets, and $1,000 to $2,500 for traffic misdemeanors.

Q2: Can a traffic lawyer get a ticket dismissed in Colorado?

Lawyers frequently get tickets dismissed or reduced to zero-point violations like defective equipment, preventing insurance hikes.

Q3: How many points suspend your license in Colorado?

Accumulating 12 points in 12 months or 18 points in 24 months results in an automatic administrative license suspension for adult drivers.

Q4: Do I have to appear in court if I hire a traffic lawyer in Colorado?

In most infraction cases, your attorney can enter an appearance on your behalf, excusing you from attending court.

Q5: Is speeding 25 over a felony in Colorado?

No, speeding 25+ MPH over the limit is a Class 2 traffic misdemeanor, punishable by fines, DMV points, and potential jail time, but not a felony.

Q6: What is a 'defective vehicle' plea in Colorado?

It is a common plea bargain where a moving violation is reduced to a non-moving equipment infraction carrying zero DMV points.

Q7: How long do points stay on your Colorado driving record?

Points remain on your official Colorado DMV driving record for 7 years, though insurance companies typically rate only the past 3 years.

Q8: Is hiring a traffic lawyer worth it for a speeding ticket?

Yes, avoiding 4 to 6 DMV points prevents insurance rate spikes that typically cost $1,200 to $2,700 over three years.
