Institutional Overview, History, and NVLAP Accreditations

NVL Laboratories, Inc., headquartered in Seattle, Washington, has operated for decades as an industry-leading environmental testing laboratory and industrial hygiene consulting firm. The laboratory is fully accredited under the National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program (NVLAP Lab Code 102063-0) administered by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). This prestigious federal accreditation confirms that NVL Labs maintains absolute compliance with international quality management standards (ISO/IEC 17025) for asbestos fiber analysis.

Beyond NIST NVLAP credentials, NVL Laboratories holds extensive certifications from the American Industrial Hygiene Association (AIHA Laboratory Accreditation Programs) under the Industrial Hygiene Laboratory Accreditation Program (IHLAP). These dual accreditations ensure that environmental data generated by NVL Labs withstands rigorous courtroom scrutiny, environmental regulatory agency audits, and municipal building permit reviews across federal, state, and local jurisdictions.

Analytical Methodologies: PLM, Point Counting, and TEM at NVL Labs

NVL Laboratories offers an exhaustive battery of analytical testing methodologies tailored to diverse material matrices and regulatory thresholds. The primary workhorse for bulk building materials is Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) with dispersion staining in accordance with EPA Method 600/R-93/116. PLM optical testing evaluates crystallographic refractive indices, fiber elongation, and birefringence to identify and quantify chrysotile, amosite, crocidolite, tremolite, anthophyllite, and actinolite fibers.

When initial PLM screening reveals trace asbestos concentrations near the critical one percent regulatory threshold, NVL Labs utilizes EPA 400-Point Count or 1,000-Point Count procedures. Point counting significantly improves statistical accuracy, preventing unnecessary and costly abatement when materials contain less than one percent asbestos. For non-friable organically bound products like vinyl floor tile and roofing tar, NVL Labs deploys Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM), utilizing magnifications up to 50,000x to resolve sub-micron fiber bundles that escape optical microscopes.

Client Services, Sample Submission, and Online Reporting

NVL Laboratories serves a broad clientele spanning licensed environmental consultants, industrial hygiene engineering practices, governmental defense agencies, and private residential property owners. The laboratory provides streamlined walk-in drop-off services at its primary Seattle facility, alongside a nationwide mail-in testing program utilizing digital chain-of-custody tracking.

Clients access test reports through NVL Labs secure online client portal, which delivers downloadable PDF certificates of analysis, detailed photographic documentation of analyzed fibers, and electronic data deliverables (EDD) formatted for municipal building permit attachments. By delivering rigorous scientific precision and rapid turnaround times, NVL Labs remains a vital pillar of environmental safety infrastructure throughout the Western United States.

How to Submit Bulk Asbestos Samples to NVL Laboratories

A practical guide for packaging, documenting, and submitting suspect building materials to NVL Labs for certified testing.

  1. Collect Safe Representative Bulk Samples

    Mist the suspect material with soapy water, extract a one-inch square core sample through all substrate layers, and place it in a clean zip-top plastic bag.

  2. Download and Complete the NVL Chain of Custody Form

    Access the NVL Labs website, download the official asbestos Chain of Custody (COC) form, and record sample IDs, locations, and requested turnaround time.

  3. Package Samples Securely for Transport

    Double-bag all physical sample containers and pack them inside a sturdy shipping envelope alongside the completed Chain of Custody manifest.

  4. Ship or Deliver Samples to NVL Laboratories

    Deliver the package to the NVL Labs walk-in facility in Seattle or send via certified carrier (FedEx/UPS/USPS) with tracking.

  5. Access Certified Laboratory Certificate Online

    Log into the NVL client portal or check your email to download the accredited laboratory test report detailing fiber identification and percentage volume.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Where is NVL Laboratories located?

NVL Laboratories, Inc. is headquartered in Seattle, Washington, operating a state-of-the-art commercial environmental testing facility that serves local walk-in clients and nationwide mail-in submissions.

Q2: What accreditation does NVL Labs hold for asbestos analysis?

NVL Labs is accredited by NIST NVLAP (Lab Code 102063-0) for bulk asbestos testing under ISO/IEC 17025, alongside AIHA LAP credentials for industrial hygiene air testing.

Q3: How much does a standard asbestos test cost at NVL Labs?

Standard Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) bulk sample analysis typically costs between $25 and $45 per sample, with tiered pricing based on requested turnaround time.

Q4: Can homeowners submit their own samples to NVL Laboratories?

Yes, NVL Labs accepts mail-in and drop-off bulk samples from private homeowners, provided samples are double-bagged and accompanied by a completed Chain of Custody form.

Q5: How long does it take NVL Labs to return asbestos test results?

Standard processing turnaround takes two to three business days, though expedited emergency rush services offer same-day or 24-hour turnaround for an additional laboratory fee.

Q6: What is the difference between PLM and TEM at NVL Labs?

PLM uses polarized optical light up to 400x for standard bulk materials, while TEM uses high-powered electron beams up to 50,000x to detect ultra-fine fibers in dense floor tiles and air samples.

Q7: Does NVL Labs perform on-site asbestos removal?

No, NVL Laboratories operates strictly as an independent analytical testing laboratory and industrial hygiene consulting firm; they do not perform removal, preventing conflicts of interest.

Q8: What is an asbestos Chain of Custody (COC) form?

A Chain of Custody form is an essential legal document that tracks sample ownership, collection dates, material descriptions, and requested lab analysis methods from collection to final reporting.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Choosing an accredited testing laboratory like NVL Laboratories guarantees that asbestos assessments are legally defensible, scientifically accurate, and fully compliant with federal EPA and OSHA mandates. Whether verifying suspect residential popcorn ceilings or executing multi-building commercial environmental due diligence, NVL Labs provides the certified diagnostic clarity required to protect human life and structural assets.
