AYFY Cable Full Form: Armoured Electrical Wire

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 18, 2026

The full form of AYFY cable in electrical cable manufacturing and power distribution engineering stands for Aluminium conductor (A), PVC insulated (Y), Flat steel wire armoured (F), and PVC outer sheathed (Y) cable. Governed by Bureau of Indian Standards specifications (IS 1554 Part 1), AYFY cables are heavy-duty armoured power distribution cables designed for underground cabling, industrial cable trays, and harsh mechanical environments.

Understanding AYFY Cable: Armoured Electrical Wire

Electrical power distribution across industrial manufacturing plants, commercial substations, and municipal infrastructure requires cables that withstand severe mechanical stress, moisture ingress, and physical impacts. In standardized cable coding systems defined by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), letters designate the exact material composition of each concentric cable layer. The AYFY cable represents one of the most reliable and economical low-voltage power distribution cables in industrial service.

Decoding the standardized letter formula of an AYFY cable reveals its exact structural anatomy: the initial 'A' indicates an Aluminium conductor; the first 'Y' denotes Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC) core insulation; the 'F' specifies Flat galvanized steel wire armouring (strip armour); and the final 'Y' indicates an extruded PVC outer protective jacket. Operating at rated voltages of 650/1100 volts (1.1 kV), AYFY cables carry high-amperage current across heavy industrial machinery.

A crucial engineering advantage of AYFY flat steel wire armouring compared to round wire armouring (AYWY) is physical surface coverage. Flat steel strips wrap tightly around the inner sheath with minimal gaps, achieving over 90% metallic surface coverage. This dense armour barrier prevents sharp gravel from puncturing the core during direct trench burial under roads or railway crossings.

When terminating an AYFY cable inside motor control centers or distribution boards, electricians must utilize brass double-compression cable glands. These glands clamp the flat steel armour securely to ground, providing continuous low-resistance earth fault paths that instantly trip protective circuit breakers during short-circuit faults.

How to Make a Terminated Gland Connection on an AYFY Armoured Cable

Follow these electrical technician guidelines to strip, prepare, and terminate an AYFY cable using a brass cable gland into an electrical panel.

  1. Isolate Power and Strip Outer PVC Jacket

    Ensure the panel is de-energized, measure the required gland length, and carefully score and strip the outer PVC jacket without nicking the armour.

  2. Trim and Flare Flat Steel Wire Armour

    Cut the flat galvanized steel armour strips with tin snips to the gland collar length and gently splay them outward over the gland cone.

  3. Lock Armour Cone in Double-Compression Gland

    Slide the gland body over the flared armour strips and tighten the compression nut firmly to lock the steel armour in a permanent metallic bond.

  4. Strip Core Insulation and Crimp Aluminium Lugs

    Strip individual core PVC insulation, coat bare aluminium strands with anti-oxidation compound, and crimp bimetallic terminal lugs using a hydraulic crimper.

  5. Fasten Lugs to Busbars and Attach Earth Braid

    Bolt lugs onto switchgear terminals using torque-marked bolts, and bolt a copper earth continuity lead from the gland earth tag to the panel ground bar.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of AYFY cable?

AYFY stands for Aluminium conductor (A), PVC insulated (Y), Flat steel wire armoured (F), and PVC sheathed (Y) cable.

Q2: What does the letter 'A' indicate in cable nomenclature?

The letter 'A' at the beginning of a cable code indicates that the conductor is made of aluminium instead of copper.

Q3: What is the difference between AYFY and AYWY cables?

AYFY uses flat steel strip armouring, while AYWY uses round galvanized steel wire armouring.

Q4: What voltage grade do AYFY cables support?

AYFY cables are standard low-voltage cables rated for 650/1100 volts (1.1 kV) operational power distribution.

Q5: Can an AYFY cable be buried directly underground?

Yes, the heavy flat steel strip armour and PVC outer sheath make AYFY cables ideal for direct underground trench burial.

Q6: Why is anti-oxidation paste used on aluminium cable terminations?

Aluminium oxidizes instantly when exposed to air, forming an insulating oxide film; anti-oxidation paste prevents contact overheating.

Q7: Which Indian standard governs AYFY cable manufacturing?

AYFY cables are manufactured and tested in accordance with Bureau of Indian Standards specification IS 1554 (Part 1).

Q8: What is the maximum continuous operating temperature of an AYFY cable?

Since AYFY utilizes standard PVC insulation, its maximum continuous conductor operating temperature is 70&deg;C.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The AYFY cable (Aluminium Conductor, PVC Insulated, Flat Steel Strip Armoured, PVC Outer Sheathed) is a workhorse of modern electrical infrastructure. Combining lightweight aluminium conductors with rugged flat steel strip armour and moisture-impermeable PVC sheathing, AYFY cables provide reliable, cost-effective power distribution across demanding industrial and underground installations.
