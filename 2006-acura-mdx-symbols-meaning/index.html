2006 Acura Mdx Symbols Meaning

Meaning
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

Cluster Indicator Layout on the 2006 First-Gen Acura MDX

The 2006 model year represents the pinnacle of the first-generation Acura MDX (chassis code YD1), powered by the legendary 3.5-liter J35A7 VTEC V6 engine. The instrument cluster features backlit analog gauges flanked by multi-function warning indicator arrays.

Acura organizes cluster warning lamps into distinct color tiers. Red indicators represent critical emergencies that require an immediate stop—including low engine oil pressure, charging system failure, or brake hydraulic loss. Amber indicators warn of disabled electronic driver aids or emissions faults, such as the Check Engine light, VSA (Vehicle Stability Assist), or SH-AWD / VTM-4 notifications.

Decoding the VTM-4 and SH-AWD Differential System

Depending on production date and trim architecture, first-generation MDX vehicles utilize the Variable Torque Management 4-Wheel Drive (VTM-4) system. This system features an electronic rear differential equipped with dual computer-controlled wet clutch packs.

On the center dashboard, a physical VTM-4 LOCK button allows the driver to manually lock the rear differential in first, second, or reverse gears at speeds below 18 MPH. When engaged, a green VTM-4 LOCK indicator glows on the cluster. However, if the rear differential fluid overheats during severe snow or mud driving, or if differential fluid changes were neglected, the amber VTM-4 warning light illuminates, automatically disabling rear-wheel drive to protect internal clutch packs.

Regularly changing VTM-4 rear differential fluid with genuine Honda/Acura fluid prevents expensive rear axle failures and keeps your MDX trail-ready.

How to Reset the 'MAINT REQD' Light on a 2006 Acura MDX

Follow this manual button sequence to clear the oil change reminder light after performing routine maintenance.

  1. Turn the Ignition Key to OFF Position

    Insert the key into the ignition cylinder and ensure the key is turned to the 0 (LOCK) position.

  2. Press and Hold the Trip Reset / Select Buttons

    Press and firmly hold both the SELECT and RESET buttons located on the instrument cluster face.

  3. Turn Ignition Key to Position II (ON)

    Continue holding both buttons while turning the ignition key two clicks to the ON (II) position without starting the engine.

  4. Hold Buttons for Approximately 10 Seconds

    Keep holding both buttons steadily until the amber 'MAINT REQD' indicator begins to blink and then turns off completely.

  5. Release Buttons and Verify Reset

    Release the buttons and start the engine; verify that the maintenance reminder light remains extinguished.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does MAINT REQD mean on a 2006 Acura MDX?

MAINT REQD is a mileage-based reminder indicating that 7,500 miles have passed since the last oil change and routine service is due.

Q2: Why is the VTM-4 light on in my 2006 Acura MDX?

The VTM-4 light indicates a fault in the electronic rear differential, often caused by old fluid, overheating, or a bad pressure switch.

Q3: What does a flashing D or D5 light mean on a 2006 Acura MDX?

A flashing D5 light indicates an automatic transmission trouble code, commonly a failing 3rd or 4th gear transmission fluid pressure switch.

Q4: Why did my VSA light and Check Engine light come on together?

Acura programming automatically disables Vehicle Stability Assist (VSA) whenever any engine code triggers, illuminating both lights.

Q5: Can I drive my 2006 Acura MDX with the VTM-4 light on?

Yes, but the vehicle will operate strictly in front-wheel drive, leaving you without all-wheel drive traction on slippery surfaces.

Q6: What does the exclamation point inside a triangle mean on an MDX?

This is the Vehicle Stability Assist (VSA) activation light; it flashes during active skid correction or stays lit if VSA is disabled.

Q7: What rear differential fluid does a 2006 Acura MDX require?

It requires genuine Acura/Honda VTM-4 Differential Fluid; using standard gear oil will ruin the internal electromagnetic clutch packs.

Q8: How do you turn off the VTM-4 lock?

Press the VTM-4 LOCK button on the dashboard, shift out of low gears into D, or accelerate past 18 MPH where it automatically disengages.
