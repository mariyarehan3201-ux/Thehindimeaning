POTS in a Box AT&T

The abbreviation POTS stands for Plain Old Telephone Service, representing the venerable twisted-pair analog copper infrastructure that anchored telecommunications for well over a century. In response to nationwide copper line retirements, regulatory policy shifts by the Federal Communications Commission, and soaring copper maintenance tariffs, telecommunications giant AT&T developed and deployed AT&T Phone for Business-Advanced, colloquially known throughout the enterprise networking sector as POTS in a Box. This specialized communications appliance bridges legacy analog telephony hardware—including life-safety fire alarm communicators, emergency elevator call boxes, security burglar alarms, analog fax machines, and facility utility meters—directly onto robust commercial cellular LTE and 5G wireless broadband networks without requiring costly upgrades to downstream building equipment.
