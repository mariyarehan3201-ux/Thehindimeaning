MMCH Full Form: Murshidabad Medical College Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The acronym MMCH stands for Murshidabad Medical College and Hospital (and regionally Malda Medical College and Hospital or Medinipur Medical College and Hospital). Established in 2012 by the Government of West Bengal in Berhampore, Murshidabad, MMCH is a premier state government medical teaching college and tertiary healthcare hospital. Recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and affiliated with the West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS), MMCH delivers undergraduate MBBS and postgraduate medical degrees while providing subsidized critical clinical healthcare across central Bengal.

Healthcare Infrastructure and Tertiary Medical Education at MMCH

Expanding accessible healthcare across densely populated rural and semi-urban regions is a top public health priority in India. For decades, patients from Murshidabad, Malda, and surrounding districts had to travel five to six hours to Kolkata hospitals for complex surgeries and specialized medical care. Murshidabad Medical College and Hospital (MMCH) was established in 2012 to bring world-class medical facilities directly to central Bengal.

Integrated around the existing Berhampore General Hospital, MMCH modern campus was developed with modern lecture theaters, digital libraries, dissection halls, and residential hostels for medical trainees. Today, MMCH functions as both an academic center training future doctors and a tertiary healthcare lifeline for millions of rural residents.

Through subsidized hospital wards, free emergency diagnostic services, and government healthcare schemes like Swasthya Sathi, MMCH ensures that financial hardship never prevents citizens from receiving life-saving medical treatment.

Super-Specialty Diagnostic Units, Blood Bank Operations, and Community Health Programs

Medical students at MMCH undergo rigorous academic and bedside clinical training over their four-and-a-half-year MBBS course, followed by a mandatory one-year rotating clinical internship. Because MMCH operates as a high-volume district referral hospital, trainees encounter diverse clinical pathology—ranging from tropical infectious diseases to acute agricultural toxicology and complex obstetric emergencies.

This extensive clinical case volume equips MMCH graduates with exceptional clinical acumen, preparing them to excel in national postgraduate medical entrance examinations (NEET-PG / INI-CET).

How to Secure an MBBS Seat at Murshidabad Medical College (MMCH)

  1. Complete 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology

    Pass higher secondary board examinations securing at least 50% aggregate marks in PCB (40% for reserved categories) and pass English.

  2. Qualify the National NEET-UG Examination

    Appear for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) and achieve a competitive state or all-India merit rank.

  3. Register on the WBMCC Counseling Portal

    Register for the West Bengal Medical Counseling Committee (wbmcc.nic.in) 85% state quota or the MCC 15% All-India Quota.

  4. Select MMCH During Online Choice Filling

    Lock Murshidabad Medical College and Hospital as your preferred choice based on your category rank during counseling rounds.

  5. Report to Berhampore Campus for Physical Verification

    Submit original NEET scorecards, domicile certificates, caste verification, pay nominal state tuition fees, and complete admission.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of MMCH in West Bengal healthcare?

MMCH stands for Murshidabad Medical College and Hospital (also Malda or Medinipur Medical College).

Q2: Where is Murshidabad Medical College located?

It is located in Berhampore, the administrative headquarters of Murshidabad district in West Bengal.

Q3: When was Murshidabad Medical College established?

MMCH was established in 2012 by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of West Bengal.

Q4: How many MBBS seats are sanctioned annually at MMCH?

Murshidabad Medical College is approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) for 125 MBBS admissions annually.

Q5: What university oversees academic examinations at MMCH?

It is affiliated with the West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS), Kolkata.

Q6: What medical facilities are available at MMCH Hospital?

24/7 emergency trauma care, critical care ICU/CCU, blood bank, dialysis unit, digital radiology, and multi-specialty surgical suites.

Q7: Does MMCH offer postgraduate (MD/MS) specialty seats?

Yes, MMCH offers postgraduate residency seats across general medicine, obstetrics/gynecology, pediatrics, and community medicine.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Murshidabad Medical College and Hospital (MMCH) is a cornerstone of healthcare and medical education in West Bengal. By combining accredited MBBS medical training with advanced tertiary hospital care in Berhampore, MMCH ensures that quality medical treatment and skilled doctors remain accessible to rural and semi-urban communities throughout the region.
