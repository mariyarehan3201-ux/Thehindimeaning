How Much Does a Boob Job Cost in Colorado?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how much does a boob job cost in colorado requires examining surgeon credentials, surgical facility fees, implant material chemistry, and regional cost of living. In Colorado—primarily across the Denver metropolitan area, Boulder, and Colorado Springs—a primary cosmetic breast augmentation typically costs between $6,000 and $12,000, with an average overall turnkey cost of approximately $7,800 to $9,200. Traditional saline implants sit at the lower end ($6,000 to $7,500), while modern cohesive silicone 'gummy bear' implants range from $8,000 to $11,500.

Colorado Regional Pricing: Denver Metro vs. Mountain Resort Towns

In the Colorado aesthetic medicine market, geographic location plays a substantial role in total surgical fees. The Denver metropolitan area (including Cherry Creek, Englewood, and Lone Tree) is the primary hub for board-certified plastic surgery, featuring high competition among accredited surgical centers that keeps average all-inclusive prices competitive between $7,200 and $8,800.

In contrast, seeking plastic surgery in affluent mountain resort communities (such as Aspen or Vail) or exclusive Boulder boutique practices can drive prices up to $10,000 to $14,000 due to higher facility overhead and specialized luxury concierge care. Conversely, practices in Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, and Pueblo offer high-quality primary breast augmentation at slightly more economical rates ($6,500 to $8,000).

Implant Technology: Saline vs. Silicone vs. Gummy Bear

The choice of breast implant shell and internal filler material represents a significant cost variable. Traditional saline implants—filled with sterile saltwater after insertion through a small incision—are the most affordable, costing roughly $1,000 to $1,500 less than silicone. Saline implants provide a firm feel and allow smaller surgical incisions, but carry a higher risk of visible rippling in lean, athletic women with minimal native breast tissue.

Modern FDA-approved cohesive silicone gel implants (such as Mentor MemoryGel, Allergan Inspira, or Sientra) are the gold standard in Colorado, chosen by over 80% of patients. Silicone gel mimics natural breast tissue softness and natural contour. Highly cohesive 'gummy bear' form-stable implants feature cross-linked silicone gel that maintains its shape even if cut in half, costing $1,500 to $2,500 more than saline.

Combining Procedures: Breast Lift (Mastopexy) Add-On Costs

Many women in Colorado seek breast augmentation after pregnancy, breastfeeding, or significant weight loss, where deflation is accompanied by moderate-to-severe skin laxity and drooping (ptosis). A breast implant alone only adds fullness; it cannot lift a sagging nipple that sits below the inframammary fold.

In these cases, plastic surgeons perform an augmentation-mastopexy (combining breast implants with a surgical breast lift). Performing a simultaneous breast lift involves removing excess stretched skin, reshaping breast tissue, and repositioning the nipple-areola complex higher on the chest wall. Adding a breast lift extends surgical time from 1.5 hours to 3.5 hours, increasing total combined costs to $11,000 to $16,500.

How to Choose a Breast Augmentation Surgeon in Colorado

Follow these five tactical steps to evaluate plastic surgeons and plan your surgical budget.

  1. Verify American Board of Plastic Surgery (ABPS) Certification

    Ensure your surgeon is certified by the ABPS; avoid general cosmetic doctors who lack accredited plastic surgery residencies.

  2. Confirm Surgery Is Performed in an Accredited Facility

    Verify the operating suite is accredited by AAAASF, AAAHC, or is an in-network licensed hospital.

  3. Demand an All-Inclusive Turnkey Written Quote

    Ensure the price quote includes surgeon fees, implant hardware, anesthesia, OR time, and all post-op follow-up visits.

  4. Experience 3D Virtual Imaging Simulation (Crisalix / Vectra)

    Utilize 3D photographic simulations during your consultation to preview saline vs. silicone volume on your actual frame.

  5. Explore Patient Medical Financing (CareCredit / Alphaeon)

    Apply for 0% promotional APR healthcare financing to split fees into manageable $200-$400 monthly payments.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the average cost of a boob job in Colorado?

The average all-inclusive cost in Colorado ranges between $7,500 and $9,500 for silicone implants.

Q2: Why is silicone more expensive than saline in Colorado?

Silicone gel implants require sophisticated manufacturing and carry higher manufacturer purchase costs than saline bags ($1,000-$1,500 difference).

Q3: Does Colorado health insurance cover breast augmentation?

No, cosmetic breast augmentation is an elective procedure not covered by insurance; post-mastectomy cancer reconstruction is 100% covered.

Q4: How long is recovery after breast augmentation?

Most patients return to desk jobs in 5 to 7 days, resume light cardio at 3 weeks, and return to full upper-body workouts at 6 to 8 weeks.

Q5: What is a gummy bear breast implant?

It is a highly cohesive form-stable silicone gel implant that holds its shape like a gummy candy even if the outer shell is cut.

Q6: How much does a breast lift with implants cost in Denver?

An augmentation-mastopexy typically costs between $11,000 and $16,000 due to extended operating time.

Q7: How often do breast implants need to be replaced?

Modern implants are not lifetime devices, but they do not need replacement at 10 years unless a leak, rupture, or capsular contracture occurs.

Q8: What is capsular contracture?

It is an immune response where scar tissue tightens around the implant, making the breast firm or painful; modern warranties cover replacement.
