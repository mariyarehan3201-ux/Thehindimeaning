Is Sternglas a Good Brand?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

Yes, Sternglas is an exceptionally good, legitimate, and highly respected German microbrand watch company that delivers authentic Bauhaus design philosophy at accessible consumer price points. Founded in 2016 by Dustin Fontaine in Hamburg, Germany, Sternglas directly challenges overpriced designer fashion watches (like Daniel Wellington or MVMT) by equipping every single timepiece with scratch-resistant double-domed sapphire crystal, surgical 316L stainless steel cases, and reliable Japanese or Swiss movements (such as Miyota, Seiko, or Ronda). For horology enthusiasts seeking sleek German minimalism without paying thousands for a Nomos Glashütte or Junghans Max Bill, Sternglas is a fantastic choice.

The Bauhaus Philosophy: Form Follows Function in Modern Horology

To evaluate Sternglas as a horological brand, one must understand the iconic German design movement that inspires every dial it produces. Founded in Weimar, Germany in 1919 by architect Walter Gropius, the Bauhaus school of art and design championed a revolutionary aesthetic principle: 'Form follows function.' Bauhaus design rejects ornate flourishes, frivolous embellishments, and busy clutter in favor of geometric clarity, clean typography, and purposeful legibility.

Sternglas embraces this century-old German tradition with authentic conviction. The dials on their signature Naos, Hamburg, and Topograph collections feature clean line indices, slender baton hands, subtle date windows, and symmetrical proportions. Unlike cheap fashion watch brands that slap minimalist dials on throwaway brass cases, Sternglas crafts high-grade cases from solid 316L surgical stainless steel, featuring satin-brushed and high-polish finishes that catch light elegantly.

Horological Hardware: Sapphire Crystal and Reliable Workhorse Movements

In the enthusiast watch community, the definitive litmus test for whether an affordable brand is taken seriously is its component hardware. Sternglas passes this test with flying colors. A standout feature across the entire Sternglas catalog—even on their sub-$300 entry models—is the inclusion of genuine double-domed sapphire crystal treated with an anti-reflective (AR) coating. Sapphire measures 9 on the Mohs Hardness Scale (second only to diamond), meaning the crystal glass will remain completely pristine and free of scratches for decades.

For internal mechanics, Sternglas avoids unproven calibers, partnering instead with the world's most proven movement manufacturers. Their quartz models utilize Swiss Ronda quartz movements celebrated for battery longevity and timing precision. Their mechanical automatic collections utilize Japanese workhorse calibers—such as the Miyota 8215, the high-beat Miyota 9015 (sweeping at 28,800 vibrations per hour), and the Seiko NH35. These movements are robust, highly accurate, and can be serviced easily by any independent watchmaker worldwide.

Strap Quality, Quick-Release Architecture, and Customer Service

Sternglas pays meticulous attention to strap craftsmanship. The brand partners with traditional German leather tanneries in the Bavarian town of Schramberg, crafting supple, vegetable-tanned genuine leather straps that age with a rich natural patina. All Sternglas straps feature integrated quick-release spring bars, allowing owners to swap from casual vintage leather to sleek stainless steel Milanese mesh bands in ten seconds without requiring specialized jeweler tools.

Customer reviews consistently praise Sternglas for transparent shipping, fair two-year international manufacturer warranties, and responsive customer service based out of Hamburg. By maintaining direct-to-consumer digital distribution alongside select authorized European jewelers, Sternglas keeps retail prices remarkably accessible while upholding high German quality control standards.

How to Set Up and Maintain a Sternglas Automatic Watch

Step-by-step operating and care instructions for a Sternglas automatic mechanical timepiece.

  1. Manually Wind the Watch to Initiate the Mainspring

    Unscrew or pull the crown out to Position 0 (flush against the case). Rotate the crown clockwise 20 to 30 times to manually wind the mainspring and jumpstart the automatic balance wheel.

  2. Avoid the 'Danger Zone' When Setting the Date

    Never quick-set the date wheel between 9:00 PM and 3:00 AM, as the internal date change gears are actively engaged. Advance the hands past midnight first before setting the calendar date.

  3. Set the Exact Time and Sync with Reference Atomic Clock

    Pull the crown out to Position 2 to engage the 'hacking' mechanism (which stops the second hand). Turn the crown clockwise to align minute and hour hands with a certified atomic clock.

  4. Utilize the Quick-Release Spring Bars to Swap Straps

    Use your fingernail to slide the tiny metal pin on the underside of the strap inward. The strap pops out instantly, allowing you to switch between leather and Milanese mesh bands effortlessly.

  5. Protect from Magnetism and Excessive Water Immersion

    Keep the watch away from strong magnetic fields (laptop speakers, magnetic phone cases) to avoid magnetized hairsprings. Ensure crown is pushed in before light water exposure (5 ATM splash rating).

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Where are Sternglas watches made?

Sternglas timepieces are designed, developed, and quality-inspected at the brand's headquarters in Hamburg, Germany, utilizing movements from Japan and Switzerland.

Q2: Does Sternglas use real sapphire crystal?

Yes. Every Sternglas watch model features scratch-resistant double-domed sapphire crystal glass with an anti-reflective coating, which cannot be scratched by keys or everyday objects.

Q3: How long does a Sternglas automatic watch run on a full wind?

Sternglas automatic watches feature a power reserve of approximately 40 to 42 hours, keeping time continuously when worn daily on the wrist.

Q4: Are Sternglas watches waterproof?

Most Sternglas dress watches feature a water resistance rating of 5 ATM (50 meters), meaning they are safe against rain, hand washing, and light splashes, but not suitable for swimming.

Q5: How does Sternglas compare to Nomos Glashütte?

Nomos is a high-end luxury manufacture ($2,000 to $4,000) with in-house German calibers, while Sternglas provides the same Bauhaus minimalist style for $300 to $600 using outsourced Japanese movements.

Q6: What movements do Sternglas watches use?

Sternglas uses proven Japanese automatic calibers (Miyota 8215, Miyota 9015, and Seiko NH35) and Swiss Ronda quartz movements.

Q7: What is Bauhaus watch design?

Bauhaus watch design focuses on radical simplicity, geometric harmony, and functional legibility, eliminating unnecessary decorative elements from the watch face.

Q8: What warranty does Sternglas offer?

Sternglas provides a standard 2-year international warranty against manufacturing defects on all timepieces purchased through authorized channels.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

To conclude, Sternglas is a good, highly reputable watch brand that delivers genuine horological value. By honoring authentic German Bauhaus design principles and equipping timepieces with scratch-proof sapphire crystal, solid 316L steel, and reliable Japanese and Swiss movements, Sternglas provides the sophisticated aesthetic of high-luxury European timepieces at an attainable price point. For professionals, creatives, and watch collectors seeking an elegant, reliable daily timepiece, Sternglas is an outstanding investment.
