How to Arrange and Preserve a Pristine White Flower Display

Follow these florist-grade design techniques to create a breathtaking all-white floral arrangement.

  1. Step 1

    Scrub your vase thoroughly with warm soapy water and bleach to eliminate all petal-browning bacteria.

  2. Step 2

    Fill the vase with cool water mixed with commercial floral nutrient preservative.

  3. Step 3

    Select white blossoms with diverse textures: combine flat white roses, ruffled hydrangeas, and spiky snapdragons.

  4. Step 4

    Incorporate deep green foliage (such as salal, Italian ruscus, or eucalyptus) to provide high-contrast framing for white petals.

  5. Step 5

    Trim stems at a sharp 45-degree angle under running water, removing any leaves that would sit below the waterline.

  6. Step 6

    Pluck brown pollen anthers from lilies immediately to prevent indelible yellow pollen from staining white petals.

  7. Step 7

    Place the arrangement in a cool location away from direct sunlight, air conditioner vents, and fruit bowls.
