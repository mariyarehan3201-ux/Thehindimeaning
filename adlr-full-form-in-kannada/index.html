ADLR Full Form in Kannada: Assistant Land Records

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of ADLR in Kannada is ಭೂದಾಖಲೆಗಳ ಸಹಾಯಕ ನಿರ್ದೇಶಕರು (Assistant Director of Land Records). In English, ADLR stands for Assistant Director of Land Records, an important gazetted administrative officer in the Department of Survey Settlement and Land Records under the Revenue Department of Karnataka. Functioning primarily at the taluk and sub-divisional levels, the ADLR exercises statutory authority over land demarcations, cadastral surveys, official land mutations, spatial boundary dispute resolutions, and the maintenance of digital parcel records under Karnataka's acclaimed Bhoomi and Mojini land administration systems.

Land administration forms the foundational bedrock of rural stability, agricultural commerce, and urban development throughout India. Clear, unchallengeable documentation of land ownership, boundaries, and soil classifications prevents prolonged litigation and ensures equitable revenue collection. In the state of Karnataka, where digital governance pioneered transformative land record projects such as Bhoomi, the Department of Survey Settlement and Land Records plays an essential role in certifying property boundaries and maintaining cartographic precision.

Within this administrative hierarchy, the Assistant Director of Land Records serves as the crucial operational authority at the taluk and sub-divisional levels. While the Deputy Commissioner (DC) and Tahsildar manage general revenue administration and executive magisterial duties, the ADLR specializes exclusively in the technical, geodetic, and cartographic aspects of real estate parcels. All licensed government surveyors, technical survey supervisors, and draughtsmen within the sub-division operate under the direct administrative supervision of the ADLR.

The modernization of Karnataka's land survey ecosystem has dramatically transformed the daily responsibilities of the ADLR. Historically, land surveys relied on physical cross-staffs, Gunter chains, and manual paper sketching, processes susceptible to measurement inaccuracies and tampering. Under contemporary reforms, the ADLR oversees the deployment of Electronic Total Stations (ETS), differential GPS (DGPS), and drone-assisted aerial surveys under the national SVAMITVA scheme, guaranteeing millimeter-level accuracy for rural land parcels.

For landowners and farmers across Karnataka, engaging with the ADLR office is vital when selling property, accessing government agricultural grants, or resolving family land inheritance distributions. By securing an ADLR-approved 11E sketch and executing a formal Tatkal Podi, property owners secure undisputed legal recognition of their land, insulating their generational assets against encroachments and disputes.

How to Apply for Land Survey and Mutation Services via the ADLR Office

  1. Obtain Updated Land Title Deeds and RTC (Pahani)

    Download certified copies of your Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops (RTC/Pahani) and registered sale deeds from the official Bhoomi portal.

  2. Submit a Survey Application (Mojini) at the Nada Kacheri

    Register an official request for land survey, Tatkal Podi (land partitioning), or boundary demarcation through the Mojini online software at your local citizen service center.

  3. Pay Prescribed Government Survey Fees

    Generate an online challan and pay the stipulated statutory survey and inspection fees to receive an official application tracking number.

  4. Attend On-Site Cadastral Survey with Government Surveyors

    Be present during the scheduled on-site GPS/ETS survey conducted by the licensed surveyor and survey supervisor under the jurisdiction of the ADLR.

  5. Obtain Final Certified 11E Sketch and Mutation Order

    Collect the verified 11E sketch, Tippani, and spatial demarcation certificate duly approved and digitally signed by the Assistant Director of Land Records.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of ADLR in Kannada?

The full form of ADLR in Kannada is Assistant Director of Land Records, translated as ಭೂದಾಖಲೆಗಳ ಸಹಾಯಕ ನಿರ್ದೇಶಕರು (Bhoodakhalegala Sahayaka Nirdeshakaru).

Q2: What does ADLR stand for in English?

In English, ADLR stands for Assistant Director of Land Records, a gazetted officer in the state revenue administration.

Q3: Which government department does the ADLR belong to in Karnataka?

The ADLR operates within the Department of Survey Settlement and Land Records (SSLR) under the Government of Karnataka Revenue Department.

Q4: What is the primary role of an ADLR?

The ADLR oversees cadastral surveys, boundary reconciliations, village map updates, Tatkal Podi partitioning, and spatial land dispute resolution.

Q5: What is the Mojini system managed under land records?

Mojini is Karnataka's specialized web-based application software used by the survey department to process land survey applications and generate 11E sketches.

Q6: How is an ADLR recruited in Karnataka?

ADLR officers are recruited through the Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) Gazetted Probationers competitive examination or promoted from senior survey supervisors.

Q7: Can an ADLR resolve boundary disputes between adjacent landowners?

Yes, the ADLR possesses quasi-judicial survey powers to inspect land records, order re-surveys, and issue authoritative boundary demarcation orders.

Q8: What is a 11E sketch approved by the ADLR office?

A 11E sketch is a statutory pre-registration land sketch prepared by a licensed surveyor and approved by the ADLR certifying the exact spatial boundaries of a partitioned property.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Assistant Director of Land Records is an indispensable administrative pillar safeguarding property rights and geographical certainty across Karnataka. Through rigorous cadastral surveys, advanced digital spatial platforms like Mojini, and quasi-judicial dispute resolution, the ADLR ensures that every square meter of land is accurately measured, recorded, and protected under state law. Understanding the ADLR's statutory functions empowers citizens to navigate property management with total legal confidence.
