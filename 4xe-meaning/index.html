4xe Meaning

Meaning
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

In automotive engineering, modern hybrid powertrains, and off-road vehicle development, '4xe' (pronounced 'four-by-e') is Jeep's proprietary trademark designation for its advanced Plug-In Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) powertrain technology—powering vehicles like the Jeep Wrangler 4xe and Grand Cherokee 4xe—combining traditional trail-rated four-wheel-drive (4x4) capability with electric propulsion, instant torque delivery, and zero-emission electric driving.

The Dual-Motor Architecture and Mechanical 4x4 Drivetrain

Many modern hybrid crossovers utilize electric all-wheel-drive setups where a combustion engine powers the front wheels while a small electric motor powers the rear axle through software without physical connection. In contrast, the Jeep 4xe retains a traditional, heavy-duty mechanical four-wheel-drive system: front and rear solid Dana 44 axles, mechanical locking differentials, a heavy-duty two-speed transfer case, and physical steel driveshafts.

The 4xe powertrain pairs a 2.0-liter turbocharged inline-4 direct-injection engine with two electric motors: a small liquid-cooled motor-generator unit (eTorque) replacing the traditional alternator on the front of the engine, and a powerful traction motor integrated directly into the 8-speed Torqueflite automatic transmission in place of a traditional torque converter. A 400-volt, 17-kilowatt-hour lithium-ion battery pack mounted safely under the rear seats delivers roughly 21 to 25 miles of pure electric driving range before transitioning seamlessly into standard hybrid propulsion.

The Three E-Selec Driving Modes: Hybrid, Electric, and eSave

To maximize energy management across different driving environments, Jeep 4xe vehicles feature three driver-selectable 'E-Selec' dashboard push-buttons located to the left of the steering wheel: Hybrid Mode, Electric Mode, and eSave Mode.

In 'Hybrid Mode' (the default setting), the computer automatically blends torque from the 2.0L turbo engine and the transmission electric motor, prioritizing battery power first. In 'Electric Mode,' the vehicle operates as a 100% pure electric vehicle until the battery reaches minimum reserve or the driver pushes past wide-open throttle. In 'eSave Mode,' the vehicle drives exclusively on the gasoline engine, preserving battery capacity for silent off-road rock crawling on wilderness trails.

How to Operate and Charge a Jeep 4xe Efficiently

Essential ownership guide for maximizing fuel savings and battery health.

  1. Plug In Daily with Level 1 or Level 2 Charging

    Charge overnight using the standard 120V home wall outlet (12 hours) or install a 240V Level 2 charger (2.5 hours) to begin every day with full EV range.

  2. Engage 'Max Regen' for Urban Stop-and-Go Driving

    Press the Max Regen button on the center dashboard to recover maximum braking energy into the battery during city traffic decel.

  3. Use eSave Mode During Sustained Highway Cruising

    On highway road trips, switch to eSave to let the gas engine run at its most efficient cruising speed, saving electric charge for city streets.

  4. Understand Fuel and Oil Refresh Mode (FORM)

    If the gas engine runs continuously despite a full battery, the system is in FORM to burn off condensation and unburnt fuel from the engine oil.

  5. Inspect Waterproof Battery Seals After Deep Water Fording

    The 4xe battery is fully sealed and waterproof up to 30 inches of water fording; inspect underbody skid plates following extreme trail runs.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does 4xe mean on a Jeep?

4xe (pronounced 'four-by-e') is Jeep's trademark name for its plug-in hybrid electric (PHEV) four-wheel-drive powertrain technology.

Q2: How far can a Jeep 4xe drive on electric power alone?

The Jeep Wrangler 4xe delivers an EPA-estimated 21 to 25 miles of pure zero-emission electric range before the gas engine activates.

Q3: Do you have to plug in a Jeep 4xe to drive it?

No. If you cannot plug it in, the vehicle operates automatically as a standard gas-electric hybrid, charging the battery through regenerative braking.

Q4: What is FORM mode in a Jeep 4xe?

FORM stands for Fuel and Oil Refresh Mode, an automated maintenance mode where the gas engine runs to burn off unburnt fuel and moisture in the oil.

Q5: Can a Jeep 4xe go through deep water?

Yes. The high-voltage battery and electronics are completely waterproof and sealed, allowing fording through up to 30 inches of water, just like standard Jeeps.

Q6: How long does it take to charge a Jeep 4xe?

A standard 120V household wall outlet charges the battery in approximately 12 hours; a 240V Level 2 charging station charges it in about 2.5 hours.

Q7: Is the Jeep 4xe fast?

Yes. With 375 horsepower and 470 lb-ft of instant torque, the 4xe accelerates from 0 to 60 MPH in roughly 6.0 seconds, making it faster than standard V6 models.
