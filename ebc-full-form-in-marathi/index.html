EBC Full Form in Marathi: Meaning, Scheme & Benefits

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The acronym EBC stands for Economically Backward Class, officially translated and known in Marathi as आर्थिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग (Aarthikdrushtya Magas Varg). It is an affirmative social welfare category created by state and central governments, particularly prominent in Maharashtra under higher education department initiatives. The EBC designation enables deserving students from economically underprivileged families belonging to the general or open category to receive substantial tuition and exam fee concessions through online portals like MahaDBT.

EBC चा मराठीत सविस्तर अर्थ आणि ऐतिहासिक पार्श्वभूमी

Higher professional education in technical, medical, and general academic streams requires substantial financial investment. In Maharashtra, the Economically Backward Class (EBC) framework provides vital economic support so that financial constraints do not derail the academic dreams of talented youth.

Formally recognized as आर्थिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग (Aarthikdrushtya Magas Varg) in regional Marathi administrative documents, this classification provides financial relief without requiring caste certificates. The cornerstone initiative executing this benefit is the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna, administered through the centralized MahaDBT platform.

Under this welfare policy, students admitted to approved undergraduate and postgraduate courses—such as engineering, pharmacy, architecture, management, and arts and commerce—receive direct reimbursements toward tuition and examination fees.

पात्रता निकष, उत्पन्नाची मर्यादा आणि अनिवार्य कागदपत्रे

Applicants must carefully maintain proper documentation before the opening of the MahaDBT academic portal each year. Any mismatch in Aadhaar names, bank account linkage, or expired income certificates leads to rejection or delayed disbursements.

The tuition fee concession is divided into slabs depending on annual family earnings. Students with family income under 2.5 lakh rupees and those between 2.5 lakh and 8 lakh rupees receive tailored tuition and examination rebates disbursed directly to their college account.

By streamlining social welfare through digital systems, the government ensures transparent subsidy allocation, enabling thousands of students to pursue engineering, medical, and arts degrees regardless of family financial constraints.

How to Apply for EBC Scholarship on MahaDBT Portal

  1. Visit the Official MahaDBT Portal

    Navigate to the Maharashtra State MahaDBT portal (mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in) and create a verified citizen user account using your Aadhaar number.

  2. Select Higher and Technical Education Department

    Locate the Directorate of Higher Education (DHE) or Technical Education (DTE) schemes and select the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna.

  3. Fill Personal and Family Details

    Enter applicant personal details, permanent Maharashtra domicile information, and upload an authentic annual family income certificate from the Tahsildar.

  4. Input College and Course Credentials

    Enter your verified college admission application ID, course name, fee structure receipts, and CAP allotment letter.

  5. Submit and Track Application Status

    Review all attached documents, submit the final online form, and track application scrutiny by your college clerk and departmental officers.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of EBC in Marathi?

In Marathi, EBC stands for Economically Backward Class, translated as आर्थिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग (Aarthikdrushtya Magas Varg).

Q2: What is the family income limit for the EBC scheme in Maharashtra?

Under the Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna, the annual family income limit is generally up to 8 lakh rupees.

Q3: What financial benefits does an EBC student receive?

Eligible students receive fifty percent concession on tuition fees and examination fees in government-aided and private un-aided professional colleges.

Q4: Can reserved category students (SC/ST/OBC) apply for EBC?

No, EBC fee concessions are designated for Open or General category students who do not avail caste-based scholarships.

Q5: Is a Tahsildar income certificate mandatory for EBC?

Yes, a valid income certificate for the relevant financial year issued by a competent revenue authority like the Tahsildar or Sub-Divisional Officer is mandatory.

Q6: Is admission through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) required?

Yes, to claim EBC tuition fee reimbursement, admission must be secured through the government CAP round rather than management quotas.

Q7: How many children from one family can avail EBC benefits?

In Maharashtra, the EBC fee concession is typically restricted to a maximum of two children per family.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Economically Backward Class (EBC), known in Marathi as आर्थिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग, represents an indispensable welfare pillar for open-category students in Maharashtra. By granting fifty percent tuition fee concessions via the MahaDBT system, the EBC program ensures that talent and hard work—not family income—dictate an ambitious student educational future.
