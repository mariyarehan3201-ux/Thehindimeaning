SMHS Full Form: Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of SMHS in public healthcare, clinical medicine, and medical education in India is Shri Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital (popularly known as SMHS Hospital, Srinagar). Established in 1948 and named after the last ruling Maharaja of Jammu and Kashmir, SMHS is the premier tertiary care teaching hospital affiliated with Government Medical College (GMC), Srinagar. Operating as the largest multi-specialty government referral hospital in the Kashmir Valley, SMHS provides advanced surgical and medical care.

What Is SMHS Hospital and What Does It Stand For?

In medical education, tertiary emergency healthcare, and public health infrastructure in Northern India, SMHS stands for Shri Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital. Commissioned in 1948 in the Karan Nagar district of Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, the hospital was founded under the royal patronage of Maharaja Hari Singh to establish modern, state-of-the-art allopathic medical care in the Kashmir Valley. Following Indian independence, SMHS grew into the primary clinical teaching hospital for Government Medical College (GMC), Srinagar, which was founded in 1959.

With an inpatient bed capacity exceeding 750 to 800 beds and treating thousands of outpatient department (OPD) patients daily, SMHS Hospital serves as the tertiary referral lifeline for all ten districts of the Kashmir Valley, as well as peripheral patients from Ladakh and Jammu regions. It provides subsidized and free advanced clinical treatments, trauma emergency management, and specialized super-specialty interventions to the general public.

Clinical Departments and Medical Specialties at SMHS

Operating as the flagship teaching hospital of GMC Srinagar, SMHS houses diverse clinical, surgical, diagnostic, and research departments. It trains hundreds of undergraduate MBBS medical students, postgraduate MD/MS resident doctors, and nursing scholars annually.

The hospital operates advanced intensive care units (ICU), surgical oncology suites, round-the-clock trauma resuscitation wards, and modern diagnostic radiology suites equipped with MRI, CT scan, and automated biochemistry analyzers. Pioneering Ocular Trauma Care and Emergency Response

Over decades of serving in a geopolitically sensitive border region, SMHS Hospital has achieved international medical acclaim for its extraordinary surgical capabilities in managing complex ocular and blast trauma. The Department of Ophthalmology at SMHS is recognized as one of the busiest and most experienced eye trauma centers in South Asia.

Ophthalmic surgeons at SMHS pioneered advanced micro-surgical techniques to repair ruptured globes, extract intraocular foreign bodies, and perform restorative vitreo-retinal surgeries on patients presenting with severe projectile and shrapnel injuries, preserving vision for thousands of vulnerable patients under extreme emergency pressure.

Infrastructure Modernization and Golden Card Integration

In recent years, the Government of Jammu and Kashmir, with support from the central Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, has invested heavily in modernizing SMHS Hospital. Upgrades include a multi-storey emergency block, expanded diagnostic laboratories, digital telemedicine linkages connecting remote district hospitals, and automated centralized medical gas pipelines.

Under the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and SEHAT universal health insurance scheme (Golden Card), SMHS provides completely cashless surgical procedures, diagnostic investigations, and free medications, ensuring that socio-economic status is never a barrier to life-saving medical care.

How to Access Outpatient (OPD) and Emergency Services at SMHS Hospital

  1. Visit the Hospital Reception / OPD Registration Counter

    Arrive at the SMHS hospital campus in Karan Nagar, Srinagar, between 8:00 AM and 11:30 AM on working days for routine OPD registration.

  2. Generate Digital OPD Token / ABHA Health ID

    Provide your Aadhaar card or Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) ID to generate a computerized OPD card for the designated clinical department.

  3. Consult Specialty Doctors in the Department Clinic

    Proceed to the allocated consultation room for clinical examination, symptom evaluation, and diagnostic laboratory test prescription.

  4. Undergo Diagnostic Investigations at In-House Labs

    Complete prescribed blood assays, digital X-rays, ultrasound, or CT scans at subsidized government hospital rates or free under Golden Card.

  5. Collect Free Medications from the In-House Pharmacy

    Collect prescribed generic medications, antibiotics, and surgical supplies from the Pradhan Mantri Jan Aushadhi Kendra or hospital dispensing counter.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of SMHS?

SMHS stands for Shri Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital, a premier government tertiary care teaching hospital located in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir.

Q2: Which medical college is SMHS Hospital affiliated with?

SMHS functions as the primary clinical teaching hospital for Government Medical College (GMC), Srinagar.

Q3: When was SMHS Hospital established?

SMHS Hospital was commissioned in 1948 and named after Maharaja Hari Singh, the last ruling monarch of Jammu and Kashmir.

Q4: Where is SMHS Hospital located?

The hospital is situated in the Karan Nagar area in the heart of Srinagar city.

Q5: What is the bed capacity of SMHS Hospital?

The hospital has an inpatient capacity of over 750 to 800 beds across its various medical and surgical specialty wards.

Q6: Are emergency services available 24/7 at SMHS?

Yes, SMHS operates round-the-clock casualty and level-1 trauma emergency services equipped with advanced intensive care units (ICUs).

Q7: Does SMHS provide free treatment under Ayushman Bharat?

Yes, patients holding the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY / SEHAT Golden Card receive 100% cashless diagnostics, surgeries, and treatment at SMHS.

Q8: What is SMHS Hospital's eye department renowned for?

Its Department of Ophthalmology is internationally recognized for its expertise in complex eye trauma, ocular reconstruction, and vitreoretinal surgery.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Shri Maharaja Hari Singh Hospital (SMHS) stands as an enduring pillar of compassion, clinical dedication, and medical excellence in Jammu and Kashmir. For nearly eight decades, its dedicated doctors, nurses, and faculty have delivered life-saving healthcare and trained future generations of medical professionals, establishing SMHS as a beloved and indispensable healthcare institution in the Kashmir Valley.
