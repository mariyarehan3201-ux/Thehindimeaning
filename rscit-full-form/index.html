RSCIT Full Form: Rajasthan IT Diploma Course

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of RSCIT is Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology. It is a foundational, highly recognized public computer literacy qualification launched by the Government of Rajasthan in collaboration with Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL) and Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota. Established to bridge the digital divide and equip citizens with practical computing skills, the RSCIT certificate is officially recognized as a mandatory basic computer qualification for numerous state government competitive recruitment examinations, administrative clerical posts, and public sector employment opportunities throughout Rajasthan.

The initiative behind the Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology emerged from the state recognition that digital literacy is indispensable for twenty-first-century governance. Modern administrative functions—including digital land record management through Apna Khata, public distribution systems, and citizen grievance redressal via Rajasthan Sampark—require grassroots personnel who navigate computers confidently. RSCIT ensures that young graduates possess functional digital capabilities before entering public service.

The curriculum of RSCIT is structured into comprehensive modules covering practical computing fundamentals, productivity software, cyber security awareness, and e-governance platforms. The table below details the primary curriculum modules taught across the three-month training program.

Assessment in the RSCIT program follows a rigorous two-tier evaluation model designed to verify both daily coursework participation and theoretical comprehension. Candidates must pass both components independently to earn their official physical certificate and digital credential stored in DigiLocker.

Earning the RSCIT credential empowers candidates to apply for competitive state recruitments with complete confidence. Beyond government employment, the practical skills gained in spreadsheet analysis, document design, and digital banking serve as enduring assets in private commerce and entrepreneurship.

How to Enroll and Pass the RSCIT Certification Examination

  1. Locate an Authorized RKCL Training Center

    Visit the official RKCL portal to identify a licensed IT Gyan Kendra (ITGK) computer training center situated near your residential area.

  2. Submit Admission Documents and Registration Fee

    Provide your secondary school mark sheet, passport-sized photographs, government identity card, and pay the subsidized state enrollment fee.

  3. Complete 132 Hours of Classroom and Lab Training

    Attend three months of structured coursework covering operating systems, office automation tools, digital financial literacy, and internet governance.

  4. Appear for and Clear the VMOU Final Examination

    Sit for the thirty-five question multiple-choice written exam administered by VMOU Kota, scoring at least twenty-eight marks out of seventy to earn your certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of RSCIT?

RSCIT stands for Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology, a state-recognized basic computing diploma.

Q2: Which university conducts the official RSCIT examination?

Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota conducts the official state evaluation and issues the recognized certificate.

Q3: What is the duration of the RSCIT course?

The course spans three months comprising one hundred thirty-two hours of classroom theory and practical hands-on computer lab training.

Q4: Is RSCIT mandatory for Rajasthan state government jobs?

Yes, qualifications like Patwari, Police Constable, LDC, Gram Sevak, and Junior Assistant require RSCIT or equivalent computer credentials.

Q5: What is the passing criteria for the RSCIT exam?

Candidates must score at least 12 marks out of 30 in internal assessments and at least 28 marks out of 70 in the VMOU final written test.

Q6: Is there negative marking in the RSCIT written exam?

No, the VMOU RSCIT written exam consists of thirty-five multiple-choice questions without any negative marking for incorrect answers.

Q7: Can working professionals complete RSCIT?

Yes, flexible morning and evening batch schedules at authorized ITGK centers accommodate students and working professionals alike.

Q8: How can candidates verify their official RSCIT result?

Results can be checked online via the official VMOU portal (rkcl.vmou.ac.in) using roll numbers or candidate name and date of birth.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

RSCIT, or Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology, represents the benchmark digital literacy qualification across Rajasthan. Administered jointly by RKCL and VMOU Kota, this course transforms novice computer users into digitally proficient candidates eligible for premier public sector posts. Investing three months in mastering the RSCIT curriculum provides practical life skills and unlocks vital career pathways.
