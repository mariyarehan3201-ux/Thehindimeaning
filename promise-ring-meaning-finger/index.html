How to Choose and Fit a Ring for Your Pointer Finger

A four-step guide to finding the perfect size and style for index finger wear.

  1. Measure Over the Prominent Knuckle

    Index finger knuckles are significantly wider than the base; ensure your ring sizer slides comfortably over the knuckle without forcing.

  2. Size Up for Wide Bands

    If choosing a wide cigar band wider than 6 mm, order a half size to a full size larger than your standard slim ring size for comfortable flexion.

  3. Opt for Smooth, Low-Profile Settings

    Select low-profile bezel or flat-top signet styles rather than high-prong settings to prevent snagging during daily manual hand tasks.

  4. Balance with Minimal Adjacent Rings

    To keep hands looking cohesive and elegant, pair an index statement ring with simple, subtle bands on the ring finger or pinky.
