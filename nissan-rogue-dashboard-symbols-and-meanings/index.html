Nissan Rogue Dashboard Symbols and Meanings

Meaning
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

In compact crossover utility vehicle maintenance, digital instrumentation, and driver safety (covering all generations of the popular Nissan Rogue), 'Nissan Rogue Dashboard Symbols' are illuminated instrument cluster icons, telltales, and digital Advanced Drive-Assist Display (ADAD) messages that inform drivers of vehicle operating status, Nissan Safety Shield 360 driver-assist active states, intelligent all-wheel-drive status, and mechanical powertrain alerts.

The Three-Tier Color Hierarchy of Nissan Warning Indicators

Nissan Rogue digital gauge clusters organize warning symbols into three standardized SAE color classifications: Red (Immediate Emergency Hazard), Yellow/Amber (System Advisory or Diagnostic Fault), and Green/Blue/White (Informational Active Status).

Red symbols indicate an acute, high-consequence mechanical crisis requiring immediate vehicle shutdown: Low Engine Oil Pressure (red oil can), High Engine Coolant Temperature (red thermometer), 12V Battery Charging System Failure, or Brake Hydraulic Malfunction. Yellow/Amber symbols indicate non-immediate diagnostic trouble codes (such as Check Engine, Vehicle Dynamic Control / VDC, or TPMS) that place electronic subsystems in failsafe modes while allowing the driver to reach a certified repair shop.

Decoding Signature Nissan Rogue Indicators: VDC, ProPILOT, and Malfunction Warnings

Modern Nissan Rogues feature specialized dashboard indicators unique to Nissan engineering. A prominent symbol is the 'VDC' light (Vehicle Dynamic Control, represented by a car with two squiggly tire tracks). When flashing, VDC is actively managing wheel slippage on wet or snowy roads. When solid, the stability system is disabled, often due to a faulty ABS wheel speed sensor.

Another signature indicator is the 'ProPILOT Assist' graphic (a blue/green steering wheel between two road lines), confirming active highway semi-autonomous steering and adaptive distance cruising. Drivers must also heed the amber 'Front Radar Unavailable' warning (two cars colliding with a star burst), which illuminates when the front grille radar sensor is blinded by highway snow, ice, or dried mud.

How to Diagnose and Reset Nissan Rogue Dashboard Warning Lights

Standard diagnostic sequence for Nissan Rogue owners.

  1. Read Plain-Text Messages on the Advanced Drive-Assist Display

    Use the steering wheel left-hand keypad to scroll to the 'Warnings' screen on the instrument cluster to read exact plain-text descriptions.

  2. Connect an OBD2 Diagnostic Scanner Under the Steering Column

    Plug an OBD2 scanner into the 16-pin diagnostic port located under the lower left dashboard to read active trouble codes from the ECM and BCM.

  3. Inspect and Reseal the Fuel Filler Cap

    If a Check Engine light appears without engine hesitation, turn the gas cap clockwise until it clicks firmly to clear evaporative fuel vapor codes.

  4. Inflate Tires and Utilize Nissan's 'Easy-Fill Tire Alert'

    When airing up tires, the vehicle's hazard lights flash and the horn honks once when you reach the exact factory door placard pressure.

  5. Clean Snow and Mud from the Front Bumper Radar Sensor

    If Automatic Emergency Braking or ProPILOT warnings appear, wipe road grime away from the transparent plastic Nissan emblem on the front grille.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does the car with wavy lines mean on a Nissan Rogue?

It is the Vehicle Dynamic Control (VDC) light; it flashes when managing traction in rain or snow and stays solid if stability control is disabled.

Q2: What does the two cars crashing symbol mean on a Nissan?

It is the Automatic Emergency Braking / Forward Collision Warning indicator; yellow means the front radar is blocked by snow, mud, or rain.

Q3: Can I drive my Nissan Rogue with the Check Engine light on?

If the amber light is solid, you can drive safely to a repair shop; if it is flashing, pull over immediately because an active misfire can ruin the catalytic converter.

Q4: What does 'PS' mean on a Nissan dashboard?

PS stands for Power Steering; a yellow or red PS light indicates an electric power steering motor failure, making steering significantly harder.

Q5: What is the 'Easy-Fill Tire Alert' on a Nissan Rogue?

When you put air in the tires with the car on, the exterior horn automatically honks once when the tire reaches the exact correct factory PSI.

Q6: What does the red key symbol mean on a Nissan Rogue?

It indicates a key fob communication issue, usually meaning the key fob's internal CR2032 battery is dying or the key is not detected inside the cabin.

Q7: What does the red oil can light mean?

It indicates dangerously low engine oil pressure; shut down the engine immediately to prevent catastrophic engine seizure and bearing failure.
