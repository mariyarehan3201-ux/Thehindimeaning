How Much Is a 2025 Toyota Sequoia?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 14, 2026

Understanding how much a 2025 Toyota Sequoia costs requires exploring its six distinct trim levels, standard hybrid powertrain, and available luxury packages. As Toyota flagship three-row full-size SUV built on the rugged TNGA-F truck platform, every 2025 Sequoia comes standard with the muscular i-FORCE MAX twin-turbo 3.4-liter V6 hybrid powertrain producing 437 horsepower and 583 pound-feet of torque. Depending on whether you choose the entry SR5 or the ultra-luxurious Capstone flagship, base MSRPs range from approximately $62,175 to over $83,610 before destination fees and dealer accessories.

2025 Toyota Sequoia Trim Levels and MSRP Breakdown

For the 2025 model year, Toyota offers the Sequoia in six primary trims: SR5, Limited, Platinum, TRD Pro, the opulent Capstone, and the newly introduced 1794 Edition that brings authentic Western-themed luxury. The base SR5 starts at an MSRP of $62,175 with rear-wheel drive, featuring standard Toyota Safety Sense 2.5, an eight-inch touchscreen, three-zone automatic climate control, and a panoramic view monitor. Stepping up to four-wheel drive adds roughly $3,000 across trims where it is not already standard.

The mid-tier Limited trim, starting around $68,575, represents the sweet spot for many family buyers by adding a massive fourteen-inch multimedia touchscreen, heated and ventilated SofTex-trimmed front seats, hands-free power liftgate, and twenty-inch alloy wheels. Higher trims push prices into luxury territory, with the TRD Pro tailored for off-roading starting at $80,045 and the top-tier Capstone topping $83,610 with premium semi-aniline leather and acoustic glass.

Powertrain Performance, Towing Specs, and Fuel Economy

Every 2025 Toyota Sequoia shares the identical i-FORCE MAX hybrid powertrain, pairing a twin-turbocharged 3.4-liter V6 engine with a motor-generator sandwiched between the engine and a ten-speed automatic transmission. Generating 437 horsepower and 583 pound-feet of torque at just 2,400 RPM, this powertrain gives the full-size SUV robust acceleration and exceptional heavy-hauling capability. Maximum towing capacity peaks at an impressive 9,520 pounds on properly configured SR5 models.

Fuel economy is a notable advantage over V8-powered domestic competitors like the Chevrolet Tahoe and Ford Expedition. Rear-wheel-drive Sequoia models earn an EPA-estimated 21 miles per gallon in the city and 24 mpg on the highway (22 mpg combined), while four-wheel-drive variants achieve 19 mpg city and 22 mpg highway (20 mpg combined). While these ratings represent substantial gains over older V8 generations, real-world towing fuel efficiency remains heavily dependent on trailer aerodynamics and load weight.

Total Ownership Costs, Options, and Dealer Pricing Dynamics

When calculating the true out-the-door price of a 2025 Sequoia, buyers must account for the mandatory $1,850 manufacturer destination freight charge, state sales tax, registration fees, and optional equipment packages. Popular add-ons include power-extending tow mirrors ($290), rear air suspension with load-leveling ($1,045), and specialized TRD off-road packages on SR5 and Limited models ($3,000 to $4,500).

Unlike recent pandemic-era supply constraints that saw widespread dealer markups, 2025 Sequoia inventory has normalized at dealerships nationwide. Buyers can typically secure models at MSRP, with modest discounts available on SR5 and Limited trims. However, high-demand variants like the TRD Pro in signature exterior colors (such as Mudbath) and the Capstone continue to command firm sticker prices due to tight factory allocation quotas.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the starting price of a 2025 Toyota Sequoia?

The base 2025 Toyota Sequoia SR5 starts at an MSRP of $62,175 for rear-wheel drive, plus an $1,850 destination charge.

Q2: How much is a fully loaded 2025 Toyota Sequoia Capstone?

A fully loaded 2025 Sequoia Capstone with 4WD, load-leveling air suspension, and premium accessories reaches approximately $88,000 to $90,000 out the door.

Q3: Does the 2025 Sequoia have a V8 engine?

No, all 2025 Sequoia models feature the i-FORCE MAX twin-turbo 3.4-liter V6 hybrid powertrain producing 437 horsepower.

Q4: Is the third row comfortable in the 2025 Sequoia?

The third row slides forward and backward by six inches, but the hybrid battery pack beneath the floor limits footwell depth for taller adults.

Q5: What is new on the 2025 Toyota Sequoia?

The 2025 model introduces the Western-inspired 1794 Edition trim, massing seats on high trims, and updated camera towing aids.

Q6: How much can the 2025 Sequoia tow?

When properly equipped, the 2025 Sequoia has a maximum towing capacity of up to 9,520 pounds on the SR5 RWD model.

Q7: Does the 2025 Toyota Sequoia require premium gas?

Toyota recommends 87-octane regular unleaded fuel for the Sequoia i-FORCE MAX hybrid, helping keep everyday running costs reasonable.

Q8: How does Sequoia pricing compare to the Chevy Tahoe?

The Sequoia has a slightly higher base price because the hybrid powertrain is standard, whereas Tahoe base trims feature non-hybrid naturally aspirated V8s.
