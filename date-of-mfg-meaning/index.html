Date of MFG Meaning

Meaning
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

In product packaging, supply chain logistics, consumer goods manufacturing, and regulatory compliance (governed by the FDA, ISO, and international consumer protection agencies), 'MFG Date' stands for 'Manufacturing Date' (or Date of Manufacture)—the exact calendar date on which a commercial product, pharmaceutical drug, food item, cosmetic, battery, or industrial chemical was synthesized, formulated, packaged, or produced at the factory.

How to Calculate Product Expiration from an MFG Date

Step-by-step consumer guide for verifying product freshness.

  1. Locate the Date Label and Check for 'MFG' Versus 'EXP'

    Examine the bottom of the bottle, crimped tube end, or outer carton; confirm whether the letters read 'MFG' (manufactured) or 'EXP' (expired).

  2. Search Packaging for the Period After Opening (PAO) Symbol

    Look for an illustration of an open cosmetic jar containing a number and 'M' (e.g., 6M, 12M, 24M), indicating months of safe use after opening.

  3. Consult Manufacturer General Shelf-Life Guidelines

    Unopened skincare typically lasts 36 months from MFG date; vitamins and supplements last 24 months; vehicle tires last 6 years from DOT date.

  4. Write the Opening Date on the Bottle with a Permanent Marker

    Whenever opening a new facial cream, sunscreen, or chemical fluid, write the current month and year on the label to track the PAO timeline.

  5. Inspect Product Texture, Scent, and Color Before Use

    Regardless of printed dates, discard products immediately if emulsions separate, liquid turns rancid or sour, or color shifts noticeably.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the primary significance of date of mfg meaning?

Examining the deeper technical mechanisms and historical evolution of date of mfg meaning reveals critical structural and operational nuances. MFG date stands for Manufacturing Date, indicating the exact day, month, and year the item was produced and packaged at the factory.

Q2: Does MFG mean the product is expired?

No. MFG is the date the product was made, not the expiration date. Most products are good for 2 to 3 years after the MFG date.

Q3: Why do Korean and Asian cosmetics print MFG dates?

In South Korea and many Asian countries, regulations mandate printing the production date (MFG or 제 조) rather than an arbitrary expiration date.

Q4: How do I know when a product expires if it only has an MFG date?

Check the packaging for a standard shelf-life statement (e.g., 'Valid for 3 years from MFG date') or look for the open jar PAO symbol (e.g., 12M).

Q5: What does the open jar symbol with '12M' mean?

It represents the Period After Opening (PAO); the product is safe and effective for 12 months after you break the seal and first open it.

Q6: How do you read a tire manufacturing date?

Look for the 4-digit DOT number on the sidewall: the first two digits are the week and the last two are the year (e.g., 3524 means the 35th week of 2024).

Q7: Can I use vitamins past their manufacturing date?

Yes. Vitamins generally have a 2-year shelf life from the MFG date; after that, vitamins are safe but gradually lose their potency.
