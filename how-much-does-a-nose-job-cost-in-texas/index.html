How Much Does a Nose Job Cost in Texas?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how much does a nose job cost in Texas requires examining surgical complexity, whether the procedure is cosmetic or functional, and regional pricing across Dallas, Houston, Austin, and San Antonio. On average, a primary cosmetic rhinoplasty in Texas costs between $7,500 and $14,000. Complex secondary revision rhinoplasty ranges from $12,000 to $22,000+, while functional septoplasty for breathing obstruction may be partially covered by health insurance.

Primary Cosmetic Rhinoplasty vs. Revision Rhinoplasty in Texas

Rhinoplasty is widely considered one of the most technically challenging and artistically demanding operations in all of plastic surgery. For a primary cosmetic rhinoplasty—performed on a patient who has never undergone nasal surgery before—costs typically range from $7,500 to $14,000. This procedure involves reshaping the osteocartilaginous nasal framework, reducing dorsal humps, narrowing wide nasal bones, and refining bulbous nasal tips.

Revision rhinoplasty—performed to correct unsatisfactory aesthetic outcomes or compromised breathing from a prior surgery—is substantially more complex. Revision surgery involves navigating dense internal scar tissue, warped cartilage, and deficient structural support, often requiring donor cartilage harvesting from the ear (conchal graft) or rib (costal graft). Because revision surgery can take 3.5 to 5 hours, specialist revision fees in Texas range from $12,000 to $22,000+.

Regional Pricing Across Dallas, Houston, Austin, and San Antonio

Geographic location within the state creates noticeable pricing variation. Dallas-Fort Worth is renowned as a global epicenter for rhinoplasty, hosting the world-famous annual Dallas Rhinoplasty Symposium. Top facial plastic surgeons in Dallas, Plano, and Highland Park typically charge between $8,500 and $15,000 for primary procedures, reflecting their international reputation.

Houston, with its world-class Texas Medical Center hub, averages $7,500 to $13,500, with top practices in the Galleria and River Oaks charging premium rates. Austin averages $8,000 to $14,000, driven by rapid population growth and high consumer demand. San Antonio and regional medical centers in Central/West Texas offer the most economical pricing in the state, averaging $6,500 to $10,500 for quality primary rhinoplasty.

Surgeon Credentials, Functional Insurance Splits, and Anesthesia

Choosing between a facial plastic surgeon (ENT with specialized facial cosmetic fellowship) and a general plastic surgeon is a primary decision for patients. Facial plastic surgeons certified by the ABFPRS dedicate their entire professional careers to the head, face, and neck, making them uniquely equipped to balance external cosmetic aesthetics with internal nasal airway aerodynamics.

If you suffer from chronic nasal airway obstruction caused by a deviated septum, enlarged inferior turbinates, or collapsed nasal valves, health insurance can significantly offset procedural expenses. In a combined 'septorhinoplasty,' health insurance covers the hospital, anesthesia, and surgeon fees for the functional medical airway correction ($3,000 to $6,000 value), leaving the patient responsible only for the cosmetic nasal reshaping balance ($4,500 to $8,000).

How to Choose a Rhinoplasty Surgeon in Texas

Follow these five clinical steps to select the best rhinoplasty surgeon in Texas and plan your procedure.

  1. Verify Dual Board Certification

    Look for surgeons certified by the American Board of Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery (ABFPRS) or American Board of Plastic Surgery (ABPS).

  2. Examine Extensive Before-and-After Galleries

    Scrutinize photographic portfolios of patients with similar ethnic nasal anatomy, looking for natural, un-pinched tip results.

  3. Participate in 3D Computer Morphing Simulation

    Ensure your surgeon utilizes Vectra 3D imaging during your consultation to align visual expectations regarding bridge height and tip projection.

  4. Get Evaluated for Internal Airway Obstruction

    Have the surgeon inspect your internal nasal valve and septum with an endoscope to explore insurance coverage for functional repair.

  5. Confirm Accredited Ambulatory Surgical Center Use

    Verify that the surgery will take place in an AAAASF or Joint Commission-accredited surgical center with a board-certified MD anesthesiologist.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the average cost of a nose job in Texas?

A primary cosmetic rhinoplasty in Texas typically costs between $7,500 and $14,000 in total fees.

Q2: Can health insurance cover a rhinoplasty in Texas?

Insurance does not cover cosmetic reshaping, but it frequently covers functional repairs like septoplasty or turbinate reduction to improve breathing.

Q3: Why is Dallas, Texas famous for rhinoplasty?

Dallas is home to the world-renowned Dallas Rhinoplasty Meeting and boasts some of the most published, innovative facial plastic surgeons in the world.

Q4: How long does rhinoplasty swelling take to completely disappear?

Roughly 80% of swelling resolves in 6 to 8 weeks, but subtle micro-swelling in the nasal tip takes a full 12 to 18 months to completely dissipate.

Q5: What is the difference between open and closed rhinoplasty?

Open rhinoplasty uses a tiny incision across the columella for maximum visibility; closed rhinoplasty places all incisions entirely inside the nostrils.

Q6: How much does revision rhinoplasty cost in Texas?

Revision rhinoplasty typically costs between $12,000 and $22,000+ due to scar tissue complexity and the frequent need for cartilage grafting.

Q7: Does a nose job hurt during recovery?

Most patients report pressure and nasal congestion rather than acute pain; prescribed pain medication easily manages post-op discomfort for 3-5 days.

Q8: What is a liquid nose job and how much does it cost?

A liquid nose job uses hyaluronic acid dermal fillers to camouflage humps or asymmetries, costing $900 to $1,800 and lasting 9 to 18 months.
