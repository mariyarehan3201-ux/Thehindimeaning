Shook and Fletcher Asbestos Trust Guide

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 23, 2026

The Shook & Fletcher Asbestos Settlement Trust is a court-established bankruptcy restitution fund created to provide financial compensation to individuals diagnosed with asbestos-related diseases resulting from Shook & Fletcher insulation products. Established following the company's Chapter 11 bankruptcy reorganization, the trust manages dedicated assets to pay qualifying claims under established Trust Distribution Procedures (TDP). Claimants and their families can recover compensation through an administrative process without going to trial.

Corporate History and the Creation of the Settlement Trust

Founded in 1901 in Birmingham, Alabama, Shook & Fletcher Insulation Company grew into one of the Southeast's major distributors and installers of commercial and industrial thermal insulation. For decades, the company contracted for major industrial projects, distributing high-temperature pipe lagging, asbestos block insulation, insulating cements, and refractory products across power generation plants, paper mills, textile operations, and steel foundries.

As the devastating health consequences of asbestos exposure materialized in the late twentieth century, Shook & Fletcher was named as a co-defendant in tens of thousands of personal injury and wrongful death lawsuits. Overwhelmed by massive tort liabilities, the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2002. In 2010, the US Bankruptcy Court confirmed the reorganization plan, establishing the Shook & Fletcher Asbestos Settlement Trust to resolve all current and future asbestos liabilities.

Trust Distribution Procedures, Disease Levels, and Payout Percentages

The Shook & Fletcher Trust operates under Trust Distribution Procedures (TDP) that categorize claims into distinct disease levels based on severity. The highest scheduled value is reserved for malignant mesothelioma (Disease Level VIII), followed by lung cancer, other asbestos-related cancers, and severe disabling asbestosis. Each disease level is assigned a scheduled baseline dollar value.

Because the total projected future liabilities of an asbestos trust frequently exceed available cash reserves, trusts establish a Payment Percentage to ensure equitable funds remain for future claimants. Claimants receive a percentage of their approved scheduled value. The trust trustees periodically conduct financial modeling reviews to adjust the payment percentage based on investment asset growth and incoming claim volumes.

How to File a Claim with the Shook & Fletcher Asbestos Trust

Step-by-step procedure for gathering evidence and submitting an administrative claim to the trust.

  1. Confirm Medical Eligibility

    Obtain official medical pathology records, tissue biopsies, and physician narrative reports confirming a qualifying asbestos disease diagnosis.

  2. Document Shook & Fletcher Site Exposure

    Assemble employment records, union dispatch tickets, and site affidavits verifying that you worked at a job site supplied by Shook & Fletcher.

  3. Select Review Pathway

    Choose between Expedited Review (for rapid payment of fixed scheduled value) or Individual Review (for complex claims seeking higher valuations).

  4. Submit Complete Claim Packet

    Lodge the electronic claim form, medical evidence, exposure affidavits, and required documentation through the trust's administrative portal.

  5. Execute Release and Receive Payout

    Upon claim approval, review the determination letter, sign the standard liability release agreement, and receive the calculated payment percentage check.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the Shook & Fletcher Asbestos Settlement Trust?

It is a court-supervised trust fund established in 2010 to pay asbestos personal injury claims stemming from Shook & Fletcher insulation products.

Q2: Where did Shook & Fletcher operate historically?

Headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama, the company primarily supplied and installed insulation across power plants and mills throughout the American Southeast.

Q3: What is an asbestos trust payment percentage?

A payment percentage is the adjusted fraction of a claim's scheduled value paid out, ensuring sufficient money remains for future claimants.

Q4: What is the difference between expedited and individual review?

Expedited review offers fast payment of fixed baseline values, while individual review allows claimants to present unique facts for higher compensation.

Q5: Do I need to go to court to receive money from the trust?

No, asbestos trust claims are resolved entirely through out-of-court administrative filings, with no court trials or depositions required.

Q6: Can surviving family members file a claim with the trust?

Yes, surviving spouses or legal estate representatives can file wrongful death claims on behalf of deceased loved ones.

Q7: Can I file claims against other asbestos trusts at the same time?

Yes, workers were usually exposed to multiple asbestos brands, allowing them to file simultaneous claims with multiple bankruptcy trusts.

Q8: How long does it take to get paid by the Shook & Fletcher Trust?

Once a complete and un-deficient claim packet is submitted under expedited review, determinations and payments typically process within three to six months.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Shook &amp; Fletcher Asbestos Settlement Trust provides a vital mechanism for workers injured by historical industrial insulation distribution across the American South. By utilizing streamlined administrative claims procedures under standardized TDP schedules, claimants avoid lengthy litigation while securing financial compensation. Individuals diagnosed with asbestos-related illnesses who worked at industrial job sites where Shook &amp; Fletcher supplied materials should consult an attorney to file timely claims.
