What Does 1067 Mean?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

The number 1067 carries distinct technical, operational, and cultural meanings depending on the context in which it appears. In public safety emergency communications and commercial two-way radio networks, 10-67 represents a specialized police ten-code, traditionally signaling "Person Down," "Clear for Net Message," or "Traffic Signal Down." Outside of first-responder communications, 1067 is widely examined in digital numerology and angel number literature as a sequence symbolizing practical persistence, self-reliance, and intellectual progress.

The Operational History of Police 10-Codes and 10-67

Public safety ten-codes originated in the late 1930s under the pioneering direction of Charles Hopper, communications director for the Illinois State Police. During the infancy of two-way radio technology, voice channels were plagued by severe static, limited battery output, and frequent signal fading. Because early tube-based transmitters required several tenths of a second to energize before transmitting voice audio, the introductory word "Ten" was standardized to ensure that the vital operational number following it was transmitted cleanly across the airwaves.

Under Association of Public-Safety Communications Officials (APCO) historical standards, 10-codes streamlined emergency radio traffic by conveying complex tactical situations in two rapid syllables. While universal codes like 10-4 (Acknowledgment) and 10-20 (Location) are identical worldwide, secondary codes such as 10-67 vary across jurisdictions. In many regional police agencies, 10-67 is dispatched as "Person Down / Medical Assistance Needed," whereas in highway departments it signifies "Traffic Signal Light Malfunction," and in civil defense radio nets it signals "Clear for Net Message."

The Modern Transition to Plain Language Protocols

In recent years, the use of specialized 10-codes, including 10-67, has been substantially curtailed across modern emergency management agencies. Following the communication bottlenecks exposed during major disasters like the 9/11 attacks and Hurricane Katrina, the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) instituted the National Incident Management System (NIMS). NIMS mandates that all multi-agency emergency operations eliminate ambiguous ten-codes in favor of "Plain Language" or common English terminology.

Because code 10-67 meant "Person Down" to one police agency and "Traffic Light Out" to a neighboring county's fire department, deploying mixed mutual-aid responders during large-scale natural disasters created hazardous tactical miscommunications. Today, dispatchers simply broadcast: "Medic 4, respond to an unresponsive subject" rather than broadcasting numerical codes. Nevertheless, 10-codes remain deeply embedded in veteran police culture, classic television dramas, and private security protocols.

How to Understand and Decipher Radio Ten-Codes

Follow these practical steps to interpret police ten-codes accurately when listening to public safety radio communications.

  1. Identify the Regional Agency Transmitting

    Determine whether the broadcast originates from municipal police, county sheriff, state highway patrol, or emergency medical services.

  2. Consult the Local Agency 10-Code Standard Sheet

    Refer to the specific local public safety agency's published ten-code list to confirm whether 10-67 means 'Person Down' or 'Signal Down.'

  3. Listen for Contextual Follow-Up Voice Data

    Pay attention to subsequent dispatch instructions regarding medical ambulances, paramedic staging, or public works road crews.

  4. Recognize Modern Plain Language Terminology

    Notice whether responders switch from ten-codes to clear plain English speech during complex multi-jurisdictional incidents.

  5. Avoid Transmitting Over Open Emergency Frequencies

    Remember that public safety radio frequencies are strictly regulated by the FCC and reserved solely for licensed emergency personnel.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does 10-67 mean on a police radio?

In many police departments, 10-67 signifies 'Person Down' requiring medical assistance, or 'Traffic Signal Out' in highway patrol agencies.

Q2: Why do police use 10-codes instead of plain English?

Ten-codes were created in the 1930s to communicate rapidly across static-filled radio channels and prevent eavesdropping citizens from panicking.

Q3: Are 10-codes the same in every city?

No. While basic codes like 10-4 are universal, secondary codes like 10-67 vary widely between different cities, counties, and states.

Q4: Why is FEMA phasing out police 10-codes?

FEMA mandates plain English under NIMS because differing 10-codes caused dangerous confusion during multi-agency disaster responses.

Q5: What does 10-4 mean?

10-4 is the universally recognized ten-code meaning 'Message Received,' 'Understood,' or 'Acknowledgment.'

Q6: What does 10-20 mean?

10-20 stands for 'What is your location?' or specifies the current physical location of a police officer or emergency unit.

Q7: What is the meaning of 1067 in angel numbers?

In numerology, 1067 symbolizes personal persistence, practical responsibility, intellectual development, and balancing home life with work.

Q8: Can citizens use 10-codes on CB radios?

Yes, civilian CB radio operators frequently use standard 10-codes to communicate road conditions and channel availability.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Whether encountered as an urgent "Person Down" alert on a police scanner, a municipal traffic signal repair code, or a recurring numerical sequence, the meaning of 1067 is shaped entirely by its operational context. In emergency services, it reflects the evolving history of public safety telecommunications, while in daily life, it serves as an inspiring symbol of persistence, balance, and focused determination.
