RSMML Full Form: Rajasthan Mines & Minerals Limited

Full Form

Understanding Rajasthan State Mines and Minerals Limited (RSMML)

Rajasthan State Mines and Minerals Limited (RSMML) is one of the most commercially profitable and operationally dynamic state public sector undertakings in India. Established to harness the abundant geological mineral wealth of the desert state of Rajasthan, RSMML plays an indispensable role in supporting national food security and heavy industrial manufacturing. Headquartered in Udaipur, Rajasthan, the company specializes in open-cast mechanized mining and mineral beneficiation, operating some of the largest non-metallic mineral deposits in Asia.

RSMML holds a virtual domestic monopoly in the supply of indigenous rock phosphate, supplying over 90% of India domestic phosphate ore required to manufacture phosphatic agricultural fertilizers (such as DAP and SSP). Additionally, the corporation is a market leader in extracting high-purity natural gypsum utilized in cement manufacturing and agricultural land reclamation, high-grade steel-melting shop (SMS) limestone for the metallurgical steel sector, and lignite coal for thermal power stations.

Four Core Strategic Mineral Divisions of RSMML

RSMML structures its vast mining operations into four specialized, autonomous Strategic Business Units (SBUs): Phosphate, Gypsum, Limestone, and Lignite. Each business unit manages dedicated open-cast mine leases, mineral processing plants, and railway siding freight logistics.

The Phosphate SBU operates the world-renowned Jhamarkotra Rock Phosphate Mine near Udaipur. Jhamarkotra houses India only industrial-scale rock phosphate beneficiation plant, which upgrades low-grade dolomitic phosphate ore (16-18% P2O5) into premium high-grade concentrate (over 31.5% P2O5) through flotation chemistry. The Gypsum SBU manages multiple mining leases across the Thar desert districts of Bikaner, Nagaur, and Barmer.

Pioneering Role in Green Energy and Environmental Management

Recognizing the ecological sensitivity of mining in arid landscapes, RSMML has diversified into renewable green energy generation. The corporation was one of the early public sector pioneers in harnessing wind energy, establishing utility-scale wind power farms in the windy deserts of Jaisalmer and Devgarh with an aggregate capacity exceeding 100 megawatts (MW). Power generated from these wind assets feeds the state electrical grid, offsetting the carbon footprint of heavy mechanized mining fleets.

Furthermore, RSMML conducts systematic environmental reclamation across its depleted mining pits. Overburden dumps are graded, stabilized with geo-textile netting, and afforested with indigenous drought-resistant tree species like Khejri (Prosopis cineraria) and Rohida, converting desolate mining voids into lush green ecological parks.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and Tribal Community Welfare

A significant portion of RSMML mining operations, particularly around Udaipur and southern Rajasthan, is located in tribal-dominated areas. Through its active CSR wing, RSMML invests heavily in community development, funding clean drinking water supply schemes, constructing secondary school buildings, operating mobile health clinics, and sponsoring vocational skill development programs for tribal youth.

Through the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) trusts, the corporation channels statutory mineral royalties into local infrastructure, ensuring that communities directly surrounding mining sites experience tangible advancements in education, healthcare, and economic prosperity.

How RSMML Beneficiates Low-Grade Rock Phosphate at Jhamarkotra

A step-by-step technological overview of the industrial flotation process used by RSMML to upgrade low-grade phosphate ore.

  1. Extract Ore via Open-Cast Drilling and Blasting

    Excavate dolomitic rock phosphate ore from open-cast benches using heavy hydraulic excavators and dump trucks.

  2. Primary and Secondary Crushing

    Feed bulk ore into jaw crushers and cone crushers to reduce boulders into uniform gravel size under 12 millimeters.

  3. Wet Ball Mill Grinding and Classification

    Grind crushed ore with water in rotating ball mills, using hydrocyclones to achieve a fine slurry with 80% passing 75 microns.

  4. Differential Froth Flotation Separation

    Introduce specialized chemical collectors and frothers into flotation cells to float and discard dolomitic carbonate impurities.

  5. Thickening, Filtration, and Concentrate Drying

    Dewater the upgraded phosphate concentrate slurry through rotary vacuum disc filters, yielding high-grade 31.5% P2O5 fertilizer powder.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of RSMML?

The full form of RSMML is Rajasthan State Mines and Minerals Limited, a public sector mining enterprise of Rajasthan.

Q2: Where is the corporate headquarters of RSMML located?

The corporate headquarters of RSMML is located in the historic city of Udaipur, Rajasthan.

Q3: What is RSMML most famous mining deposit?

The Jhamarkotra Rock Phosphate Mine near Udaipur, which contains India largest and richest indigenous phosphate deposit.

Q4: What are the four main mineral divisions of RSMML?

The four Strategic Business Units (SBUs) are Phosphate, Gypsum, Limestone, and Lignite.

Q5: Why is RSMML rock phosphate vital for Indian agriculture?

Rock phosphate is the essential raw material for producing Single Super Phosphate (SSP) and DAP agricultural chemical fertilizers.

Q6: Does RSMML generate renewable clean energy?

Yes, RSMML has developed over 100 MW of utility-scale wind power farms in Jaisalmer and Devgarh.

Q7: Where does RSMML mine high-purity SMS limestone?

RSMML operates world-class SMS-grade limestone quarries at Sanu in Jaisalmer district, supplying major Indian steel manufacturers.
