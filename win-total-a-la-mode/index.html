Architecture, Heritage, and Core Capabilities of TOTAL Software

The appraisal software industry has undergone significant technological evolution over the past three decades, shifting from manual physical forms and DOS-based typing utilities to cloud-connected desktop databases. At the forefront of this evolution was WinTOTAL, which became the foundational technology upon which modern residential real estate appraisal practices were standardized. As computing platforms modernized, a la mode redesigned the entire underlying engine to create TOTAL, a high-performance 64-bit multi-threaded software suite specifically architected to handle complex residential appraisal assignments while preserving the speed and keyboard shortcuts appraisers had mastered over decades.

Modern TOTAL software functions as a comprehensive enterprise valuation hub. Within its central desktop interface, appraisers can manage order tracking, historical report archives, customized client address books, and detailed electronic workfiles. When property appraisers assess property rights or consider how much does a commercial appraisal cost relative to residential assignments, workflow efficiency and administrative speed dictate operational profitability. TOTAL supports all standard GSE forms—including Fannie Mae Form 1004, Freddie Mac Form 70, condominium Form 1073, exterior-only drive-by forms, and small residential income property Form 1025—while maintaining a robust database capable of storing thousands of completed reports with full digital documentation attached.

Mobile Field Integration, Sketching, and Cloud Synchronization

One of the most transformative advances integrated into TOTAL is its seamless synchronization with mobile field inspection platforms. Historically, an appraiser would inspect a subject dwelling armed with a clipboard, paper measuring forms, physical disto laser meters, and a digital camera, returning to the office to manually transcribe scribbled measurements and match photos to form fields. With TOTAL for Mobile on iOS and Android tablets, the entire inspection workflow is digitized. While evaluating whether can a real estate agent do an appraisal versus a licensed certified valuer, rigorous measurement standards and Gross Living Area calculations remain exclusive hallmarks of appraisal practice.

TOTAL for Mobile connects directly to Bluetooth laser measuring devices, populating exterior wall lengths into the integrated sketching module in real time. As interior and exterior photographs are captured on the mobile device, they are automatically geo-tagged, organized, and mapped directly into the corresponding photo pages of the appraisal report. Back in the office, a single click synchronizes the cloud workfile to the desktop workstation. The appraiser receives a fully populated form complete with scaled vector sketches, calculated square footage figures, labeled photographs, and voice-to-text inspection notes, saving up to ninety minutes of manual administrative entry per assignment.

UAD Compliance, Automated Verification, and Secure Delivery

Following the 2008 housing market collapse, government-sponsored enterprises Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac established the Uniform Appraisal Dataset (UAD) to enforce standardized data formats across all single-family residential loan appraisals. UAD standardized abbreviations and specific grading criteria for condition (C1 through C6) and quality (Q1 through Q6) of construction. TOTAL incorporates a real-time, interactive UAD rules validation engine that monitors form inputs as the appraiser types. If an appraiser enters non-compliant date formats, omits required basement finish descriptions, or enters contradictory ratings, TOTAL flags the violation immediately, preventing submission rejections from lender appraisal management companies.

Furthermore, in today commercial environment where understanding whether can you put a lien on a business or real property hinges on sound legal and financial records, secure digital report transmission is vital. TOTAL integrates advanced digital certificate signing and secure PDF container creation. The software wraps the appraisal report in a MISMO-compliant XML data file with embedded digital signatures and SHA-256 encryption. This ensures the appraisal deliverable cannot be altered, intercepted, or tampered with as it transmits through Appraisal Management Company portals, Fannie Mae Uniform Collateral Data Portal (UCDP), or Freddie Mac Electronic Collateral Assessment (ECA) gateways.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the difference between WinTOTAL and TOTAL by a la mode?

WinTOTAL was the original 32-bit legacy appraisal software platform developed by a la mode in the 1990s and 2000s. TOTAL is its modern, fully re-architected successor built on a multi-threaded 64-bit platform, offering cloud synchronization, advanced mobile field tools, and faster processing while maintaining familiar workflows.

Q2: Does TOTAL by a la mode run natively on Mac operating systems?

No, TOTAL is designed specifically for Windows operating systems. Mac users who wish to run TOTAL on desktop or laptop computers typically do so by running Windows through virtualization software such as Parallels Desktop or Apple Boot Camp on Intel-based hardware.

Q3: How does TOTAL for Mobile sync data with the desktop application?

TOTAL for Mobile synchronizes data through a secure cloud connection called the TOTAL Cloud. Appraisers can upload completed field inspection data, sketches, notes, and photos from their iOS or Android mobile device, and download the data directly into their desktop workfile with a single click.

Q4: What is QuickSource in TOTAL by a la mode?

QuickSource is an integrated data verification and comparable analysis tool within TOTAL. It allows appraisers to import property data simultaneously from multiple sources, including MLS feeds and public tax records, highlighting differences and auto-populating comparable sales grids.

Q5: Does TOTAL software ensure compliance with Uniform Appraisal Dataset standards?

Yes, TOTAL incorporates real-time UAD formatting validation and an extensive Errors and Omissions checking engine. It alerts appraisers to formatting errors, missing required fields, and conflicting data before the report is packaged for delivery to lenders or appraisal management companies.

Q6: Can multiple appraisers in the same firm share reports and databases in TOTAL?

Yes, TOTAL supports network multi-user configurations. Multiple appraisers, trainees, and office staff can access a shared network database, allowing team members to collaborate on files, share comparable databases, and manage office order pipelines seamlessly.

Q7: How does TOTAL handle digital signatures and report encryption?

TOTAL utilizes secure PKI digital signature certificates. When an appraiser signs a report, the software seals the document with cryptographic hashing to prevent unauthorized editing, packaging the deliverable into an encrypted MISMO-compliant XML and PDF container.

Q8: What sketch programs are compatible with TOTAL by a la mode?

TOTAL comes with its own built-in sketching software, TOTAL Sketch, which calculates Gross Living Area automatically. It is also compatible with third-party industry sketch applications such as Apex Sketch, RapidSketch, and Phoenix Sketch through integrated software bridges.

TOTAL by a la mode continues to serve as the technological backbone of the modern residential real estate appraisal industry because it strikes an exceptional balance between legacy speed and forward-thinking innovation. By integrating robust desktop form processing with responsive mobile field inspection apps, sophisticated vector sketching engines, automated comparable sales validation, and rock-solid UAD compliance checking, TOTAL empowers real estate appraisers to deliver authoritative, compliant, and defensible valuation reports in less time. For independent valuation professionals and multi-appraiser firms seeking to enhance daily productivity while adhering strictly to USPAP standards, TOTAL remains an indispensable software investment.
