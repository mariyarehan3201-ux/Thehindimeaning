SCAQMD Rule 1403 and Cal/OSHA Regulatory Mandates

In the Los Angeles metropolitan basin, environmental air quality is strictly regulated by the South Coast Air Quality Management District (SCAQMD) under Rule 1403. Under this regional air regulation, property owners and contractors must have a comprehensive asbestos survey conducted by a Cal/OSHA Certified Asbestos Consultant (CAC) prior to any demolition or renovation activity. Rule 1403 establishes strict notification rules, requiring a formal online notification to SCAQMD at least ten working days prior to beginning any project that disturbs one hundred square feet or more of regulated material.

Furthermore, Cal/OSHA enforces Title 8 of the California Code of Regulations, Section 1529, which regulates contractor licensing and worker protection. Any commercial contractor removing more than one hundred square feet of surface material containing greater than 0.1 percent asbestos must be registered with the Cal/OSHA Asbestos Contractor Registration Organization (ACRO) and employ certified supervisors and technicians.

Containment Engineering and Wet Scraping Protocols

Because spray-applied acoustic ceiling texture is classified as highly friable, uncontained scraping creates dangerous airborne dust clouds that can linger in living spaces for days. Certified Los Angeles abatement contractors construct airtight containment chambers using two layers of six-mil flame-retardant poly sheeting across all walls and floors. Industrial negative air filtration units equipped with HEPA filters exhaust clean air outside, maintaining continuous negative pressure within the work envelope.

Technicians saturate the ceiling texture with surfactant-treated amended water using airless garden sprayers, allowing moisture to penetrate down to the drywall substrate. Workers scrape the saturated slurry using wide manual scraping blades attached directly to HEPA vacuum shrouds, immediately bagging the wet debris. How to Safely Remove Asbestos Acoustic Ceilings in Los Angeles

Follow these five certified regulatory steps to coordinate legal popcorn ceiling removal in Los Angeles County.

  1. Commission a Survey by a Cal/OSHA Certified Consultant

    Hire an independent Certified Asbestos Consultant (CAC) to collect bulk acoustic samples and prepare a certified survey report.

  2. Submit SCAQMD Rule 1403 10-Day Advance Notification

    Ensure the licensed abatement contractor submits the mandatory 10-day notification and fee to the South Coast Air Quality Management District.

  3. Construct Airtight Six-Mil Poly Containment Enclosure

    Seal all walls, flooring, doors, windows, and HVAC registers with two layers of six-mil plastic sheeting and run HEPA negative air scrubbers.

  4. Perform Continuous Wet Scraping and Lockdown Encapsulation

    Saturate the texture with amended water, gently scrape the wet slurry into disposal bags, and spray the bare ceiling with bridging lockdown primer.

  5. Obtain Third-Party Air Clearance and Hazardous Waste Manifest

    Have an independent technician run aggressive air clearance tests and secure the California hazardous waste manifest for disposal records.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Is popcorn ceiling removal regulated in Los Angeles?

Yes, SCAQMD Rule 1403 and Cal/OSHA strictly regulate popcorn ceiling removal, requiring certified surveys, notifications, and licensed contractors.

Q2: What is SCAQMD Rule 1403 in Southern California?

Rule 1403 is the South Coast AQMD regulation governing asbestos emissions during demolition and renovation in Los Angeles, Orange, and Riverside counties.

Q3: Can Los Angeles homeowners scrape their own popcorn ceilings?

Single-family owner-occupants have narrow exemptions, but DIY scraping of friable texture is exceptionally hazardous and strongly discouraged.

Q4: How much does asbestos ceiling removal cost in Los Angeles?

Costs typically range from $4 to $8 per square foot, with a full 1,500 square foot home averaging between $4,500 and $8,500.

Q5: Can I paint over an asbestos popcorn ceiling instead of removing it?

Yes, encapsulating intact acoustic texture with heavy latex paint or drywall overlay is an approved, economical management-in-place option.

Q6: How long does the 10-day SCAQMD notification take?

SCAQMD requires ten working days (excluding weekends and legal holidays) between filing the electronic notification and beginning physical work.

Q7: Where does asbestos waste from Los Angeles get disposed of?

Regulated waste must be hauled by certified hazardous waste transporters to licensed Class I or Class II disposal landfills under a DTSC manifest.

Q8: What air clearance test is required after ceiling removal in LA?

Independent industrial hygienists conduct aggressive air sampling, typically utilizing Phase Contrast Microscopy (PCM) or Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM).

Asbestos ceiling removal in Los Angeles demands strict adherence to regional environmental rules, advanced containment engineering, and licensed contracting oversight.
