Asbestos Removal Green Bay: Wisconsin Guide

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 23, 2026

Asbestos removal in Green Bay, Wisconsin is governed by strict environmental and workplace safety regulations enforced by the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (DNR) and the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS). Situated along the Fox River in Brown County, Green Bay's historic paper mills, maritime ports, and pre-1980 residential neighborhoods frequently require certified abatement before renovation or demolition.

The Wisconsin Regulatory Landscape: DNR & DHS Oversight

In Wisconsin, asbestos oversight is managed through coordinated state regulatory frameworks. The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (DNR) regulates environmental air emissions, project notifications, and waste disposal under Wisconsin Administrative Code Chapters NR 445 and NR 447 (Wisconsin's codification of the federal EPA NESHAP standard, 40 CFR Part 61 Subpart M).

Concurrently, the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS) oversees asbestos contractor licensing, worker training, and accreditation under Chapter DHS 159. Contractors performing abatement in Green Bay must submit an official Wisconsin DNR Asbestos Notification form at least ten working days prior to disturbing friable asbestos exceeding statutory reporting thresholds.

Common Asbestos Materials in Green Bay Architecture

Green Bay's industrial history as a major paper manufacturing and maritime shipping hub shaped the extensive use of asbestos building products. Along the Fox River industrial corridor, historic paper mills, food processing plants, and power generating stations utilized immense volumes of high-temperature thermal insulation. Boilermakers and pipefitters installed thick 85% magnesia block insulation and aircell pipe wrap on steam systems.

In residential areas—including Astor Historic District, Preble, and vintage neighborhoods around Lambeau Field—homes constructed between 1920 and 1975 frequently contain acoustic popcorn ceilings, 9x9-inch vinyl asbestos floor tiles, and black cutback mastic in basements. Exterior transite siding shingles and corrugated cement wallboards are also common on detached garages and dairy barns across Brown County.

Contractor Verification and Regional Waste Disposal

Property owners hiring an abatement contractor in Green Bay must confirm active credentials through the Wisconsin DHS online licensing portal. Legitimate contractors must hold an active Wisconsin Asbestos Company Certificate, employ certified supervisors on site at all times, and maintain comprehensive commercial general liability and dedicated asbestos pollution liability insurance.

Following removal, hazardous waste must be transported by a licensed carrier to a permitted municipal solid waste landfill approved by the Wisconsin DNR to accept special asbestos waste (such as the Brown County South Landfill). Waste must be thoroughly wetted, double-bagged inside certified 6-mil poly bags with OSHA warning labels, and accompanied by a completed Wisconsin DNR Waste Shipment Record.

How to Plan an Asbestos Abatement Project in Green Bay, Wisconsin

  1. Schedule a Certified DHS Building Survey

    Hire an independent Wisconsin DHS-certified asbestos inspector to survey the property and extract bulk samples using wet methods.

  2. Obtain Bids from Licensed Wisconsin Contractors

    Solicit detailed proposals from remediation firms holding active Wisconsin Asbestos Company Certificates issued by DHS.

  3. Submit 10-Day Notification to Wisconsin DNR

    Ensure the contractor submits the mandatory 10-day advance notification form to the Wisconsin DNR Northeast Region office.

  4. Erect Negative Pressure Containment

    The remediation crew seals work areas with 6-mil poly plastic sheeting, HEPA negative air scrubbers, and a multi-stage decon unit.

  5. Pass Independent Clearance and Manifest Waste

    Receive clean third-party air clearance certification before teardown, and transport double-bagged waste to a permitted Brown County landfill.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: Who regulates asbestos removal in Green Bay, Wisconsin?

The Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (DNR) regulates emissions under NR 447, while the Department of Health Services (DHS) oversees licensing.

Q2: When is state notification required in Green Bay?

A 10-working-day advance notice to the Wisconsin DNR is mandatory for projects disturbing &gt; 160 sq ft or 260 lin ft of friable asbestos.

Q3: Can homeowners remove asbestos themselves in Wisconsin?

Single-family residential owner-occupants have limited exemptions, but state health officials strongly recommend professional abatement.

Q4: Where is asbestos waste disposed of in Brown County?

Waste must be double-bagged in 6-mil poly bags, manifested, and transported to permitted landfills such as the Brown County South Landfill.

Q5: How much does asbestos removal cost in Green Bay?

Residential popcorn ceiling or pipe wrap removal typically costs between $1,500 and $4,500 depending on square footage and accessibility.

Q6: Why was asbestos used so heavily in Green Bay?

Green Bay's historic paper manufacturing mills and commercial maritime shipping required immense volumes of high-temperature steam insulation.

Q7: What is the final air clearance standard in Wisconsin?

Post-abatement reoccupancy clearance requires visual cleanliness and aggressive air sampling results below 0.01 fibers per cubic centimeter (f/cc).

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Asbestos removal in Green Bay requires strict compliance with Wisconsin DNR NR 447 regulations, certified contractor execution, and 10-day advance state notification. Whether updating a historic Victorian home in the Astor neighborhood or renovating an industrial facility along the Fox River, engaging licensed environmental abatement professionals guarantees safety, environmental protection, and independent air clearance verification.
