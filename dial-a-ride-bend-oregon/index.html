Dial a Ride Bend Oregon

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

Navigating Central Oregon without personal vehicle access can be challenging, particularly for seniors, individuals with disabilities, and residents living beyond standard fixed-route bus corridors. In Bend, Oregon, the Dial-A-Ride transit service—operated by Cascades East Transit (CET) under the Central Oregon Intergovernmental Council (COIC)—provides vital curb-to-curb shared-ride public transportation. Understanding service areas, eligibility requirements, booking windows, and fares ensures seamless travel to medical appointments, work, and community services.

What is Dial-A-Ride in Bend, Oregon and Who Qualifies?

Dial-A-Ride is an on-demand, demand-responsive public transit service operating throughout the City of Bend and surrounding Deschutes County service zones. Operated by Cascades East Transit, Dial-A-Ride functions under two distinct service tiers: ADA Paratransit service for individuals with qualifying mobility limitations, and General Public Dial-A-Ride for community members residing outside the reach of fixed-route bus lines.

Under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), federally mandated ADA Paratransit service is available to individuals whose physical, cognitive, or visual disabilities prevent them from independently boarding or riding standard fixed-route CET city buses. ADA-certified riders receive prioritized scheduling and can book trips originating and terminating anywhere within three-quarters of a mile of CET fixed bus routes. General public riders may also utilize Dial-A-Ride on a space-available basis.

Fares, Operating Hours, and Cascades East Transit Service Zones

Fares for Bend Dial-A-Ride are structured to remain highly affordable for community transit users. As of current CET fare schedules, a standard one-way Dial-A-Ride trip within the Bend urban service area costs between two dollars and fifty cents and three dollars and seventy-five cents per ride. Certified ADA paratransit riders pay two dollars and fifty cents per one-way trip, and eligible Personal Care Attendants (PCAs) travel free of charge when escorting a certified passenger.

Service operating hours mirror CET's fixed-route schedule to maintain complete ADA transit equivalence. In Bend, Dial-A-Ride vehicles operate Monday through Friday from approximately 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM, with Saturday service running from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Fares can be paid with exact cash onboard the vehicle, via pre-purchased multi-ride ticket punch cards, or through CET's modern digital mobile ticketing app (Umo Mobility).

How to Schedule Rides, Cancellation Policies, and Travel Tips

Booking a trip with Cascades East Transit Dial-A-Ride requires advance planning. The CET call center (541-385-8680) accepts reservations Monday through Friday between 7:00 AM and 4:00 PM. ADA-eligible passengers can schedule rides up to fourteen days in advance, while general public riders can book between one and three business days ahead. When calling, riders must provide exact pickup addresses, appointment drop-off times, and specify any mobility equipment (such as motorized wheelchairs, walkers, or service animals).

Because Dial-A-Ride is a shared-ride service, passengers must anticipate a thirty-minute pickup window. The bus may pick up or drop off other passengers along the route, so schedule your trip to arrive well before critical medical appointment times. CET enforces a strict cancellation policy: trips must be cancelled at least two hours before the scheduled pickup window; repeated 'no-shows' or late cancellations can result in temporary service suspensions under CET administrative rules.

How to Book a Dial-A-Ride Trip in Bend in 5 Steps

Follow these steps to register, reserve, and complete a ride with Cascades East Transit Dial-A-Ride.

  1. Complete ADA Paratransit Application (If Applicable)

    If you have a qualifying disability, submit the CET ADA paratransit certification form with physician verification to qualify for priority booking.

  2. Call CET Dispatch During Booking Hours

    Contact Cascades East Transit call center at 541-385-8680 between 7:00 AM and 4:00 PM Monday through Friday.

  3. Provide Trip Details and Mobility Needs

    Provide your exact pickup address, destination, desired arrival time, and mention if traveling with a wheelchair or personal care attendant.

  4. Be Ready During the 30-Minute Pickup Window

    Wait in a visible location near the primary door during your assigned 30-minute window; drivers wait five minutes before departing.

  5. Pay Exact Fare or Scan Mobile Pass

    Board the vehicle, pay exact cash fare (drivers carry no change), or scan your Umo digital pass on the onboard reader.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is Dial-A-Ride in Bend, Oregon?

Dial-A-Ride is a public curb-to-curb shared-ride transit service operated by Cascades East Transit (CET) for seniors, people with disabilities, and the general public.

Q2: How much does Dial-A-Ride cost in Bend?

A standard one-way trip within Bend costs two dollars and fifty cents for certified ADA riders and seniors, and three dollars and seventy-five cents for the general public.

Q3: Who is eligible for ADA Paratransit in Bend?

Individuals with physical, cognitive, or sensory disabilities that prevent them from independently using fixed-route bus services qualify for ADA paratransit certification.

Q4: How far in advance can you schedule a ride?

Certified ADA passengers can book rides up to fourteen days in advance, while general public riders can schedule one to three days prior to travel.

Q5: What is the phone number to book Dial-A-Ride in Bend?

You can schedule a ride by calling the Cascades East Transit dispatch reservation center at 541-385-8680 during weekday business hours.

Q6: Are Dial-A-Ride buses wheelchair accessible?

Yes, one hundred percent of CET Dial-A-Ride vehicles are fully ADA compliant, equipped with hydraulic wheelchair lifts or ramps and secure tie-down stations.

Q7: Can a Personal Care Attendant (PCA) ride for free?

Yes, a designated Personal Care Attendant accompanying a certified ADA passenger rides completely free of charge, provided they are registered when booking.

Q8: Does Dial-A-Ride operate on Sundays in Bend?

No, Dial-A-Ride does not operate regular service on Sundays; service runs Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Saturday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
