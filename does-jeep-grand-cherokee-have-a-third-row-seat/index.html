Does Jeep Grand Cherokee Have a Third Row Seat?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

The Jeep Grand Cherokee is universally celebrated for combining rugged, trail-rated off-road pedigree with the road manners and luxury of an executive crossover. When shopping for this iconic sport utility vehicle, growing families frequently inquire whether the Grand Cherokee includes a third-row seat. The answer depends on which model you select: the standard two-row Jeep Grand Cherokee seats five passengers, while the extended-wheelbase Jeep Grand Cherokee L comes factory-equipped with three rows of seating accommodating six or seven passengers.

Understanding the Lineup: Grand Cherokee Versus Grand Cherokee L

Throughout its first four generations, the Jeep Grand Cherokee was engineered exclusively as a two-row midsize SUV. However, to meet booming consumer demand for family-friendly luxury vehicles, Jeep introduced the extended Jeep Grand Cherokee L alongside the fifth-generation (WL) platform redesign.

The Grand Cherokee L is not an aftermarket package or a cramped jump-seat addition; it is an entirely distinct, extended-wheelbase vehicle. Measuring 204.9 inches in overall length with a 121.7-inch wheelbase (compared to 193.5 inches and 116.7 inches on the standard two-row model), the Grand Cherokee L was purpose-built from the chassis up to house a spacious third row with dedicated air vents, USB ports, and comfortable passenger seating.

Interior Space and Third-Row Comfort for Adults

A primary concern for SUV shoppers is whether a vehicle's third row can accommodate real adults or only small children. In the Jeep Grand Cherokee L, the third row delivers a generous 30.3 inches of legroom, 37.3 inches of headroom, and elevated stadium-style seat cushions that prevent knees from resting uncomfortably high. Average-sized adults can ride comfortably on extended road trips.

Ingress and egress are remarkably effortless thanks to Jeep's second-row 'tip-and-slide' seat mechanisms. With the pull of a single top-mounted lever, the second-row seats slide and tip forward smoothly, creating a wide entryway into the rear cabin. This slide function operates seamlessly even when an empty forward-facing child car seat is secured in the second row.

Cargo Space, Towing Muscle, and Trim Levels

The Grand Cherokee L preserves exceptional cargo utility even with all three rows occupied. It provides 17.2 cubic feet of cargo volume behind the third row, expanding to 46.9 cubic feet with the third row folded flat, and opening to a massive 84.6 cubic feet with both rear rows folded flat into the floor.

The three-row Grand Cherokee L is offered across a comprehensive trim hierarchy—from the accessible Laredo and Limited to the ultra-luxurious Overland, Summit, and Summit Reserve. Buyers can choose between the reliable 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 (293 hp, towing up to 6,200 lbs) or the muscular 5.7-liter HEMI V8 (357 hp, towing up to 7,200 lbs) equipped with legendary Quadra-Drive II 4WD systems.

How to Choose the Right Three-Row Jeep Grand Cherokee in 5 Steps

Follow these five steps to select the ideal Jeep Grand Cherokee L model and options for your family.

  1. Verify the 'L' Designation

    Ensure you are viewing the Grand Cherokee L; standard Grand Cherokees are exclusively two-row models.

  2. Choose Between 6 or 7 Passenger Seating

    Select standard captain's chairs for a center walkway or the optional second-row bench for maximum seven-passenger capacity.

  3. Assess Towing Requirements (V6 vs V8)

    If towing heavy boats or travel trailers over 6,200 pounds, search for an Overland or Summit trim equipped with the 5.7L HEMI V8.

  4. Test Drive Third-Row Access in Person

    Practice operating the second-row tip-and-slide mechanism and sit in the third row to evaluate comfort.

  5. Measure Garage Length Before Purchase

    Verify that your garage has at least 210 inches of clear length to park the 204.9-inch Grand Cherokee L comfortably.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can you get a third row on a regular Jeep Grand Cherokee?

No, the standard Grand Cherokee is strictly a two-row vehicle; you must buy the extended Jeep Grand Cherokee L for third-row seating.

Q2: How many seats does a Grand Cherokee L have?

The Grand Cherokee L has six seats with standard second-row captain's chairs, or seven seats with the optional second-row bench.

Q3: Does the Jeep Grand Cherokee 4xe have a third row?

No, the plug-in hybrid 4xe powertrain is available only on the two-row Grand Cherokee due to battery placement.

Q4: Do the third-row seats fold flat?

Yes, the 50/50 split third-row seats fold completely flat into the cargo floor with the push of a button or pull of a strap.

Q5: Is the Grand Cherokee L bigger than the Dodge Durango?

The Grand Cherokee L and Dodge Durango share similar platform architectures, but the Grand Cherokee L is slightly longer and features a more modern luxury interior.

Q6: Does the Grand Cherokee L third row have USB ports?

Yes, the third row includes dedicated USB-A and USB-C fast-charging ports, cup holders, and pillar-mounted air vents.

Q7: When did Jeep start making the Grand Cherokee L?

Jeep introduced the three-row Grand Cherokee L for the 2021 model year.

Q8: Can child car seats fit in the Grand Cherokee L third row?

Yes, the third row features complete LATCH lower anchors and top tethers to securely install child safety seats.
