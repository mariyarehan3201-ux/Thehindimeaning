Gas Mileage of a Jeep Grand Cherokee

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding the gas mileage of a Jeep Grand Cherokee is crucial for prospective buyers navigating the wide spectrum of powertrains available on this iconic midsize SUV. Renowned for legendary Quadra-Trac four-wheel-drive capability, luxurious interior craftsmanship, and robust towing ratings, the Grand Cherokee caters to diverse driving lifestyles. From the ubiquitous 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 and legendary 5.7-liter HEMI V8 to the cutting-edge 4xe plug-in hybrid (PHEV), fuel economy numbers vary drastically depending on your choice under the hood.

Powertrain Breakdown: Pentastar V6, HEMI V8, and 4xe Hybrid

The baseline engine across the modern WL generation Jeep Grand Cherokee is Stellantis' proven 3.6-liter Pentastar V6, paired with an 8-speed TorqueFlite automatic transmission. Producing 293 horsepower, the V6 is equipped with standard engine stop-start (ESS) technology that turns off combustion during red lights to curb idle consumption. In rear-wheel drive (4x2) models, the V6 achieves an EPA-rated 19 MPG city and 26 MPG highway, while four-wheel drive (4x4) variants achieve a nearly identical 19 MPG city and 26 MPG highway.

For drivers demanding maximum efficiency and electrified torque, the Grand Cherokee 4xe combines a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine with two electric motors and a 17.3 kWh lithium-ion battery pack. Generating a formidable 375 horsepower and 470 lb-ft of torque, the 4xe offers 25 miles of pure electric driving before transitioning into a conventional hybrid. In contrast, older models equipped with the thirsty 5.7-liter HEMI V8 delivered effortless V8 rumble and 7,200 pounds of towing, but penalized drivers with 14 MPG city and 22 MPG highway ratings.

Real-World Fuel Efficiency Drivers and 4xe Plug-In Dynamics

In daily real-world operation, achieving the EPA fuel economy ratings in a Jeep Grand Cherokee requires understanding the vehicle's aerodynamic and mechanical profile. Weighing between 4,200 and 5,300 pounds depending on luxury trim and battery equipment, the Grand Cherokee requires substantial energy to accelerate. Enthusiasts who install aggressive all-terrain tires, steel rock sliders, or heavy roof racks will notice an immediate 2 to 3 MPG drop on interstate highway routes.

For the Grand Cherokee 4xe, real-world efficiency depends entirely on charging discipline. Owners who plug the vehicle in nightly at home and commute within twenty-five miles round-trip rarely visit commercial gas stations, routinely achieving over 70 MPG equivalent. However, on long cross-country road trips where the battery remains uncharged, the 4xe operates as a heavy conventional hybrid, yielding approximately 22 to 24 MPG highway, comparable to the standard V6.

Strategies to Optimize Grand Cherokee Fuel Economy

Jeep engineers incorporated several electronic features into the Grand Cherokee to help drivers maximize efficiency. Activating the green Eco Mode button on the center console alters transmission shift mapping, commanding the 8-speed gearbox to upshift early and dampening aggressive throttle inputs. On models equipped with Quadra-Lift air suspension, the vehicle automatically lowers its ride height at highway speeds (Aero Mode), reducing underbody aerodynamic drag by several percent.

Maintaining proper tire pressure is equally essential. Factory recommendations specify between 33 and 36 PSI for standard 18-inch and 20-inch wheels. Under-inflated tires generate excessive rolling resistance that wastes fuel and prematurely wears expensive luxury SUV tires. In addition, disabling the Engine Stop-Start (ESS) system may smooth out bumper-to-bumper traffic crawls, but keeping it active conserves up to eight percent of fuel during dense urban gridlock.

How to Maximize Fuel Economy in a Jeep Grand Cherokee

Follow these five tactical vehicle settings and maintenance habits to achieve peak gas mileage in your Jeep Grand Cherokee.

  1. Keep Eco Mode and Stop-Start Engaged

    Ensure the factory Eco Mode is toggled on and avoid deactivating the Engine Stop-Start (ESS) button to prevent unnecessary idle fuel burn.

  2. Utilize Aero Mode on Air Suspension Models

    Allow the factory Quadra-Lift air suspension system to drop automatically into Aero Mode at highway speeds to streamline aerodynamic airflow.

  3. Plug in the 4xe Battery Daily

    If driving a 4xe plug-in hybrid, recharge the 17.3 kWh battery overnight using a Level 1 or Level 2 home charger to maximize zero-emission electric commuting.

  4. Monitor Tire Pressure Placard Ratings

    Check cold tire pressures every three weeks to ensure all four tires match the driver door sticker specification of 33 to 36 PSI.

  5. Maintain Steady Interstate Speeds

    Set adaptive cruise control to 65 to 70 MPH rather than 80 MPH on interstates, preventing aerodynamic drag from penalizing fuel economy.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the average gas mileage of a Jeep Grand Cherokee?

Standard V6 models achieve 19 MPG city and 26 MPG highway (21 to 22 MPG combined), while the 4xe plug-in hybrid is rated at 56 MPGe.

Q2: Does the Jeep Grand Cherokee require premium gas?

The 3.6L V6 runs on regular 87-octane; the 5.7L HEMI V8 recommends 89-octane mid-grade; the 2.0L Turbo 4xe recommends 91-octane premium.

Q3: How far can a Jeep Grand Cherokee 4xe drive on electric power alone?

The Grand Cherokee 4xe delivers an EPA-estimated twenty-five miles of all-electric driving range before the gas engine starts.

Q4: How big is the gas tank on a Jeep Grand Cherokee?

Standard gas Grand Cherokee models have a 23.0-gallon fuel tank, while the 4xe plug-in hybrid features a slightly smaller 19.0-gallon fuel tank.

Q5: What gas mileage does the three-row Grand Cherokee L get?

The three-row Grand Cherokee L V6 achieves 18 MPG city and 25 MPG highway, while the discontinued HEMI V8 model gets 14 MPG city and 22 MPG highway.

Q6: Does 4x4 decrease gas mileage in a Jeep Grand Cherokee?

Modern Grand Cherokee 4x4 models lose only about one mile per gallon compared to 4x2 models, thanks to front-axle disconnect systems.

Q7: Why is my Jeep Grand Cherokee getting low MPG?

Low fuel economy is commonly caused by under-inflated tires, dirty engine air filters, aggressive driving, heavy aftermarket accessories, or cold weather.

Q8: What is the highway range of a Jeep Grand Cherokee V6?

With a 23-gallon fuel tank and a 26 MPG highway rating, the V6 Grand Cherokee can travel over five hundred and fifty miles between fill-ups.
