What Does the Maint Reqd Light Mean?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

In Toyota and Lexus vehicle maintenance, digital instrumentation, and scheduled servicing (covering Toyota Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, and Tundra), 'MAINT REQD' stands for 'Maintenance Required'—an automated dashboard indicator light that illuminates every 5,000 miles to remind drivers that routine factory-recommended scheduled maintenance (specifically engine oil change, oil filter replacement, and tire rotation) is due.

The Automated 5,000-Mile Mileage Counter Mechanism

A pervasive source of unnecessary panic among Toyota owners is mistaking the 'MAINT REQD' light for an ominous 'Check Engine' malfunction light. In reality, MAINT REQD is not triggered by a mechanical breakdown or sensor failure; it is simply an automated odometer countdown timer programmed into the instrument cluster software.

The system operates on an exact mathematical sequence: At 4,500 miles following the last reset, the light flashes for approximately 10 to 12 seconds each time the vehicle is started to serve as an advance advisory reminder. At exactly 5,000 miles, the MAINT REQD light illuminates solid and remains lit continuously until the maintenance is performed and the computer counter is manually reset.

What Factory Service is Due at 5,000-Mile Intervals?

Toyota's factory scheduled maintenance warranty booklet recommends servicing vehicles every 5,000 miles or 6 months (whichever occurs first). The specific service alternates based on vehicle mileage and synthetic oil specifications.

For Toyotas running full synthetic 0W-20 or 0W-16 engine oil, the manufacturer specifies full oil and filter replacement every 10,000 miles (under normal driving conditions). Therefore, at the 5,000-mile mark, the required service is primarily a tire rotation, brake pad thickness inspection, fluid top-off, and visual multi-point safety inspection. At the 10,000-mile mark (and every subsequent 10,000 miles), the service includes full synthetic oil change, OEM oil filter, tire rotation, and cabin air filter inspection.

Understanding the MAINT REQD reminder allows Toyota owners to maintain factory warranty coverage, preserve peak fuel efficiency, and enjoy Toyota's world-famous 300,000-mile durability.

How to Reset the Toyota MAINT REQD Light in Under 60 Seconds

Step-by-step reset instructions for push-button and key-ignition Toyotas.

  1. Turn Ignition to ON and Display 'Trip A' on the Odometer

    Turn the key to ON (or press push-button start twice with foot off brake); press the trip odometer stalk until 'Trip A' or 'ODO' displays.

  2. Turn the Ignition Completely OFF

    Turn the key to Off, or press the engine Start/Stop button once to power down the instrument cluster.

  3. Press and Hold the Odometer Reset Button (Trip Stalk)

    With the car off, press and hold down the Trip Odometer reset button firmly with your thumb.

  4. Turn Ignition Back to ON While Continuing to Hold the Button

    Turn the key back to ON (or double-press Start button with foot off brake) while keeping the reset button held down.

  5. Watch the Five Dashes Count Down to Zero

    Observe the digital odometer screen; five horizontal dashes will flash and count down ('-----', '----', '---', '--', '-'), display '000000', and turn off.

  6. Release the Button and Start the Engine

    Release the reset button; start the engine to confirm the MAINT REQD light is completely extinguished for the next 5,000 miles.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does MAINT REQD mean on a Toyota?

MAINT REQD stands for 'Maintenance Required.' It is a routine 5,000-mile reminder that your vehicle is due for an oil change, tire rotation, or inspection.

Q2: Is MAINT REQD the same as the Check Engine light?

No. The Check Engine light indicates a broken part or sensor; MAINT REQD is simply a mileage countdown timer reminding you to service your car.

Q3: Can I drive my car with the MAINT REQD light on?

Yes, it is completely safe to drive. The light is not an emergency, but you should schedule an oil change and tire rotation within the next few hundred miles.

Q4: Why does MAINT REQD flash for 10 seconds when starting?

When you reach 4,500 miles, the light flashes for 10 seconds as an early warning that scheduled maintenance is due in 500 miles.

Q5: How often does the MAINT REQD light come on in a Toyota?

The light automatically illuminates every 5,000 miles after it is reset, regardless of how clean the oil is or how the car was driven.

Q6: How do you reset the MAINT REQD light on a Toyota?

Hold the Trip A reset button, turn the ignition to ON, watch the five dashes count down to zero on the odometer, and release the button.

Q7: Does my Toyota need an oil change every 5,000 miles if I use synthetic?

Most modern Toyotas using 0W-20 or 0W-16 full synthetic oil require oil changes every 10,000 miles; at the 5,000-mile light, you only need a tire rotation.
