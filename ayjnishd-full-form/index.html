AYJNISHD Full Form: Speech & Hearing Institute

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of AYJNISHD is the Ali Yavar Jung National Institute of Speech and Hearing Disabilities (Divyangjan). Established on 9th August 1983 in Bandra (West), Mumbai, AYJNISHD is the apex national autonomous institute functioning under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India. Dedicated to the comprehensive rehabilitation, clinical therapy, educational empowerment, and research advancement for individuals with hearing, speech, language, and communication impairments, AYJNISHD operates regional centres across Kolkata, New Delhi, Secunderabad, and Janla, serving as India's premier academic center for audiology and speech-language pathology education.

Historical Inception and Institutional Vision of AYJNISHD

Communication through spoken language and auditory perception is fundamental to human socialization, cognitive development, educational attainment, and economic livelihood. For millions of children and adults suffering from congenital sensorineural deafness, progressive hearing loss, vocal cord pathologies, stuttering, or post-stroke aphasia, the inability to hear and articulate words erects daunting societal barriers.

Recognizing the urgent national need for a centralized apex institution dedicated to speech and hearing sciences, the Government of India established the Ali Yavar Jung National Institute for the Hearing Handicapped (AYJNIHH) in 1983, named in honor of Nawab Ali Yavar Jung, a revered scholar and former Governor of Maharashtra. Modernized and renamed as the Ali Yavar Jung National Institute of Speech and Hearing Disabilities (Divyangjan), the institute has served for over four decades as the national beacon of human resource training, clinical diagnosis, biomedical assistive research, and community outreach.

Clinical Services and Assistive Technology Dispensing

Every day, the clinical departments of AYJNISHD in Mumbai and its regional hubs provide diagnostic and rehabilitative care to hundreds of patients from across the subcontinent. The Department of Audiology operates sound-treated acoustic chambers equipped with computerized two-channel audiometers, otoacoustic emission (OAE) newborn hearing screeners, and Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry (BERA) systems to diagnose infant deafness within the first months of life.

Simultaneously, the Department of Speech-Language Pathology conducts therapeutic interventions for cerebral palsy, autism spectrum communication delays, cleft palate speech, and post-laryngectomy speech rehabilitation. Under the Government of India’s Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances (ADIP) scheme, AYJNISHD acts as the nodal agency for fitting advanced programmable digital hearing aids and coordinating free surgical cochlear implants for underprivileged young children, enabling them to enter mainstream schooling.

Indian Sign Language (ISL) and Community Outreach Programs

AYJNISHD has been a pioneer in standardizing and promoting Indian Sign Language (ISL) as an official visual-gestural language. The institute develops multimedia digital sign language dictionaries, trains certified sign language interpreters for national television broadcasts and courtrooms, and promotes inclusive bilingual education for the deaf community.

Recognizing that rural citizens cannot travel to Mumbai or regional capitals, AYJNISHD operates mobile diagnostic vans that travel into remote tribal and rural districts. These mobile units conduct free community hearing screening camps, distribute thousands of assistive hearing devices, educate grassroots Anganwadi workers on early childhood developmental milestones, and empower rural families to break the silent cycle of disability stigma.

How to Obtain Clinical Speech and Hearing Services at AYJNISHD

  1. Visit Registration Counter with Identity Proof

    Visit the outpatient department (OPD) at the Mumbai headquarters or nearest regional centre with the patient's Aadhaar card and prior medical records.

  2. Undergo Initial ENT and Audiological Assessment

    Consult with medical ENT specialists and licensed audiologists for otoscopic examination, Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA), and Tympanometry.

  3. Complete Speech and Language Diagnostic Evaluation

    Attend clinical evaluation sessions with speech-language pathologists to assess articulation, language comprehension, stammering, or voice disorders.

  4. Receive Hearing Aid Trial and Assistive Technology

    Undergo digital hearing aid fitting trials, earmold impression fabrication, or evaluation for cochlear implant candidacy under government welfare schemes (ADIP).

  5. Enroll in Regular Speech Therapy and Rehabilitation

    Schedule individualized speech therapy sessions, auditory-verbal therapy (AVT), and parental counseling to support continuous communication development.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of AYJNISHD?

The full form of AYJNISHD is Ali Yavar Jung National Institute of Speech and Hearing Disabilities (Divyangjan).

Q2: When was AYJNISHD established and where is it headquartered?

AYJNISHD was established on 9th August 1983 and is headquartered in Bandra (West), Mumbai, Maharashtra.

Q3: Which central government ministry governs AYJNISHD?

It operates under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

Q4: Who was Ali Yavar Jung, after whom the institute is named?

Nawab Ali Yavar Jung was a distinguished Indian diplomat, scholar, and former Governor of Maharashtra.

Q5: Where are the Regional Centres of AYJNISHD located?

Regional centres are located in Kolkata (West Bengal), New Delhi, Secunderabad (Telangana), and Janla (Bhubaneswar, Odisha).

Q6: What professional degrees are offered at AYJNISHD?

AYJNISHD offers BASLP, M.Sc (Audiology), M.Sc (Speech-Language Pathology), B.Ed Special Education (Hearing Impairment), and Ph.D. degrees.

Q7: What is the ADIP scheme implemented at AYJNISHD?

The ADIP scheme provides free or subsidized high-quality digital hearing aids and cochlear implant surgeries to underprivileged disabled children.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Ali Yavar Jung National Institute of Speech and Hearing Disabilities (AYJNISHD) stands as an indispensable national monument to human dignity, clinical compassion, and scientific excellence. By producing thousands of licensed audiologists and speech pathologists, delivering state-of-the-art diagnostic interventions, and dispensing life-changing assistive technologies to underprivileged children nationwide, AYJNISHD empowers individuals with communication disabilities to discover their voice. Through unwavering dedication to education, research, and rehabilitation, the institute ensures that every citizen can listen, speak, and participate fully in the vibrant tapestry of national life.
