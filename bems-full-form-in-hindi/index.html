BEMS Full Form in Hindi: Electro Homeopathy Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of BEMS in Hindi is बैचलर ऑफ इलेक्ट्रो-होम्योपैथी मेडिसिन एंड सर्जरी (विद्युत-समचिकित्सा औषधि एवं शल्य चिकित्सा स्नातक). In English, BEMS stands for Bachelor of Electro-Homeopathy Medicine and Surgery. It is an undergraduate diploma and degree program in alternative natural medicine rooted in the nineteenth-century therapeutic principles developed by Italian researcher Count Cesare Mattei. Electro-homeopathy operates on the fundamental medical philosophy that human illnesses stem from vitiation or metabolic imbalances in the blood and lymphatic circulatory fluids. Practitioners administer non-toxic spagyric herbal extracts distilled from medicinal botanical plants to restore cellular vitality and stimulate the body innate biological self-healing mechanisms without synthetic pharmaceuticals.

The Foundational Philosophy of Electro-Homeopathy Medicine

Modern society is witnessing an unprecedented renaissance in natural and holistic healthcare. While conventional pharmacology delivers potent interventions for acute trauma and infectious crises, patients increasingly seek non-toxic therapies for chronic metabolic, circulatory, and degenerative disorders. Electro-homeopathy arose in nineteenth-century Europe as an alternative therapeutic modality designed to restore physiological balance without synthetic chemicals or invasive procedures.

The term electro in electro-homeopathy does not denote electrical currents or mechanical devices. Instead, Count Cesare Mattei coined the designation to signify the rapid, dynamic, and bio-energetic action of spagyric plant essences upon human cells, comparing the therapeutic speed to bio-electrical impulses. By treating the biological terrain of the human body, electro-homeopathy aims to eliminate the root causes of disease.

The Science of Spagyric Plant Remedies and Clinical Practice

A unique pillar of BEMS education is the mastery of spagyric pharmacy. Spagyrism involves a multi-stage botanical extraction process where herbs are fermented, distilled, incinerated to extract mineral ash salts, and recombined into a harmonious, energetic tincture. This classical process ensures that all active phytonutrients, trace minerals, and volatile bio-compounds are preserved.

In clinical practice, electro-homeopaths categorize patient constitutions into sanguine, lymphatic, or mixed temperaments. By matching botanical remedy complexes to specific constitutional tissue states, practitioners stimulate lymphatic drainage, optimize blood purification, and promote lasting wellness.

How to Pursue and Complete a BEMS Medical Program

  1. Verify Higher Secondary Science Qualifications

    Complete 10+2 higher secondary education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) securing at least forty-five to fifty percent aggregate marks.

  2. Enroll in a Recognized Alternative Medicine Institute

    Select an institute affiliated with recognized electro-homeopathy councils or boards (such as NEHM of India) through merit admission.

  3. Complete Four Academic Years of Clinical Studies

    Study anatomy, human physiology, pathology, spagyric pharmacy, botanical materia medica, and clinical diagnostics across four years.

  4. Complete Mandatory Six-Month Hospital Internship

    Undertake hands-on clinical internship in an affiliated herbal hospital or dispensary, receiving practice registration upon graduation.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the complete full form of BEMS in Hindi and English?

In Hindi, it is बैचलर ऑफ इलेक्ट्रो-होम्योपैथी मेडिसिन एंड सर्जरी; in English, it is Bachelor of Electro-Homeopathy Medicine and Surgery.

Q2: What is the total duration of the BEMS course?

The BEMS program spans four and a half years, comprising four years of didactic study and a compulsory six-month clinical internship.

Q3: Who developed the medical system of electro-homeopathy?

Count Cesare Mattei introduced the healing philosophy of electro-homeopathy in Italy in 1865 using botanical spagyric distillations.

Q4: Can a BEMS practitioner prescribe allopathic pharmaceutical drugs?

No, BEMS practitioners are legally authorized to prescribe certified electro-homeopathic herbal remedies, not allopathic medications.

Q5: What is the legal status of electro-homeopathy practice in India?

Qualified practitioners can practice alternative electro-homeopathy, as upheld by Supreme Court orders and Central Health Ministry notifications.

Q6: How does BEMS differ from traditional homeopathy (BHMS)?

BHMS is a classical Hahnemannian homeopathy degree under the National Commission for Homoeopathy; BEMS relies strictly on plant spagyrism.

Q7: How are electro-homeopathic medicines manufactured?

Remedies are prepared from 114 non-toxic medicinal plants utilizing spagyric cohobation and water distillation techniques.

Q8: What career opportunities are available after BEMS?

Graduates establish private wellness clinics, serve as consultants in alternative therapy centers, or conduct herbal research.
