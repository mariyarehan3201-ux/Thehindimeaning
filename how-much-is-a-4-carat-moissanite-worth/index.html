How Much Is a 4 Carat Moissanite Worth?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 14, 2026

Understanding how much a 4 carat moissanite is worth requires recognizing its status as an engineered silicon carbide gemstone celebrated for exceptional fire and accessible luxury. Because natural moissanite from meteorite craters is virtually non-existent in sizes large enough for jewelry, all gem-grade moissanite is synthesized in high-tech laboratories. A loose 4-carat equivalent moissanite stone (measuring approximately 10.0 to 10.5 millimeters across) typically retails between $500 and $2,200, representing a fraction of the cost of mined or lab-grown diamonds while delivering intense brilliance.

Color Grading, Brand Pedigree, and Retail Price Tiers

In retail jewelry markets, the price of a 4-carat moissanite depends primarily on its color grading tier and manufacturing brand. Premium colorless stones classified in the D-E-F color range command the highest prices, typically between $800 and $2,000 for a loose 4-carat gem. Stones in the near-colorless G-H-I range display faint warm or greenish-gray undertones under certain lighting conditions and sell for between $450 and $900.

Brand recognition also introduces substantial price stratification. Industry pioneer Charles & Colvard charges premium rates for its trademarked Forever One colorless moissanite, where a 4-carat equivalent round gem retails around $1,600 to $2,400. Conversely, overseas manufacturers utilizing similar silicon carbide crystallization techniques offer unbranded loose 4-carat moissanite stones through wholesale direct-to-consumer portals for $350 to $700.

Physical Properties: Moissanite Versus Natural and Lab Diamonds

Evaluating moissanite worth necessitates comparing its physical and optical metrics against diamonds. Moissanite has a refractive index of 2.65 to 2.69, which is distinctly higher than a diamond index of 2.42. This property, combined with a dispersion rating more than double that of diamond (0.104 versus 0.044), produces the signature disco-ball or rainbow fire effect that becomes especially prominent in large 4-carat sizes.

In terms of durability, moissanite scores 9.25 on the Mohs hardness scale, trailing only diamond (10.0). It easily scratches sapphire, ruby, and glass, making it impervious to everyday abrasion and clouding. While a natural 4-carat diamond can easily cost $60,000 to $120,000, and a 4-carat lab diamond runs $2,500 to $5,000, a 4-carat moissanite achieves a striking visual appearance for a tiny fraction of the investment.

The Resale Reality: Secondary Market and Salvage Worth

While a 4-carat moissanite makes an exceptional wearable statement piece, prospective buyers must understand its secondary liquidation profile. Unlike natural diamonds and precious metals, synthetic moissanite possesses virtually no investment or collateral value. Pawnshops and commercial gold buyers treat loose moissanite as a costume stone, generally refusing to buy loose gems or offering a token $20 to $50 for salvage.

When a complete 4-carat moissanite ring is sold to a jeweler or pawnshop, the payout is calculated almost entirely on the scrap melt value of the precious metal setting—such as 14k gold or platinum. However, if you sell the complete ring directly to another private consumer through second-hand wedding forums or social marketplaces, you can typically recover thirty to fifty percent of what you originally paid by marketing it as a gorgeous ready-to-wear engagement ring.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can a jeweler tell that a 4 carat moissanite is not a diamond?

Yes, an experienced jeweler will immediately identify moissanite using a 10x loupe by spotting double refraction or using a basic gemological tester.

Q2: Does a 4 carat moissanite look fake because of its size?

Because a 4-carat gem measures over 10mm wide, its intense rainbow fire dispersion is very pronounced and can look synthetic if compared directly to a diamond.

Q3: Will a 4 carat moissanite pass a diamond tester?

Standard thermal diamond testers will register moissanite as diamond because silicon carbide conducts heat similarly; specialized electrical testers are required.

Q4: How much does a complete 4 carat moissanite ring cost?

A complete ring mounted in solid 14k gold or platinum generally costs between $1,200 and $3,200 depending on setting complexity.

Q5: Does moissanite lose its sparkle over time?

No, moissanite never turns cloudy or loses its internal fire; any dullness is simply surface skin oils that clean off easily with warm soapy water.

Q6: What is the resale value of a 4 carat moissanite?

Resale value is low; pawnshops and jewelers rarely pay for loose stones, but private buyers on secondary marketplaces may pay 30 to 50 percent of retail.

Q7: What shape looks best for a 4 carat moissanite?

Elongated shapes like emerald, radiant, and crushed-ice oval cuts look stunning in 4 carats while tempering excessive rainbow flashes.

Q8: Is moissanite considered a fake diamond?

No, moissanite is a legitimate, distinct gemstone with its own unique chemical formula (SiC) and superior optical fire, not an imitation glass or CZ.
