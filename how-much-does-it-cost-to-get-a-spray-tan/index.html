How Much Does It Cost to Get a Spray Tan?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

Achieving a radiant, golden complexion without exposing your delicate skin to damaging ultraviolet (UV) radiation has made professional spray tanning a premier beauty service. If you are budgeting for a sunless glow before a vacation, wedding, or photo shoot, a single spray tan session generally costs between $25 and $85. Automated spray tan booths offer a budget-friendly option at $25 to $45 per session, while personalized custom airbrush sessions applied by an experienced artist range from $45 to $90. Luxury mobile concierge spray tans in your home or hotel suite command between $85 and $175.

Automated Spray Booths vs Custom Airbrush Technicians

The primary factor influencing spray tan pricing is the application method selected. Automated spray tan booths, including widely recognized systems like VersaSpa Pro and Mystic Tan, deliver rapid, private application through multi-directional automated spray nozzles. Customers stand inside the enclosed private booth following recorded voice prompts to turn their body. Automated booths cost between $25 and $45 per session and take less than five minutes, making them a popular, economical choice for routine maintenance.

Custom airbrush tanning represents a personalized luxury experience costing between $45 and $90. A certified sunless tanning technician utilizes a handheld HVLP (High Volume Low Pressure) airbrush gun to manually apply cosmetic bronzer and dihydroxyacetone (DHA) mist across your contours. The artist customizes solution intensity to your skin undertones, provides barrier cream around dry cuticles, and can perform subtle muscle contouring along collarbones and abs that automated booths cannot replicate.

Formulation Upgrades, Rapid Solutions, and Add-On Treatments

Sunless tanning salons offer a variety of formula enhancements and skin-prep add-ons that enhance color depth and extend wear time. Standard spray tan solutions require eight to twelve hours of development time before your initial water rinse, prohibiting sweating, showering, or getting caught in rain. Rapid rinse formulations (costing an additional $10 to $20) utilize accelerated delivery systems that permit you to rinse after just one to four hours while the tan continues developing over the next twenty-four hours.

Salons also offer skincare-infused additive drops that can be blended directly into your custom solution. Anti-aging drops infused with hyaluronic acid, collagen peptides, and botanical antioxidants cost an extra $5 to $15 per session. Similarly, shimmer drops for bridal galas, post-tan drying powders to prevent stickiness, and pH-balancing prep spray primers add nominal fees that elevate the overall sensory experience.

Skin Prep, Maintenance Rituals, and Salon Membership Economics

The longevity and flawless fade of any spray tan depends heavily on rigorous client skin preparation. DHA interacts with amino acids in dead surface skin cells of the stratum corneum to produce melanoidin pigments. Exfoliating with an oil-free mitt twenty-four hours prior removes patchy dead skin patches, while avoiding moisturizing body washes on the morning of your session prevents barrier films that cause streaking.

For sunless tanning enthusiasts who maintain a year-round tan, purchasing single sessions becomes financially inefficient. Most dedicated tanning salons offer monthly unlimited memberships ranging from $60 to $120 per month, or multi-session packages (such as 3 sessions for $120 or 5 sessions for $180). Visiting a salon every ten days under an unlimited membership cuts the effective cost per spray tan down to under twenty dollars per visit.

How to Prepare for and Maintain a Spray Tan in Five Steps

Follow these verified preparation and aftercare steps to ensure a streak-free, long-lasting golden sunless tan.

  1. Exfoliate and Shave 24 Hours in Advance

    Use an oil-free exfoliating mitt and body wash 24 hours prior to shed dead skin cells and allow pores to close after shaving.

  2. Arrive with Clean Skin Free of Cosmetics

    Shower before your appointment without applying body lotions, perfumes, deodorants, or makeup that create barriers to DHA absorption.

  3. Apply Barrier Cream to Palms and Dry Zones

    Rub barrier cream onto fingernails, toenails, palms, heels, and knuckles to prevent unnatural dark pigmentation on calloused skin.

  4. Wear Loose Dark Clothing and Flip-Flops

    Dress in baggy, dark cotton clothing and slip-on sandals immediately after tanning to avoid creasing or rubbing away fresh bronzer.

  5. Adhere Strictly to Designated Initial Rinse Timing

    Perform your first warm-water-only rinse at the exact hour recommended by your technician, avoiding soaps until 24 hours post-session.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How long does a spray tan last?

A professional spray tan typically lasts 7 to 10 days with proper skin hydration and daily moisturizing with oil-free lotions.

Q2: Will a spray tan make me look orange?

Modern salon solutions feature violet, green, and neutral undertones that counteract warm tones, producing natural olive and bronze shades rather than orange discoloration.

Q3: How many days before an event should I get a spray tan?

The optimal window to get a spray tan is two days (48 hours) prior to your event, giving the tan time to develop fully and allowing cosmetic bronzers to be rinsed clean.

Q4: What should I wear during a spray tan session?

You can wear a dark swimsuit, disposable underwear provided by the salon, or go fully nude depending on your comfort level and desired tan lines.

Q5: Can I shower right after a spray tan?

No, standard tans require waiting 8 to 12 hours before showering, while rapid rinse solutions allow an initial warm-water rinse in 1 to 4 hours.

Q6: Does a spray tan protect against sunburn?

No, spray tans provide zero ultraviolet sun protection; you must apply broad-spectrum sunscreen daily when outdoors.

Q7: Can I workout or sweat after a spray tan?

You must avoid heavy sweating, swimming, or working out until after your initial shower rinse to prevent streaking and patchy discoloration.

Q8: How much should I tip my spray tan artist?

A standard gratuity of fifteen to twenty percent ($10 to $15 on a $60 tan) is customary for personalized custom airbrush services.
