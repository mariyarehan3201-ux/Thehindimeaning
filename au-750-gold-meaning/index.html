Au 750 Gold Meaning

Meaning
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

In fine jewelry manufacturing, luxury horology, and precious metals assaying, the stamp 'Au 750' is an internationally recognized hallmark certifying that the jewelry piece is forged from authentic 18-karat (18K) solid gold, containing exactly 75.0% pure elemental gold alloyed with 25.0% other precious or strengthening metals. The symbol 'Au' derives from the Latin 'Aurum' (the chemical element for gold on the periodic table), while '750' indicates the metal's millesimal fineness—measuring 750 parts of pure gold per 1,000 parts of total alloy.

How to Verify and Care for Au 750 Gold Jewelry

Essential care and authentication steps for owners of 18K solid gold pieces.

  1. Locate and Inspect the Hallmark with a 10x Loupe

    Examine the inner ring shank, necklace clasp, or watch caseback for crisp, laser-etched or struck 'Au 750' or '750' hallmarks.

  2. Conduct a Specific Gravity or Magnet Test

    Solid 18K gold is non-magnetic; if your jewelry is attracted to a strong neodymium magnet, it contains a magnetic iron or nickel core.

  3. Clean Regularly with Warm Water and Mild Dish Soap

    Soak 18K jewelry in warm soapy water for 15 minutes, gently brush with an extra-soft toothbrush, and rinse thoroughly with warm water.

  4. Store Separately in Velvet-Lined Compartments

    Because 18K gold is softer than diamond and sapphire gemstones, store pieces in individual pouches to prevent surface scratches.

  5. Request XRF Assay Testing at a Reputable Jeweler

    Have a certified gemologist verify the exact precious metal alloy percentages non-destructively using an X-ray fluorescence (XRF) analyzer.
