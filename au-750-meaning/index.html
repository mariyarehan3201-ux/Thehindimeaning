Au 750 Meaning

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

When inspecting luxury jewelry, Swiss watches, or fine heirlooms, understanding Au 750 meaning confirms precious metal authenticity. The hallmark Au 750 combines the chemical symbol for elemental gold (Au, from Latin aurum) with the millesimal fineness number 750, signifying 75.0% pure gold alloyed for 18-karat luxury.

Precious metal hallmarking is one of humanity's oldest forms of consumer protection, dating back to ancient Byzantine and medieval European assay guilds. By stamping standardized fineness markings into precious metals, independent assay offices protect buyers from counterfeit alloys, providing verifiable legal proof of precious metal content recognized across global borders.

The Millesimal Fineness System and International Hallmarking

While American jewelry markets traditionally utilize the 24-point karat system (stamping "18K" or "18Kt"), international trade relies on the millesimal fineness system established by the Convention on the Control and Marking of Articles of Precious Metals (the Vienna Convention). In this system, purity is calculated out of 1,000 proportional parts.

The table below illustrates how Au 750 relates to standard karat designations and international precious metal fineness standards:

Au 999 / 999 24 Karat (24K) 99.9% Pure Gold Bullion bars, investment coins

An Au 750 hallmark is frequently accompanied by a maker's sponsor mark and an independent national assay office mark (such as the Swiss Helvetia head or the British anchor/leopard head), providing multi-layered provenance for luxury buyers.

Color Formulations in 18K Au 750 Gold

Because 25% of an Au 750 alloy consists of non-gold elements, metallurgists can alter the metal's visual color, luster, and hardness by adjusting the recipe of secondary metals. Whether crafting yellow gold, white gold, or rose gold, the gold content always remains locked at exactly 75.0%.

Au 750 represents the preferred choice for premier luxury jewelry houses such as Cartier, Tiffany & Co., Van Cleef & Arpels, and Rolex. Its 75% gold concentration imparts a distinctly rich, warm saturation that 14k gold cannot match, while providing structural strength that keeps precious diamonds firmly secured.

How to Authenticate an Au 750 Hallmark Stamp

  1. Examine the Hallmark Under 10x Magnification

    Use a jeweler's loupe to inspect the engraving inside a ring or on a watch case back, verifying crisp "Au 750" or "750" lettering.

  2. Check for Official Assay Office Sponsor Marks

    Look for accompanying national assay marks, such as the Common Control Mark (scales symbol) of the Vienna Convention.

  3. Perform an Electronic XRF Spectrometer Analysis

    Have an accredited jeweler scan the metal using X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) to measure the exact elemental 75% gold concentration without damage.

  4. Test Magnetic Susceptibility

    Hold a strong neodymium magnet to the piece; genuine 18-karat gold contains non-ferrous alloys and will show zero magnetic attraction.

  5. Calculate Hydrostatic Density and Specific Gravity

    Measure the item's dry weight and submerged water displacement to confirm a specific gravity between 15.2 and 15.9 g/cm³.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does Au 750 mean on a piece of jewelry?

Au 750 means the piece is crafted from 18-karat gold containing exactly 750 parts per thousand (75.0%) pure elemental gold, with "Au" being the chemical symbol for gold.

Q2: Is Au 750 real gold or just plated?

Au 750 designates solid 18-karat gold throughout the entire item, not gold plating. It represents one of the highest international purity standards used in luxury fine jewelry.

Q3: What metals make up the remaining 25% in Au 750?

The remaining 25% consists of alloying metals like copper and silver (for yellow and rose gold) or palladium, nickel, and silver (for white gold) to provide durability.

Q4: How much is Au 750 gold worth per gram?

Its raw intrinsic melt value equals exactly 75% of the live market spot price for pure 24k gold per gram, plus additional artistic and brand premiums.

Q5: Why do European jewelers stamp 750 instead of 18K?

Under the international Hallmarking Convention (Vienna Convention), purity must be stated in millesimal fineness parts per thousand (750) rather than fractional American karats (18K).

Q6: Can Au 750 gold tarnish or turn skin black?

Solid Au 750 is highly resistant to corrosion and will not tarnish under normal conditions, though skin reactions can rarely occur if someone has a severe allergy to trace nickel or copper.

Q7: Is Au 750 durable enough for daily engagement ring wear?

Yes. Au 750 offers an exceptional balance of rich, saturated color and structural strength, making it the preferred alloy for luxury engagement rings worldwide.
