IXE Full Form: Mangaluru Airport Code & Aviation Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 18, 2026

The code IXE is the official three-letter international location identifier assigned by the International Air Transport Association (IATA) to Mangaluru International Airport (formerly Bajpe Airport) serving the coastal city of Mangalore (Mangaluru) in Karnataka, India. Known in international civil aviation for its dramatic hilltop setting and challenging tabletop runway engineering, IXE is Karnataka's second busiest international airport, providing direct non-stop flights to major domestic metros and the Arabian Gulf.

Understanding IXE: Gateway to Coastal Karnataka

Every commercial airport across the globe receives a standardized three-letter location code from the International Air Transport Association (IATA) to facilitate seamless ticketing, worldwide baggage tag routing, air traffic control flight planning, and automated airline reservation global distribution systems (GDS). The unique identifier IXE identifies Mangaluru International Airport. The ICAO four-letter location code for the same airport is VOML.

Commissioned in 1951 by former Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru, the airport is situated on the elevated crest of a hill near Bajpe village, approximately 13 kilometers northeast of central Mangaluru city. The airport serves as the primary aviation hub for the entire coastal Kanara region, Kodagu (Coorg), and neighboring Kasaragod district of Kerala. In 2006, the airport commenced international flights, serving the vast expatriate Indian workforce employed across the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman.

Tabletop Runway Engineering and Runway 06/24 at IXE

The geographical topography of Mangaluru International Airport makes it one of the most distinctive aviation facilities in South Asia. The table below details the technical runway specifications of IXE.

Runway Identifier Runway Length & Width Surface Construction Material Operational Status
Runway 09/27 (Old Runway) 1,625 meters (5,330 ft) x 45m Asphalt Bituminous Pavement Decommissioned for commercial jets; used for emergency operations
Runway 06/24 (New Concrete Runway) 2,450 meters (8,038 ft) x 45m High-strength Pavement Quality Concrete (PQC) Primary active operational runway accommodating Airbus A320/Boeing 737
Instrument Landing System (ILS) Cat-I ILS on Runway 24 Localizer and Glide Path radio guidance Permits precision instrument landings during heavy coastal monsoon rains
RESA Extension Runway End Safety Area (240m x 90m) Compacted engineered soft ground bed Arrests aircraft in the event of an accidental runway overshoot

A 'tabletop runway' is an aviation term describing an airstrip constructed by leveling the flat crest of a hill or plateau, surrounded on one or both ends by steep descending cliffs or deep ravines. Because the surrounding terrain drops away abruptly, pilots have no visual peripheral ground reference during final flare, creating an optical illusion where the aircraft appears higher than its true altitude. Landing on a tabletop runway requires rigorous specialized airline pilot simulator training and strict adherence to touchdown point markers.

Terminal Infrastructure and Passenger Facilities at IXE

To accommodate expanding passenger traffic, a state-of-the-art integrated passenger terminal was commissioned on the Kenjar hillside in 2010. The table below highlights key infrastructure dimensions of the terminal.

Terminal Feature Infrastructure Capacity Passenger Amenity Provided
Terminal Floor Area Over 18,200 square meters integrated terminal Handles both domestic and international passengers in modern air-conditioned comfort
Passenger Handling Peak Over 1,000 peak-hour passengers (2+ million annually) Features multiple check-in islands, immigration counters, and customs kiosks
Aerobridge Boarding Gates 4 glass-walled passenger boarding bridges Direct aircraft boarding shielding passengers from torrential monsoon downpours
Cargo Terminal (Domestic/International) Dedicated air cargo logistics complex Exports fresh coastal fish, vegetables, and cashews directly to Gulf markets

Under the management of Adani Airport Holdings Limited (AAHL) under a public-private partnership (PPP) concession, IXE has undergone comprehensive terminal renovations, runway recarpeting, smart baggage handling system installations, and runway center-line lighting upgrades to ensure safe round-the-clock operations.

How Passengers Book and Navigate Departures at Mangaluru Airport (IXE)

Follow this travel guide to check flight schedules, navigate security screening, and depart smoothly from Mangaluru International Airport.

  1. Book Ticket Using Destination Code IXE

    Enter airport code 'IXE' on airline booking engines or travel aggregators to find non-stop flights to Mumbai, Bengaluru, Dubai, or Doha.

  2. Arrive at Kenjar Terminal with Adequate Time

    Reach the terminal building 2 hours prior for domestic flights and 3 hours prior for international Gulf departures, carrying valid passport and ticket.

  3. Complete Baggage Drop and Boarding Pass Printing

    Use self-service kiosk check-in machines or airline counters to drop hold baggage and collect your paper or mobile boarding passes.

  4. Pass CISF Security and Emigration Screening

    Proceed through central CISF security portals and bureau of immigration desks (for international travelers), presenting boarding passes.

  5. Board Aircraft via Covered Aerobridges

    Relax in duty-free lounges or coffee shops until boarding announcement, boarding through glass aerobridges connected directly to aircraft doors.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does the airport code IXE stand for?

IXE is the three-letter IATA airport location code for Mangaluru International Airport in Karnataka, India.

Q2: What is the ICAO code for Mangaluru Airport?

The four-letter ICAO code for Mangaluru International Airport is VOML.

Q3: Why is Mangaluru airport called a tabletop airport?

Because its runways are built on top of a leveled plateau hill surrounded by steep valley ravines at both ends.

Q4: Which international destinations have direct flights from IXE?

Direct flights operate to Gulf destinations including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Muscat, Doha, Dammam, Bahrain, and Kuwait.

Q5: Who operates Mangaluru International Airport?

It is operated by Mangaluru International Airport Limited under Adani Airport Holdings Limited (AAHL) in a PPP model.

Q6: How far is IXE airport from central Mangalore city?

The airport is located at Kenjar/Bajpe, approximately 13 kilometers northeast of central Mangaluru city center.

Q7: Can large wide-body aircraft like Boeing 777 land at IXE?

Due to runway length (2,450m) and tabletop geography, IXE operates narrow-body aircraft like Airbus A320 and Boeing 737.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The IATA airport code IXE designates Mangaluru International Airport, a vital commercial aviation bridge connecting coastal Karnataka with Indian metropolitan hubs and the Middle East. Through continuous engineering upgrades, expanded runway safety areas, and modernized terminal amenities, IXE remains a cornerstone of economic growth, tourism, and diaspora connectivity for the region.
