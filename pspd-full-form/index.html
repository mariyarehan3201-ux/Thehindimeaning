PSPD Full Form: ITC Paperboards Division Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of PSPD is Paperboards and Specialty Papers Division, the specialized paper, packaging, and pulp manufacturing business division of Indian conglomerate ITC Limited (ITC PSPD). Headquartered in Secunderabad, Telangana, with its flagship integrated manufacturing mill in Bhadrachalam, ITC PSPD is India's largest, most technologically advanced, and ecologically sustainable producer of value-added paperboards, packaging boards, graphic papers, and specialty papers. The division manufactures premium packaging substrates for pharmaceuticals, fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), food service, electronics, and cigarette packaging, leading India's transition toward recyclable, plastic-free packaging solutions.

Corporate Profile and Industrial Scale of ITC PSPD

The modern packaging industry is undergoing a monumental transition. Driven by global mandates to phase out single-use plastics, consumer demand for circular packaging, and stringent food contact safety regulations, multi-national brand owners increasingly demand high-performance paperboard solutions. At the vanguard of this industrial movement stands ITC Limited's Paperboards and Specialty Papers Division (ITC PSPD).

Originally incorporated in 1979 as Bhadrachalam Paperboards Limited before merging into ITC Limited in 2002, the division operates state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities across India, including Bhadrachalam and Bollaram in Telangana, Tribeni in West Bengal, and Kovai in Tamil Nadu. The Bhadrachalam complex ranks among the largest single-location integrated pulp and paperboard mills in Asia, producing over 900,000 metric tonnes of premium paperboards and specialty papers annually.

Environmental Sustainability and Clonal Social Forestry

Paper and pulp manufacturing has historically faced scrutiny over deforestation and water consumption. ITC PSPD transformed this environmental paradigm through its Clonal Farm and Social Forestry initiative. Rather than harvesting natural virgin forests, ITC research scientists developed high-yielding, drought-resistant clones of Eucalyptus, Subabul, and Casuarina trees that thrive in semi-arid soils.

Partnering with over 200,000 marginalized rural and tribal farmers across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, ITC supplies disease-resistant saplings and guarantees buy-back pricing for harvested pulpwood. To date, this program has afforested over one million acres of degraded private land, creating over 170 million person-days of rural employment while sequestering millions of tonnes of atmospheric carbon dioxide, enabling ITC to maintain its status as a carbon-positive, water-positive enterprise.

Technological Innovation in Recyclable and Barrier Packaging

With increasing regulatory bans on single-use polyethylene (PE) laminated paper cups and plates, ITC PSPD pioneered bio-dispersion barrier technologies. Conventional hot paper cups feature an extruded plastic lining that resists liquids but prevents standard paper repulping and recycling, forcing billions of cups into municipal landfills.

ITC PSPD’s patented Omega series replaces traditional plastic liners with a proprietary water-based bio-emulsion. Paperboards manufactured with this barrier are 100% recyclable in conventional mill repulpers, compostable under industrial conditions, and completely free of harmful polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS). By delivering leak-proof performance for piping hot coffee and oily snacks, ITC PSPD enables quick-service restaurant (QSR) chains to eliminate thousands of tons of plastic waste annually.

How to Procure and Select Paperboard Grades from ITC PSPD

  1. Identify Packaging Application and Caliper Needs

    Determine packaging requirements, such as folding cartons for pharmaceuticals, moisture-barrier food trays, or high-definition graphic prints.

  2. Select Specific ITC PSPD Brand Line

    Choose appropriate board grades, such as Cyber XL Pac (FBB for pharma), Safire Graphik (SBS for cosmetics), or Carte Lumina for luxury packaging.

  3. Verify GSM Weight and Stiffness Parameters

    Review technical data sheets to select grammage (GSM) ranging from 180 to 450 gsm based on carton structural integrity and stacking load demands.

  4. Submit Bulk Specification Order to ITC PSPD

    Place orders specifying sheet dimensions or reel widths, grain direction, and coating requirements through ITC's authorized regional distributors.

  5. Conduct High-Speed Printing and Die-Cutting Trials

    Run test print sheets through offset lithography, flexography, or gravure presses to verify ink adhesion, creasing performance, and fold fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of PSPD?

The full form of PSPD is Paperboards and Specialty Papers Division, the paper manufacturing unit of ITC Limited.

Q2: Where is the flagship manufacturing mill of ITC PSPD located?

The flagship integrated pulp and paperboard mill is located in Bhadrachalam, Bhadradri Kothagudem district, Telangana.

Q3: What are the primary products manufactured by ITC PSPD?

Products include Folding Box Boards (FBB), Solid Bleached Sulphate (SBS) boards, specialty cigarette tissues, and barrier paperboards.

Q4: What sustainable packaging initiatives has ITC PSPD launched?

ITC PSPD developed biodegradable, compostable brands like 'OmegaBev' and 'OmegaBarr' to replace single-use plastic takeaway containers.

Q5: Does ITC PSPD practice social forestry for pulp wood?

Yes, through its Social and Farm Forestry programs, ITC has greened over 1 million acres, providing rural livelihoods to tribal farmers.

Q6: Which industries consume paperboard from ITC PSPD?

Major consumer industries include pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, FMCG food packaging, electronics, luxury spirits, and publishing.

Q7: Is ITC PSPD certified by the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC)?

Yes, ITC PSPD holds FSC Chain of Custody certification, verifying that its pulp is sourced from responsibly managed forests.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

ITC Limited's Paperboards and Specialty Papers Division (PSPD) stands as a beacon of world-class industrial manufacturing, ecological stewardship, and innovative packaging science. From its mammoth integrated mill at Bhadrachalam to packaging conversions across global supply chains, ITC PSPD provides the sustainable foundation that allows modern brands to thrive in a post-plastic economy. Through visionary social forestry, circular bio-barrier technologies, and unmatched metallurgical manufacturing precision, ITC PSPD continues to set the benchmark for Asian pulp and paper manufacturing.
