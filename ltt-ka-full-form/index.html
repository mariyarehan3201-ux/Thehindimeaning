LTT Full Form: Lokmanya Tilak Terminus Station Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The acronym LTT in Indian Railways and travel logistics stands for Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (station code: LTT). Historically known as Kurla Terminus, LTT is a major railway terminus situated in the eastern suburb of Kurla in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Administered under the Mumbai division of the Central Railway (CR) zone, LTT was developed to decongest the iconic Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) and serves as the primary gateway originating and terminating hundreds of long-distance superfast, express, and AC special trains connecting Mumbai with northern, eastern, and southern India.

लोकमान्य टिळक टर्मिनस (LTT) चा ऐतिहासिक परिचय आणि रेल्वे महत्त्व

Mumbai, often celebrated as the financial heartbeat of India, welcomes millions of passengers every week across its rail network. For decades, long-distance rail traffic was concentrated heavily at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) in South Mumbai. As population and train frequencies expanded, CSMT reached absolute track capacity. Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT) was developed as the modern terminus to absorb this growing passenger demand.

Constructed in the 1990s and initially named Kurla Terminus, the station was formally renamed in 1996 to honor the pioneering Indian freedom fighter and social reformer Bal Gangadhar Tilak, revered across Maharashtra with the honorary title "Lokmanya" (meaning accepted by the people).

Today, LTT operates under the Central Railway zone as a primary terminal hub, originating and receiving dozens of long-haul trains linking Mumbai to Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha, Karnataka, and Kerala.

Over the last decade, Central Railway has invested heavily in modernizing LTT. Older, cramped waiting halls have been replaced with an expansive multi-story passenger terminal building featuring green architectural elements, rainwater harvesting, and solar power installations.

LTT also features an advanced coaching maintenance depot with automated coach-washing plants and bio-toilet evacuation systems, ensuring trains depart clean and mechanically inspected.

How to Navigate and Board Trains at Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT)

  1. Reach LTT via Suburban Train or Metro

    Arrive at Tilak Nagar or Kurla suburban railway station on the Harbour/Central lines, or take an auto-rickshaw directly to the LTT main station porch.

  2. Verify Live Train Status on Display Boards

    Check your train number and allocated platform number on the large electronic LED display boards located in the central passenger concourse.

  3. Pass Through Security and Concourse Amenities

    Pass baggage through X-ray screening scanners; utilize waiting lounges, cloakrooms, or IRCTC food plazas if arriving early.

  4. Access Platforms via Foot Overbridges and Escalators

    Use the wide foot overbridges (FOBs) equipped with escalators and lifts to reach your designated platform without crossing tracks.

  5. Locate Coach Using Electronic Indicators

    Match your reserved coach number (e.g., B1, S4) with the hanging digital coach guidance indicators along the platform edge and board safely.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is LTT ka full form in railways?

LTT stands for Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (station code: LTT).

Q2: Where is Lokmanya Tilak Terminus located?

LTT is located in Kurla East, Mumbai, Maharashtra, operated by Central Railway.

Q3: What was the previous name of LTT station?

It was formerly known as Kurla Terminus before being renamed in 1996 in honor of freedom fighter Bal Gangadhar Tilak.

Q4: Why was LTT built in Mumbai?

To decongest Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) and Dadar station by shifting northern and eastern long-distance trains to Kurla.

Q5: Which is the nearest local suburban railway station to LTT?

Tilak Nagar railway station (on the Harbour Line) and Kurla Junction (on the Central Line) are closest to LTT.

Q6: How many platforms are operational at LTT?

LTT features 5 high-level operational platforms capable of handling full-length 24-coach passenger trains.

Q7: What popular long-distance express trains originate from LTT?

LTT-Gorakhpur Superfast, LTT-Varanasi Kamayani Express, LTT-Howrah Jnaneshwar Superfast, and LTT-Prayagraj Duronto Express.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT) is one of the busiest and most important railway terminals in Mumbai. Developed in Kurla to absorb interstate passenger traffic, LTT offers modern concourses, spacious platforms, and seamless connectivity, serving as a vital transportation bridge linking the metropolis of Mumbai to every corner of India.
