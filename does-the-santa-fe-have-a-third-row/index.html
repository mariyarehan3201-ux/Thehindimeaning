Does the Santa Fe Have a Third Row?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

Yes, the redesigned 2024 and newer Hyundai Santa Fe comes standard with three-row seating across all trim levels. However, whether a Hyundai Santa Fe includes a third row depends entirely on the specific model year you are evaluating. Earlier models from the 2019 through 2023 model years were exclusively two-row, five-passenger crossovers, whereas older generations from 2013 to 2018 offered a dedicated long-wheelbase three-row version known as the Santa Fe XL. Understanding these generational shifts is essential for family car shoppers evaluating new and pre-owned crossover inventories.

Generational Evolution of Hyundai Santa Fe Seating Configurations

The seating architecture of the Hyundai Santa Fe has undergone several strategic revisions over the past two decades. During the third generation spanning 2013 to 2018, Hyundai sold two distinct body styles: the two-row Santa Fe Sport and the extended three-row Santa Fe (later renamed the Santa Fe XL). When Hyundai introduced the flagship three-row Palisade for the 2020 model year, the fourth-generation Santa Fe (2019 to 2023) was repositioned strictly as a midsize two-row crossover accommodating five passengers.

For the fifth-generation debut in 2024, Hyundai executed a dramatic boxy redesign that stretched the wheelbase by nearly two inches and expanded the overall body length. This architectural overhaul allowed Hyundai engineers to integrate standard fold-flat third-row seating into every 2024 and 2025 Santa Fe model. Buyers can select either a seven-passenger layout with a second-row bench or a premium six-passenger configuration equipped with power-reclining second-row captain chairs.

Interior Dimensions, Passenger Comfort, and Cargo Versatility

While the newest fifth-generation Santa Fe provides three rows of seats, understanding realistic passenger dimensions is crucial. The first two rows offer class-leading legroom, supportive cushioning, and generous headroom thanks to the vehicle upright, geometric roofline. The third row provides approximately thirty inches of legroom and thirty-seven inches of headroom, making it exceptionally comfortable for children, teenagers, and smaller adults on daily commutes or cross-town trips.

Cargo versatility is one of the standout highlights of the modern Santa Fe interior. When the third row is deployed, cargo volume behind the rearmost seats measures approximately 14.6 cubic feet, which accommodates several grocery bags or carry-on luggage. Folding down the 50/50 split third-row seats expands cargo capacity to over 40 cubic feet, providing an enormous, completely flat load floor for sports equipment, camping gear, and bulky cargo.

Comparing the Santa Fe to the Hyundai Palisade for Family Buyers

Shoppers evaluating a three-row Hyundai frequently weigh the newest Santa Fe against the larger Hyundai Palisade. While both vehicles seat multiple passengers, they serve slightly different lifestyle profiles. The Palisade is a full midsize crossover with eight-passenger seating capability and an authoritative 3.8-liter V6 engine, catering to large families who require constant adult seating in all three rows and heavier towing capacities.

In contrast, the Santa Fe appeals to drivers seeking adventurous styling, better fuel economy, and agile urban maneuverability. With its turbocharged four-cylinder powertrain and available hybrid configuration returning superior miles per gallon, the Santa Fe functions as an agile everyday family crossover where the third row can be deployed as needed for carpools and weekend outings.

How to Configure and Operate Santa Fe Third-Row Seats in 5 Steps

Follow these five operational steps to adjust, fold, or deploy the third-row seating in a modern Hyundai Santa Fe crossover.

  1. Access the Rear Liftgate Cargo Area

    Open the hands-free power smart liftgate from the key fob button or exterior rear handle to view the folded seatbacks.

  2. Pull the Upright Deployment Straps

    Grasp the heavy-duty release straps located on the back of each third-row seat and pull firmly upward until the seatback locks into place.

  3. Adjust the Headrest Positions

    Lift the fold-down headrests upward until they click securely into their upright safety support positions for passenger travel.

  4. Slide the Second Row for Optimal Legroom

    Use the one-touch second-row slide buttons to balance knee space between second-row and third-row occupants.

  5. Fold Flat for Maximum Cargo Deck Space

    Pull the seat release levers and push the seatbacks forward to create a seamless flat cargo loading surface across the cabin floor.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Does every 2024 Hyundai Santa Fe have a third row?

Yes, all 2024 and newer Hyundai Santa Fe trims include three-row seating as standard factory equipment.

Q2: Can adults sit comfortably in the Santa Fe third row?

Yes, average-sized adults can ride comfortably on short to medium journeys, though taller passengers prefer the first two rows.

Q3: Did the 2022 or 2023 Hyundai Santa Fe have a 3rd row?

No, fourth-generation models from 2019 through 2023 were strictly two-row, five-passenger crossover vehicles.

Q4: How many car seats fit in the modern Santa Fe third row?

The third row features two full seating positions equipped with tether anchors, fitting two forward-facing booster or child seats.

Q5: Is the Hyundai Santa Fe available as a 6-passenger vehicle?

Yes, upper trim levels like the Calligraphy feature second-row captain chairs, configuring the vehicle for six passengers.

Q6: Does the Santa Fe Hybrid model retain the third row?

Yes, the Santa Fe Hybrid preserves the exact same three-row seating configuration as the standard turbocharged gasoline model.

Q7: What is the difference between Santa Fe and Santa Fe XL?

The Santa Fe XL was the long-wheelbase three-row version produced from 2013 to 2018 prior to the launch of the Palisade.

Q8: How much cargo room is left behind the Santa Fe third row?

There is approximately 14.6 cubic feet of dedicated luggage storage space behind the upright third-row seatbacks.
