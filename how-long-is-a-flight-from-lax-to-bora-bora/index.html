How Long Is a Flight From Lax to Bora Bora?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how long is a flight from lax to bora bora requires understanding that there are no nonstop commercial flights directly connecting Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) to Bora Bora Airport (BOB). Because the runway on Motu Mute in Bora Bora is too short to accommodate wide-body intercontinental commercial jets, all travelers from the United States must fly first to Tahiti's Faa'a International Airport (PPT) and transfer to an inter-island turboprop flight. In total, the travel time from LAX to Bora Bora spans between 10.5 to 13.5 hours, including approximately 8 to 8.5 hours in the air and a mandatory layover in Tahiti.

Leg One: Nonstop Wide-Body Flight from LAX to Tahiti (PPT) (8 Hours)

The first and longest portion of the journey is the transpacific flight departing from Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) heading due south across the equator to Faa'a International Airport (PPT) in Papeete, Tahiti. Spanning a flight distance of roughly 4,100 nautical miles, nonstop flights operated by carriers like Air Tahiti Nui, Air France, and Delta Air Lines take approximately 8 hours to 8 hours and 30 minutes of flight time.

Most westbound flights from LAX to PPT depart in late evening (around 11:00 PM or 11:50 PM), allowing passengers to sleep overnight on modern Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners or Airbus A350 aircraft and land in Tahiti at roughly 5:00 AM local French Polynesia time. Daytime flights are also scheduled on select days of the week, departing LAX around 1:00 PM and touching down in Tahiti around 6:30 PM.

The Tahiti Layover and Island Hopper: Flying PPT to BOB (50 Minutes)

Upon touching down at Tahiti's Faa'a International Airport, all international passengers must disembark across the outdoor tarmac, proceed through French Polynesia border passport control, collect their checked baggage, and clear agricultural customs. This arrival and immigration process typically takes 45 to 60 minutes.

After exiting international customs, travelers walk approximately 100 feet within the same open-air terminal building to the domestic Air Tahiti check-in counters. Air Tahiti operates domestic ATR 72-600 twin-engine turboprop regional aircraft that take only 50 minutes of flight time to travel 160 miles northwest across the Society Islands to Bora Bora Airport (BOB). Because Air Tahiti uses unassigned open seating on island hops, experienced travelers arrive early to secure window seats on the left side of the aircraft for world-famous aerial panoramas of Mount Otemanu and the turquoise lagoon.

Resort Boat Transfers: Arriving at Your Overwater Bungalow

Touching down at Bora Bora Airport (BOB) on the islet of Motu Mute does not immediately place you at your resort. Because Bora Bora's premier luxury resorts—such as the Four Seasons, St. Regis, Conrad, and InterContinental Thalasso—are built on secluded outer barrier reef motus surrounding the main island, there are no roadways or automobile taxis connecting the airport.

Instead, arriving passengers exit the tiny thatched-roof open-air terminal directly onto the marina docks where private mahogany resort speedboats await. Resort staff welcome travelers with flower leis, take luggage, and navigate across the crystalline lagoon in an unforgettable 15-to-30-minute water boat transfer directly to your overwater bungalow dock.

How to Book and Navigate Travel from LAX to Bora Bora

Follow these five strategic tips to ensure a seamless, stress-free flight connection to French Polynesia.

  1. Book Your LAX-PPT and Domestic Air Tahiti Tickets Together

    Book an integrated ticket or allow at least 2 to 3 hours layover in Tahiti to prevent missing inter-island connections.

  2. Pack Within Air Tahiti's Domestic Baggage Weight Limits

    Domestic ATR turboprops strictly enforce 50 lb (23 kg) checked bag limits and 11 lb (5 kg) carry-on limits.

  3. Coordinate Your Resort Boat Transfer in Advance

    Inform your luxury resort of your Air Tahiti flight arrival number so their private boat captain greets you at the airport dock.

  4. Secure a Left-Side Window Seat on the Domestic Flight

    Choose a seat on the left side of the ATR turboprop departing Tahiti for iconic views of Bora Bora's lagoon.

  5. Adjust Your Watch for the 2 to 3-Hour Time Zone Difference

    French Polynesia (Tahiti Time) is 2 hours behind Los Angeles (PST) or 3 hours behind during Daylight Saving Time (PDT).

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can you fly directly from LAX to Bora Bora without stopping?

No, Bora Bora's airport cannot accommodate wide-body jets; all travelers must stop and connect through Tahiti (PPT).

Q2: How long is the actual flying time from LAX to Bora Bora?

Actual flying time is approximately 9 hours: 8 hours and 15 minutes to Tahiti plus 50 minutes from Tahiti to Bora Bora.

Q3: How long is the layover in Tahiti usually?

Layovers typically range from 1.5 to 3.5 hours depending on morning inter-island flight schedules.

Q4: What airlines fly from LAX to Tahiti?

Air Tahiti Nui, Air France, and Delta Air Lines operate nonstop flights between Los Angeles (LAX) and Tahiti (PPT).

Q5: What airline flies between Tahiti and Bora Bora?

Air Tahiti (and newer boutique carrier Air Moana) operates daily scheduled turboprop flights between PPT and BOB.

Q6: How far is Bora Bora from Los Angeles in miles?

The flight distance is roughly 4,100 nautical miles (approx. 4,700 statute miles or 7,500 kilometers).

Q7: Do you need a visa to visit Bora Bora from the US?

US citizens do not need a visa for tourist stays under 90 days, but a valid passport with at least 3 to 6 months validity is required.

Q8: How do you get from the Bora Bora airport to your hotel?

By boat; luxury resorts provide private catamaran and speedboat transfers directly from the airport dock to their resort motus.
