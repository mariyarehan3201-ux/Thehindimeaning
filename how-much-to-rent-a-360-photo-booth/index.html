How Much to Rent a 360 Photo Booth?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Renting a 360 photo booth typically costs between $175 and $350 per hour, with most event bookings requiring a two- to four-hour minimum package ranging from $500 to $1,400 total. For upscale weddings, corporate galas, and milestone birthday celebrations, comprehensive packages often run $800 to $1,500, which includes a commercial-grade rotating platform, professional studio ring lighting, an on-site technician, custom branded video overlays, slow-motion effects, and instant QR code or AirDrop sharing stations. Understanding platform size, software capabilities, and venue power requirements will help you secure the perfect interactive entertainment for your event.

Hourly Rates, Package Inclusions, and Platform Diameters

The rental rate for a 360 video booth is primarily driven by operating duration and platform capacity. Standard compact platforms measure 27 to 32 inches in diameter and accommodate 1 to 2 guests safely, pricing at the lower end of the spectrum ($450 to $750 for 2 to 3 hours). Medium platforms measuring 35 to 40 inches hold 3 to 4 adults, serving as the industry benchmark for standard wedding receptions at $650 to $1,100.

Large commercial platforms measuring 45 inches or wider support 5 to 7 guests simultaneously, featuring heavy-duty motorized revolving arms, counterbalanced camera gimbals, and high-torque motors. These high-capacity setups command $900 to $1,600+ for four hours, making them ideal for high-traffic corporate experiential marketing activations and lively fraternity or prom events where large groups want to dance together on the platform.

Technical Features: Software Overlays, Slow-Motion, and Instant Sharing

What elevates a modern 360 booth rental above a static camera booth is specialized video processing software (such as Touchpix, Snappic, or Lumabooth). As the revolving arm sweeps around guests at high speed, high-frame-rate cameras (typically an iPhone 15 Pro, GoPro Hero Black, or mirrorless DSLR) capture 120 to 240 frames per second footage. The software instantly processes the clip, applying boomerang loops, high-definition slow-motion ramping, audio soundtracks, and custom event border graphics.

Within 30 seconds of stepping off the platform, guests can scan a dynamically generated on-screen QR code, receive an SMS text, or use Apple AirDrop to download their finished vertical MP4 video directly to their smartphones. This instantaneous delivery allows attendees to immediately publish viral content to Instagram Stories, TikTok, and corporate LinkedIn feeds during the party.

Venue Space Requirements, Safety Clearances, and Attendant Roles

Securing a 360 booth requires verifying that your venue possesses adequate physical square footage and electrical infrastructure. Because the motorized camera arm spins rapidly in an unobstructed circle, safety guidelines mandate a minimum 10x10 foot clearance zone, with 12x12 feet preferred. Professional rental vendors demarcate this safety perimeter using velvet stanchions or crowd-control barricades to prevent passing guests from being struck by the revolving arm.

Never hire a 360 photo booth that operates unassisted without a live attendant. Professional rental agreements include one or two uniformed technicians who guide guests onto the platform, inspect footwear to prevent slips, operate the wireless motor start/stop remote, cue music, and assist elderly or intoxicated guests. The attendant ensures continuous throughput, safely processing 40 to 60 guest videos per hour.

How to Book and Prepare for a 360 Photo Booth Rental

Follow these five straightforward steps to hire a reputable 360 booth vendor for your upcoming celebration.

  1. Confirm Venue Space and Electrical Outlets

    Ensure the event venue can allocate a flat 10x10 foot floor space located within 25 feet of a dedicated standard 110V 15A outlet.

  2. Verify Camera Hardware and High Frame Rates

    Ask vendors if they use 4K 120fps cameras (such as modern iPhones or GoPros) rather than cheap tablets that yield blurry videos.

  3. Design Custom Graphic Overlays in Advance

    Provide your vendor with high-resolution PNG logos, color schemes, and event typography two weeks prior to the booking.

  4. Confirm Two On-Site Professional Attendants

    Ensure your package includes dedicated technicians to direct guests, maintain safety stanchions, and facilitate instant sharing.

  5. Request a Post-Event Online Master Video Gallery

    Confirm that the vendor will provide a cloud folder link containing all full-resolution MP4 clips recorded throughout the night.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How much does a 360 photo booth cost for a wedding?

A typical 3- to 4-hour wedding 360 photo booth package costs between $650 and $1,200, including custom overlays, props, and attendants.

Q2: How much space does a 360 photo booth require?

A 360 photo booth requires a flat, cleared footprint of at least 10x10 feet to allow the motorized arm to spin safely without hitting guests.

Q3: How many people can stand on a 360 booth at once?

Platform capacity depends on diameter: 28-inch platforms hold 1 to 2 people, 35-inch platforms hold 3 to 4, and 45-inch platforms hold 5 to 7 guests.

Q4: Do 360 photo booths print physical photos?

No, 360 photo booths produce high-definition dynamic video clips with music and slow motion, shared digitally via AirDrop, SMS, and QR codes.

Q5: Can a 360 photo booth be set up outdoors?

Yes, provided the ground is completely flat and level, protected by a canopy tent from direct sun or rain, with reliable power available.

Q6: How long does setup take for a 360 photo booth?

A professional attendant typically arrives 60 to 90 minutes before your scheduled start time to assemble platforms, ring lights, and stanchions.

Q7: Do guests need Wi-Fi to receive their videos?

No, the booth operator provides their own wireless hotspot or Apple AirDrop connection to beam videos directly to guest smartphones.

Q8: What should you wear on a 360 photo booth?

Flowing dresses, jackets, and accessories that look dynamic in slow motion work best; flat shoes are recommended for stability on the platform.
