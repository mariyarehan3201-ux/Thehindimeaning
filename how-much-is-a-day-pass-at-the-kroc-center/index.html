How Much Is a Day Pass at the Kroc Center?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Constructed through a visionary multi-billion-dollar philanthropic bequest from philanthropist Joan Kroc to The Salvation Army, Ray and Joan Kroc Corps Community Centers represent some of the most impressive recreational, aquatic, and educational facilities in North America. If you are asking how much is a day pass at the kroc center, single-day guest passes generally cost between $6 and $12 for youth or seniors, $10 and $18 for adults (ages 18 to 61), and $25 to $45 for a family day pass. Pricing fluctuates depending on the specific location—such as San Diego, Phoenix, Chicago, or Coeur d Alene—and the seasonal amenities available.

National Kroc Center Day Pass Pricing Across Major Locations

Because each Kroc Center is operated as an independent regional community hub under local Salvation Army division management, day pass rates are structured around local economic demographics and facility square footage. Flagship locations—such as the massive 12-acre San Diego Kroc Center or the expansive Phoenix South Mountain Kroc Center—charge slightly higher adult rates ranging from $14 to $18 per day. These premier hubs boast resort-style aquatic centers with lazy rivers, dual 30-foot water slides, competition lap pools, ice arenas, and climbing towers.

Midwestern and regional community Kroc Centers—including locations in South Bend (Indiana), Quincy (Illinois), Omaha (Nebraska), and Dayton (Ohio)—feature very affordable rates, with adult day passes costing between $9 and $14, and youth passes priced at $6 to $9. Many facilities also offer discounted twilight passes or evening rates for guests arriving two hours before closing, as well as punch cards (such as 10-visit passes for $90 to $130) that provide 15 to 25 percent savings over single-day gate prices.

Amenities and Facilities Included with Your Day Pass

A standard Kroc Center day pass delivers remarkable value, granting guests all-day, full-access privileges to world-class sports and fitness amenities that rival luxury commercial health clubs. The primary attraction for families is the indoor aquatic complex, which features heated leisure recreation pools, zero-depth beach entries, interactive water play structures, dumping buckets, high-speed twisting water slides, and relaxing lazy rivers. Certified lifeguards supervise all aquatic spaces, and life jackets are provided free of charge for novice swimmers.

Beyond the water park, adult day pass holders enjoy unrestricted access to the comprehensive cardio and strength fitness center, equipped with modern treadmills, ellipticals, free weights, and resistance machines. Day passes also cover drop-in open gym hours for basketball, volleyball, and pickleball on regulation hardwood courts. Where applicable, day passes grant access to the indoor climbing wall and indoor turf fields, while specialty amenities like public ice skating sessions require a modest $3 to $5 skate rental fee.

Entry Requirements, Photo ID Policies, and Membership Credits

To ensure maximum community safety, all Salvation Army Kroc Centers enforce strict guest intake protocols. Every guest aged 18 and older must present a valid, government-issued photo ID (such as a driver license, military ID, or passport) at the member services welcome desk. The staff scans all adult identification cards through a national sex offender background screening registry before issuing wristbands. Minors under age 18 must have a digital liability waiver signed by a legal parent or guardian on file.

If you fall in love with the facilities during your day visit, many Kroc Centers offer an attractive membership conversion policy. If you decide to join as an ongoing monthly member on the same day as your visit, the front desk will apply the full cost of your day pass toward your first month membership dues or waive the standard registration setup fee. In addition, The Salvation Army offers need-based scholarship subsidies, ensuring low-income families can access facilities regardless of financial means.

How to Purchase and Use a Day Pass at the Kroc Center

Follow these five tactical steps to check schedules, register at the desk, and enjoy your visit to the Kroc Center.

  1. Review the Daily Aquatics and Gym Schedule

    Check your local Kroc Center online calendar for public open-swim hours, slide operational times, and open gym availability.

  2. Bring Valid Government Photo Identification

    Ensure every visitor aged 18 and older brings a valid driver license or state ID for mandatory safety database screening.

  3. Sign the Online Electronic Liability Waiver

    Complete the mandatory guest waiver online prior to arrival to bypass long check-in lines at the front desk.

  4. Pack Proper Swim and Athletic Attire

    Bring family-appropriate swimsuits, pool towels, a lock for day lockers, and dry athletic sneakers for the gym.

  5. Inquire About Same-Day Membership Credits

    Ask the front desk staff if your day pass receipt can be credited toward a monthly recurring membership before leaving.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How much is a day pass at the Kroc Center for a child?

Day passes for youth (ages 3 to 17) typically cost between $6 and $10 depending on the specific Kroc Center location.

Q2: Do you have to be a Salvation Army member to go to the Kroc Center?

No, Kroc Centers are open to the general public regardless of religious affiliation; anyone can purchase a day pass or membership.

Q3: Do adults need an ID to buy a Kroc Center day pass?

Yes, all adults aged 18 and older must present a valid government-issued photo ID for safety and sex-offender database screening.

Q4: Are towels and lockers provided at the Kroc Center?

Day lockers are free to use, but guests must bring their own padlock and shower towels, as towel service is not provided.

Q5: Is the water park and slide included in the day pass?

Yes, the day pass includes full access to the leisure pool, lazy river, and water slides during scheduled open recreational swim hours.

Q6: What is included in a Kroc Center family day pass?

A family day pass ($25 to $45) covers up to two adults and up to four dependent children residing in the same household.

Q7: Can you bring outside food into the Kroc Center?

Small personal water bottles are permitted, but coolers, fast food, and large outside food spreads are prohibited inside the facility.

Q8: Does the Kroc Center offer discounts for military or seniors?

Yes, active military personnel, veterans, and seniors aged 62+ receive discounted day pass rates and reduced monthly membership dues.
