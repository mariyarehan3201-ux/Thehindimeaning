Decoding the Term: SF Express and the Physical Pick Up Card

In cross-border shipping and international courier operations, SF refers to SF Express (ShunFeng Express), one of the largest multinational courier and logistics networks in Asia. The company handles extensive freight, e-commerce deliveries, and air mail across China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and international destinations.

A Pick Up Card (also called a Delivery Notice Card or Pickup Voucher) is an electronic SMS notification, paper delivery attempt slip, or in-app QR code generated when a courier cannot complete home delivery. It notifies the consignee that the package has been securely routed to a nearby SF Express service store, authorized retail partner, or automated smart parcel locker (such as an SF Hive locker) awaiting recipient pickup.

Why Packages Are Diverted to Pickup Lockers and Time Limits

In dense metropolitan cities throughout Hong Kong, Singapore, and mainland China, direct door-to-door courier delivery can be hindered by secured gated apartment communities, absent residents during business hours, or high-rise courier access restrictions. Diverting deliveries to self-service smart lockers provides 24/7 flexibility for working consumers.

However, pick up cards carry strict storage time windows. Most SF Express smart lockers grant recipients a free storage window of 24 to 48 hours from the timestamp of the pick up card notification. If a parcel is not collected within the designated window, the system may assess modest hourly holding fees (typically a few local dollars per day) or return the package to the central distribution hub, delaying delivery.

Responding quickly to pickup notifications guarantees you receive your package without incurring storage penalties or risk of return to sender.

How to Claim a Parcel Using an SF Express Pick Up Card

Retrieve your held package from an SF Express smart locker or service depot using these simple steps.

  1. Locate the SMS or Paper Pickup Notification

    Find the message sent to your phone; identify the physical locker location, smart cabinet number, and 6-digit collection code.

  2. Visit the Smart Locker Within the Free Window

    Proceed to the automated locker kiosk before the 24-to-48-hour free holding window expires to avoid overtime holding charges.

  3. Select 'Pick Up' on the Touchscreen Terminal

    Tap the Pick Up / Collect Package button on the central touchscreen display of the smart locker bank.

  4. Enter the 6-Digit Pickup Code or Scan QR Code

    Type your pickup code on the digital keypad or present your mobile phone QR code to the optical barcode scanner below the screen.

  5. Retrieve Package and Firmly Close Locker Door

    The corresponding locker door will pop open automatically; remove your parcel and push the metal door firmly shut until it latches.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does pick up card SF mean?

It means SF Express (ShunFeng) has left a pickup notification (SMS or paper slip) for you to collect your package from a locker or service center.

Q2: Who is SF in shipping?

SF stands for SF Express (ShunFeng Express), a leading Chinese and international express delivery and logistics company.

Q3: How long do I have to pick up my package from an SF locker?

SF Express lockers typically provide 24 to 48 hours of free storage; holding fees may accumulate after the free window expires.

Q4: What if I did not receive a pickup code from SF Express?

Log into the SF Express official app using the recipient phone number, or contact SF customer service with your tracking number to regenerate the code.

Q5: Can someone else pick up my SF package for me?

Yes. Anyone who possesses the exact 6-digit pickup code or app QR code can open the smart locker door to retrieve the parcel.

Q6: What happens if I never pick up my SF package?

After several days of unclaimed storage, the courier will retrieve the package from the locker and return it to the central depot or sender.

Q7: Where are SF Express lockers usually located?

They are located in residential apartment lobbies, subway transit stations, convenience stores, and community shopping centers.

Q8: Do I have to pay to pick up my package?

Standard pickup within the free window is free, unless the shipment was sent Cash on Delivery (COD) or exceeded the overtime storage window.
