How to Choose Between a Dodge SXT and GT Trim

Evaluate these key differences when deciding between the classic SXT and the sportier GT.

  1. Compare Exterior Hood and Body Styling

    The SXT features sleek, understated muscle lines; the GT incorporates aggressive widebody-inspired front splitters, performance hoods, and rear decklid spoilers.

  2. Evaluate Suspension Tuning and Steering

    SXT features standard touring suspension for smooth highway comfort; GT features performance steering and firmer performance suspension.

  3. Inspect Steering Wheel Paddle Shifters

    GT models come standard with zinc die-cast steering wheel paddle shifters and Performance Pages; SXT uses standard automatic shifting.

  4. Examine Wheel Diameters and Tires

    SXT comes standard with 18-inch wheels and comfortable thick-sidewall tires; GT models upgrade to larger 20-inch wheels with low-profile tires.

  5. Factor in All-Wheel Drive Availability

    Both SXT and GT trims offer available All-Wheel Drive (AWD), making both outstanding winter cruisers in northern states.

Q2: Does a Dodge Charger SXT have a V6 or V8 engine?

All modern Dodge SXT models are powered by the 3.6-liter Pentastar V6 engine, producing approximately 292 to 300 horsepower.

Q3: What is the difference between Dodge SXT and GT?

Both share the same 3.6L V6 engine, but the GT adds aggressive SRT performance styling, a functional hood scoop, 20-inch wheels, and firmer suspension.

Q4: Is the Dodge Challenger SXT fast?

Yes. With 303 horsepower, a Challenger SXT accelerates from 0 to 60 MPH in approximately 6.0 seconds, offering spirited muscle performance.

Q5: Does Dodge SXT come in All-Wheel Drive (AWD)?

Yes. Dodge offers an intelligent AWD system on both Charger SXT and Challenger SXT models with automatic front-axle disconnect.

Q6: What is the difference between Dodge SE and SXT?

SE was the basic entry-level fleet model, while SXT adds alloy wheels, touchscreen infotainment, dual exhaust, and upgraded interior materials.

Q7: Can you put regular gas in a Dodge SXT?

Yes. The 3.6L Pentastar V6 engine in the SXT is engineered to run optimally on standard 87 octane regular unleaded gasoline.

Q8: Does a Dodge SXT have leather seats?

SXT models come standard with premium cloth seating, but Nappa leather upholstery is available through optional Plus or Cold Weather packages.
