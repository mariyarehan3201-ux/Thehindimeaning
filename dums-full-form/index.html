DUMS Full Form: Meaning, Course Scope & Eligibility

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The acronym DUMS stands for Diploma in Unani Medicine and Surgery. It is a traditional professional healthcare diploma within the AYUSH (Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy) framework, focusing on the Greco-Arabic traditional system of medicine. Unani medicine is built upon the foundational principles of four bodily humors and individual temperament (Mizaj). The DUMS program prepares students to diagnose conditions through pulse examination (Nabz), prescribe herbal formulations, and manage common ailments using natural therapeutics and regimental therapies.

Historical Foundations and Philosophical Principles of Unani Medicine

The Unani system of medicine, originating in ancient Greece with Hippocrates and Galen and extensively enriched by Arab and Persian scholars like Avicenna (Ibn Sina), represents one of the oldest systematic healthcare frameworks in human history. The Diploma in Unani Medicine and Surgery preserves this rich legacy by training competent frontline healthcare workers.

Central to Unani healing is the concept of Mizaj (temperament) and Akhlat (four bodily humors). When the humors remain balanced in quantity and quality, an individual experiences sound health. Any internal or environmental derangement of this balance produces disease, which Unani practitioners treat by restoring natural equilibrium.

Throughout the DUMS course, students gain comprehensive theoretical and practical knowledge of natural pharmacognosy, compounding botanical formulas, and applying regimental therapies like Hijama (cupping) and Dalk (therapeutic massage).

Integrative Therapeutics: Regimental Regimens and Pharmacology

In addition to classical therapeutics, modern DUMS curricula introduce foundational hygiene, public health, minor surgical procedures (Jarahat), and emergency first aid to ensure patient safety in community health settings.

Graduates of DUMS play a pivotal role in delivering affordable, accessible healthcare, particularly across rural and semi-urban communities where herbal and holistic modalities enjoy widespread cultural trust.

Through systematic training in classical diagnosis and natural herbs, the DUMS program continues to support traditional medicine systems that work harmoniously with human physiological vitality.

How to Pursue a Diploma in Unani Medicine and Surgery (DUMS)

  1. Complete Higher Secondary Education

    Pass 10+2 examinations with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology from a recognized board, preferably with Urdu or Arabic as a language subject.

  2. Appear for Entrance or Qualifying Tests

    Register for state-level AYUSH diploma entrance tests or satisfy institutional merit criteria for Unani medical colleges.

  3. Participate in Counseling and Seat Allocation

    Select your preferred Unani training institute during centralized or institutional counseling rounds.

  4. Undergo Academic and Practical Training

    Complete 2 to 3 years of rigorous study covering Unani anatomy (Tashreeh), physiology (Munafe-ul-Aza), pharmacology (Ilmul Advia), and pathology.

  5. Finish Mandatory Clinical Internship

    Complete a compulsory hospital internship in outpatient and inpatient Unani clinics to gain hands-on diagnostic experience and register with state councils.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of DUMS?

DUMS stands for Diploma in Unani Medicine and Surgery.

Q2: How does DUMS differ from BUMS?

DUMS is a 2 to 3 year diploma program focusing on foundational Unani practices, while BUMS is a comprehensive 5.5-year undergraduate medical degree including internship.

Q3: What are the four humors in Unani medicine?

The four humors (Akhlat) are Dam (Blood), Balgham (Phlegm), Safra (Yellow Bile), and Sauda (Black Bile).

Q4: Is knowledge of Urdu required for DUMS?

Most Unani institutes require students to have studied Urdu, Arabic, or Persian at high school level since fundamental classical texts are documented in these languages.

Q5: Can a DUMS graduate practice as a Unani doctor?

Graduates can practice as registered Unani medical practitioners or clinical assistants, subject to state medical council guidelines and regulations.

Q6: What therapies are covered in DUMS?

The course covers Ilaj-bil-Ghiza (dietotherapy), Ilaj-bil-Dawa (pharmacotherapy), and Ilaj-bil-Tadbeer (regimental therapy such as cupping, massage, and leeching).

Q7: Where do DUMS graduates find employment?

In Unani dispensaries, herbal pharmaceutical companies, wellness retreats, community AYUSH health centers, and private natural clinics.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Diploma in Unani Medicine and Surgery (DUMS) provides an accessible gateway into the time-tested traditions of Greco-Arabic natural medicine. By blending classical principles of humoral balance with practical herbal compounding and regimental therapy, DUMS equips healthcare practitioners to serve diverse patient communities with holistic, natural healthcare solutions.
