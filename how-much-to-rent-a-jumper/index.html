How Much to Rent a Jumper?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Renting a jumper (bounce house) typically costs between $125 and $350 for a standard four- to eight-hour daily rental, with large combination units featuring climbing walls, obstacle courses, or inflatable water slides ranging from $350 to over $700. For a standard 13x13 or 15x15 foot commercial vinyl bounce house at a backyard children's birthday party, parents usually invest around $150 to $225 including drop-off, commercial blower setup, anchoring stakes, and post-party pickup. Understanding inflatable dimensions, surface anchoring requirements, and park permit fees ensures a seamless party setup.

Jumper Categories and Rental Rates: Standard, Combos, and Obstacles

Inflatable jumpers are categorized by structural footprint, activities provided, and wet versus dry operation. The classic standard jumper (often styled as a medieval castle or modular theme) measures 13x13 or 15x15 feet and features an open jumping cushion enclosed by mesh safety netting. These standard units represent the most affordable rental options, pricing between $120 and $220 per day.

Combination inflatables (combos) elevate the excitement by integrating interior basketball hoops, climbing stairs, and attached exterior slides. Dry combos generally rent for $250 to $400, while wet water slide combos equipped with splash pools and integrated garden hose misting systems rent for $350 to $650 due to increased cleaning, water extraction, and heavy transport labor. Large commercial obstacle courses stretching 30 to 70 feet command $400 to $900 per day for school field days and community festivals.

Setup Surfaces, Commercial Blowers, and Generator Power

Operating an inflatable jumper requires continuous electrical airflow and secure ground anchoring. Commercial 1.0 to 2.0 horsepower electric blowers must run non-stop to maintain internal air pressure, drawing 7 to 12 amps of continuous electrical current on a dedicated standard 110V circuit. Rental companies provide 50- to 100-foot heavy-duty extension cords; connecting through improper thin household cords can trip circuit breakers or burn out the blower motor.

The setup surface fundamentally affects safety and installation labor. Grass is the ideal surface because rental crews can drive heavy 18-inch steel stakes into the soil to resist wind gusts. Setting up on concrete driveways, asphalt parking lots, or artificial turf requires heavy sandbag weights or water barrels (typically 200 to 400 pounds per corner) to anchor the inflatable safely, which often incurs a surface surcharge of $25 to $60.

Safety Regulations, Weather Thresholds, and Park Permits

Inflatable safety is governed by strict wind velocity thresholds established by ASTM International (Standard F2374). Jumpers must be immediately evacuated and deflated whenever sustained winds or sudden gusts reach 15 to 20 miles per hour. Failure to anchor or deflate during high wind events can cause catastrophic airborne tipping, making professional anchoring non-negotiable.

If you plan to host your party at a public city park rather than your private backyard, you cannot simply show up with a rental jumper. Most municipal parks and recreation departments require a specialized park pavilion permit, proof of $1,000,000 commercial general liability insurance naming the city as an additional insured party, and a portable gas generator since park electrical outlets are rarely provided or rated for continuous blower operation.

How to Rent and Coordinate a Bounce House Jumper

Follow these five straightforward steps to book, verify safety, and host an inflatable jumper safely.

  1. Measure Backyard Space and Overhead Clearances

    Ensure you have a level area with at least a 3-foot clearance on all four sides and no low-hanging electrical wires or tree branches.

  2. Clear Ground of Rocks, Debris, and Pet Waste

    Walk the installation zone to remove sticks, sprinkler heads, gravel, and animal feces that could puncture commercial vinyl.

  3. Confirm Delivery Window and Power Proximity

    Verify that an outdoor GFCI electrical outlet is located within 50 to 75 feet of the planned blower connection site.

  4. Enforce Strict Safety Rules During Operation

    Group children by similar height and weight, enforce a no-shoes and no-glasses rule, and prohibit rough gymnastics or climbing nets.

  5. Deflate Immediately During High Winds or Rain

    Power off the blower and safely escort children out immediately if winds exceed 15 mph or heavy rain causes slick surfaces.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How much does it cost to rent a bounce house for a day?

A standard bounce house costs between $125 and $220 per day, while larger combo slides range from $250 to $450.

Q2: How long is a typical jumper rental period?

Most companies rent inflatables for 4 to 8 hours, often dropping off in the morning between 8 AM and 11 AM and picking up after 5 PM.

Q3: Can adults go in a rental jumper?

Standard residential bounce houses are engineered for children; however, commercial-grade inflatables with 18oz vinyl can support adults up to weight limits.

Q4: How much power does an inflatable jumper need?

An inflatable blower runs on a standard 110V 15-amp household outlet and draws between 7 and 12 amps of continuous electricity.

Q5: Do jumper rental companies provide delivery and setup?

Yes, professional rental services include delivery, inflation, safety staking, sanitation, and teardown in their standard rental fees.

Q6: Can a jumper be set up on a driveway or concrete?

Yes, but you must notify the rental company so they bring heavy ballast sandbags or water barrels instead of standard soil stakes.

Q7: What happens if it rains on the day of the rental?

Reputable inflatable companies offer flexible weather cancellation policies allowing free rescheduling if rain or high winds are forecast.

Q8: Do you tip the bounce house delivery drivers?

Tipping is customary for hard physical setup labor; $15 to $30 per delivery driver is widely appreciated for polite, prompt service.
