IFFDC Full Form: Indian Farm Forestry Cooperative

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The acronym IFFDC stands for Indian Farm Forestry Development Cooperative Limited. Registered in 1993 as a premier multi-state cooperative society promoted by the Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited (IFFCO), IFFDC is dedicated to converting uncultivated wastelands, sodic lands, and ravine terrains into productive, eco-friendly community forests. Through grassroots Primary Farm Forestry Cooperative Societies (PFFCS), IFFDC integrates ecological afforestation with sustainable rural livelihoods, empowering smallholder farmers, landless laborers, and rural women across India.

Community Forestry, Wasteland Reclamation, and the Founding of IFFDC

India environmental and rural economic vitality depends heavily on the health of its soil and forest cover. Decades of deforestation, topsoil erosion, and soil salinization have left millions of hectares of agricultural land barren and unproductive. The Indian Farm Forestry Development Cooperative Limited (IFFDC) was established to transform these neglected wastelands into thriving green ecosystems.

Conceived by the visionary leadership of IFFCO in the late 1980s and formally incorporated as a multi-state cooperative society in October 1993, IFFDC pioneered a participatory community model. Instead of treating afforestation as a top-down bureaucratic exercise, IFFDC entrusted forest governance directly to local farming families.

By organizing village communities into Primary Farm Forestry Cooperative Societies (PFFCS), the organization provided technical forestry guidance, soil reclamation inputs, and high-quality saplings, transforming barren saline patches into lush woodlands that generate steady local income.

Carbon Sequestration, Soil Moisture Retention, and Rural Livelihoods

A distinctive achievement of IFFDC is its focus on gender inclusion and social equity. In traditional rural economies, landless laborers and marginalized women rarely own land titles. By leasing community panchayat wastelands to the cooperative, IFFDC enables landless families to hold direct equity in the timber and minor forest produce.

Thousands of rural women actively lead nursery raising projects, cultivating millions of saplings annually while participating equally on cooperative board committees.

How IFFDC Implements a Community Wasteland Afforestation Project

  1. Identify Degraded Wasteland Parcels

    Survey barren community panchayat commons, ravine tracts, or waterlogged sodic soils unsuited for traditional crop agriculture.

  2. Mobilize Village Communities into a PFFCS

    Form a village-level Primary Farm Forestry Cooperative Society, enrolling smallholder farming families and women as cooperative shareholders.

  3. Execute Soil Reclamation and Water Harvesting

    Apply gypsum amendments on sodic soils, dig contour trenches, and construct check dams to retain seasonal monsoon rainwater.

  4. Plant Multi-Purpose Tree Species and Grasses

    Plant resilient agro-forestry species like Acacia, Subabul, Teak, Neem, and high-yield fodder grasses suited to local microclimates.

  5. Harvest Sustainable Forest Produce and Distribute Earnings

    Harvest minor forest produce, firewood, fodder, and commercial timber sustainably, distributing net cooperative profits among village members.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of IFFDC?

IFFDC stands for Indian Farm Forestry Development Cooperative Limited.

Q2: Who established and promoted IFFDC?

IFFDC was promoted in 1993 by IFFCO (Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited), the world leading fertilizer cooperative.

Q3: What is the core mission of IFFDC?

To reclaim degraded wastelands through community afforestation, enhance biodiversity, combat climate change, and generate rural employment.

Q4: What is a PFFCS under IFFDC?

A Primary Farm Forestry Cooperative Society, the grassroots village cooperative through which local residents participate and share earnings.

Q5: Which states have major IFFDC farm forestry projects?

Prominent projects operate across Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, and Odisha.

Q6: How does IFFDC empower rural women?

Women constitute over 35% of cooperative membership, leading Self-Help Groups (SHGs) involved in nursery raising, vermicomposting, and seed gathering.

Q7: Does IFFDC engage in carbon credit and climate finance projects?

Yes, IFFDC community forests generate verified carbon sequestering credits, funneling international climate finance back to rural village communities.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Indian Farm Forestry Development Cooperative Limited (IFFDC) is a trailblazer in community-led environmental restoration. By organizing rural farming families into forestry cooperatives and rehabilitating unproductive wastelands, IFFDC combats desertification, sequesters atmospheric carbon, and creates dignified, sustainable livelihoods across rural India.
