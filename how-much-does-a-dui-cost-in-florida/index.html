How Much Does a DUI Cost in Florida?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how much does a DUI cost in Florida reveals why driving under the influence is among the most financially devastating misdemeanor offenses in the state. On average, a first-offense DUI in Florida costs between $8,000 and $12,000 when accounting for statutory fines, court costs, attorney fees, impound charges, and mandatory FR-44 high-risk insurance premiums. Repeat offenses, high-BAC incidents, or collisions causing property damage or injury quickly escalate total costs past $20,000 to $30,000.

Statutory Fines, Court Costs, and Administrative Penalties

Under Florida Statute Section 316.193, a first-time DUI conviction carries mandatory minimum judicial fines ranging from $500 to $1,000 for a blood alcohol concentration (BAC) under 0.15. If the driver had a BAC of 0.15 or higher, or was accompanied by a minor in the vehicle, statutory fines increase to $1,000 to $2,000. In addition to statutory fines, Florida courts impose mandatory court costs, local law enforcement training surcharges, and state crime victim compensation assessments totaling $350 to $600.

Administrative penalties by the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV) add immediate expenses. Reinstating a suspended driver's license requires an administrative fee of $130, a license reissue fee of $75, and a revocation fee of $45. Convicted drivers must also pay for 10 days of mandatory vehicle impoundment ($350 to $600) and complete 50 hours of mandatory community service, which can sometimes be bought out at $10 per hour ($500).

The Crushing Financial Impact of Florida FR-44 Insurance

The single largest long-term expense resulting from a Florida DUI conviction is the mandatory FR-44 insurance certification. While many states require standard SR-22 certificates, Florida law mandates an FR-44 certificate for DUI offenders, requiring dramatically higher liability coverage limits: $100,000 per person bodily injury, $300,000 per accident bodily injury, and $50,000 property damage (100/300/50 coverage). Furthermore, state law requires drivers to prepay this policy in full for six months.

Before a DUI, an average Florida motorist pays approximately $1,800 to $2,600 annually for automobile insurance. With an active DUI conviction and an FR-44 requirement, annual premiums skyrocket to $4,500 to $8,000 or more. Because the FR-44 filing must be maintained continuously for three consecutive years from the conviction date, the cumulative additional insurance expense typically exceeds $9,000 to $18,000 alone.

DUI Defense Attorney Rates and Trial Costs in Florida

Hiring an experienced DUI defense attorney in Florida represents an upfront investment of $2,500 to $6,000 for a misdemeanor first offense. Most private practitioners charge flat fees covering administrative license suspension hearings with the FLHSMV, discovery review, suppression motions regarding field sobriety exercises and breathalyzer calibration, and plea negotiations. If a client elects to take the case to trial, an additional trial fee of $2,000 to $4,000 is common.

While hiring counsel represents an immediate financial outlay, skilled legal defense frequently yields substantial long-term savings. If an attorney negotiates a charge reduction to reckless driving ('wet reckless'), the driver avoids mandatory FR-44 insurance requirements, mandatory license revocation, and the devastating insurance surcharges, saving $10,000 or more in overall out-of-pocket costs.

How to Minimize Financial Fallout After a Florida DUI Arrest

Follow these five strategic steps immediately after a Florida DUI arrest to contain financial damage.

  1. Request a Formal Administrative Review Within 10 Days

    Submit a hearing request or file for an immediate hardship license waiver with the FLHSMV within 10 calendar days of arrest.

  2. Retain a Qualified Florida DUI Defense Lawyer

    Hire an attorney experienced in breath test suppression and video review to explore possibilities for a reckless driving reduction.

  3. Enroll Promptly in State-Approved DUI School

    Register for the Level 1 DUI education course ($280 to $350) early to satisfy statutory requirements for hardship driving permits.

  4. Retrieve Your Vehicle from Impoundment Immediately

    Pay towing and daily storage fees promptly to prevent compounding daily storage fees that exceed hundreds of dollars.

  5. Shop Extensively for Florida FR-44 Insurance Quotes

    Compare multiple non-standard insurance carriers to find competitive prepaid rates for mandatory high-risk coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the total average cost of a first DUI in Florida?

A first-time DUI in Florida typically costs between $8,000 and $12,000 in total direct fines, court costs, attorney fees, and high-risk insurance.

Q2: What is the fine for a first-time DUI in Florida?

Statutory fines range from $500 to $1,000 for a standard BAC, or $1,000 to $2,000 if BAC is 0.15 or higher or a minor was in the vehicle.

Q3: What is Florida FR-44 insurance and why is it expensive?

FR-44 is a mandatory high-risk insurance certificate requiring 100/300/50 liability limits prepaid for 6 months, tripling insurance premiums for 3 years.

Q4: Can a Florida DUI be expunged or sealed?

No, under Florida law, a DUI conviction cannot be expunged or sealed and remains permanently on your criminal and driving records.

Q5: Is jail time mandatory for a first DUI in Florida?

Jail is not mandatory for a standard first offense, though judges can impose up to 6 months (or up to 9 months for BAC over 0.15).

Q6: How long is your license suspended for a DUI in Florida?

A first-offense conviction carries a mandatory driver's license revocation of 6 to 12 months, though hardship licenses may be available.

Q7: What is a 'wet reckless' in Florida?

A wet reckless is a plea bargain reducing a DUI charge to reckless driving with alcohol as a factor, avoiding mandatory DUI penalties and FR-44 insurance.

Q8: How much does an ignition interlock device cost in Florida?

Installation costs $75 to $150, followed by monthly rental and monitoring fees of $70 to $100 ($500 to $1,100 total for 6 months).
