SRPF Full Form in Marathi: State Reserve Police Force

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of SRPF in Marathi is राज्य राखीव पोलीस बल (State Reserve Police Force). In English, SRPF stands for State Reserve Police Force, which is the specialized armed police wing of the Maharashtra State Police. Established under the statutory provisions of the Bombay State Reserve Police Force Act of 1951, the SRPF functions as a highly trained, rapid-deployment tactical paramilitary force. Headed by an officer of the rank of Additional Director General of Police (ADGP), this specialized armed reserve is organized into sixteen operational groups strategically stationed across Maharashtra to maintain law and order, execute anti-Naxal operations in dense forest zones, counter communal disturbances, and provide impenetrable security during critical state events.

The internal security apparatus of Maharashtra relies heavily on the disciplined tactical capability of the State Reserve Police Force. While regular district police forces and city commissionerates handle daily crime investigations, traffic management, and routine civil policing, armed escalations require specialized intervention. When civil agitations turn violent, communal tensions flare, or armed insurgents threaten sovereign installations, the state administration deploys battalions of the SRPF to neutralize threats, restore civil peace, and protect vital public infrastructure.

Founded shortly after Indian independence to replace the earlier provincial armed constabularies, the SRPF has evolved into one of the most professional state armed police reserves in India. Each operational group functions as a self-sufficient battalion comprising headquarters staff, motor transport wings, signal units, medical squads, and active armed companies. The personnel are trained to operate under extreme psychological and physical stress, capable of mobilizing within hours to any corner of the state.

Joining the SRPF is a prestigious career aspiration for thousands of young candidates across rural and semi-urban Maharashtra. The recruitment framework is designed to select individuals exhibiting exceptional physical endurance, mental fortitude, and disciplined character. Because armed reserve personnel frequently undertake arduous route marches and prolonged field deployments in dense terrain, the selection criteria place rigorous emphasis on physical fitness benchmarks.

Once selected, recruits undergo intensive physical and tactical conditioning at specialized training centers such as Nanvij (Daund). The curriculum incorporates weapon handling (including INSAS rifles, AK-47 assault rifles, and tear-gas launchers), unarmed combat, human rights protocols, disaster relief techniques, and tactical teamwork. SRPF personnel also earn distinction when deployed on deputation to central security bodies such as the National Security Guard (NSG) and Special Protection Group (SPG).

How to Apply for SRPF Armed Police Constable Recruitment in Maharashtra

  1. Verify Educational and Physical Eligibility Standards

    Ensure you have passed the Higher Secondary Certificate (10+2 / 12th standard) examination and meet mandatory physical criteria including minimum height (168 cm for male candidates) and expanded chest measurements.

  2. Submit Online Application on Maharashtra Police Portal

    Register on the official Maharashtra Police recruitment portal (mahapolice.gov.in) during the active recruitment notification and submit your group preferences.

  3. Clear the Rigorous Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

    Participate in mandatory physical fitness assessments, including a 5-kilometer endurance run, 100-meter sprint, and shot put throw to earn essential qualification points.

  4. Qualify in the Comprehensive Written Examination

    Appear for the 100-mark written test evaluating general knowledge, Maharashtra history, mathematics, analytical reasoning, and Marathi language comprehension.

  5. Undergo Medical Examination and Complete Induction Training

    Clear the medical fitness board assessment followed by character verification, then complete nine months of rigorous armed tactical training at an assigned SRPF training center.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of SRPF in Marathi?

The full form of SRPF in Marathi is State Reserve Police Force, phonetically written and officially translated as राज्य राखीव पोलीस बल (Rajya Rakhiv Police Bal).

Q2: What does SRPF stand for in English?

In English, SRPF stands for State Reserve Police Force, the specialized armed reserve constabulary of Maharashtra.

Q3: Under which statutory act was the Maharashtra SRPF created?

It was established under the Bombay State Reserve Police Force Act (SRPF Act) of 1951.

Q4: How many operational groups does the Maharashtra SRPF possess?

Maharashtra SRPF consists of sixteen operational armed groups alongside specialized training institutions positioned across Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Jalna, and Gondia.

Q5: What are the primary operational responsibilities of the SRPF?

SRPF personnel manage riot control, anti-insurgency and anti-Naxalite patrols in Gadchiroli, VIP protection, disaster rescue support, and election bandobast.

Q6: What is the minimum educational qualification for SRPF constable posts?

Candidates must have passed the 12th standard (HSC) or equivalent examination recognized by the state education board.

Q7: Is weapon training provided to SRPF constables during induction?

Yes, SRPF recruits receive extensive tactical training with automatic assault rifles, light machine guns, riot control munitions, and jungle warfare techniques.

Q8: What rank heads the entire State Reserve Police Force in Maharashtra?

The force is headed by an officer of the rank of Additional Director General of Police (ADGP), headquartered in Mumbai.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The State Reserve Police Force stands as an unwavering guardian of peace, democratic stability, and public safety across Maharashtra. Through strict discipline, rigorous tactical training, and selfless commitment, SRPF jawans continuously protect citizens against violent civil unrest, insurgencies, and natural catastrophes. Understanding the proud legacy and functional framework of the SRPF illuminates the courage and professionalism upholding the Maharashtra Police motto, Sadrakshanaya Khalanigrahanaya.
