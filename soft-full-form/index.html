SOFT Full Form: School of Fashion Technology Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

In fashion design education, apparel merchandising, textile technology, and creative arts in India, the full form of SOFT is School of Fashion Technology. Established in 1998 in Pune, Maharashtra, under the aegis of the renowned Maharshi Karve Stree Shikshan Samstha (MKSSS) in collaboration with the Ministry of Textiles, SOFT is an autonomous premier design institute affiliated with SNDT Women's University. Dedicated exclusively to women's education, SOFT offers accredited undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs in fashion design, textile styling, fashion communication, and apparel production management.

The Indian fashion and textile industry represents one of the largest contributors to national employment, manufacturing exports, and cultural expression. As global consumer markets shift toward sustainable textiles, haute couture, and fast-fashion retail, the demand for professionally trained fashion designers, textile stylists, and visual merchandisers has surged. Established to nurture female leadership and creative enterprise in this sector, the School of Fashion Technology (SOFT) in Pune has established itself as one of the country's most respected private design institutions.

Founded in 1998 under the visionary leadership of Maharshi Karve Stree Shikshan Samstha (MKSSS)—a historic charitable educational trust established in 1896 by Bharat Ratna Maharshi Dhondo Keshav Karve—SOFT was born out of an institutional partnership with the National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) and the Ministry of Textiles. Affiliated with Shreemati Nathibai Damodar Thackersey (SNDT) Women's University, SOFT combines classical Indian textile traditions with contemporary global fashion software, runway production techniques, and supply chain logistics.

A key pedagogical pillar of SOFT is its annual graduating fashion show, commonly known as 'Design Showcase'. Graduating students conceptualize, source fabrics for, drape, stitch, and present cohesive collections on a professional runway before panels of leading Indian fashion icons, retail buyers, and fashion house directors. This immersive showcase provides direct networking opportunities, leading to immediate corporate job placements and launchpads for independent fashion entrepreneurship.

How to Secure Admission to the School of Fashion Technology (SOFT) Pune

  1. Verify Academic Eligibility Requirements

    Pass your 10+2 (HSC) board examination in any stream (Arts, Science, or Commerce) with at least 50% marks for undergraduate design courses.

  2. Apply for the SOFT Online Entrance Examination

    Register through the official SOFT Pune portal to take the General Ability Test (GAT) and Studio Test evaluating creative aptitude.

  3. Develop a Creative Design Portfolio

    Assemble an original portfolio of hand sketches, color theory illustrations, fabric swatches, and creative styling concepts for your interview.

  4. Attend Personal Interview and Portfolio Review

    Participate in the personal interview where design faculty evaluate your aesthetic sensibility, material awareness, and motivation.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of SOFT in education?

SOFT stands for School of Fashion Technology, a premier fashion design institute located in Pune, Maharashtra.

Q2: Which university is SOFT Pune affiliated with?

SOFT is affiliated with SNDT Women's University, Mumbai, and collaborates closely with the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.

Q3: Is SOFT Pune exclusively for women?

Yes, established under Maharshi Karve Stree Shikshan Samstha (MKSSS), SOFT is dedicated exclusively to empowering women through design education.

Q4: What courses are offered at SOFT Pune?

Courses include B.Des in Fashion Design, Textile Design, and Fashion Communication, alongside PG diplomas in Fashion Retail and Merchandising.

Q5: How does SOFT compare to NIFT?

While NIFT is a central government autonomous statutory institute, SOFT is a top-ranked specialized private university-affiliated college.

Q6: Does SOFT offer international student exchange programs?

Yes, SOFT collaborates with international design schools in the UK, France, and Italy for student workshops and international exchange.

Q7: What entrance exam is conducted by SOFT?

SOFT conducts its own Online Entrance Test comprising GAT (General Ability Test), Studio Test, and a Personal Interview / Portfolio evaluation.

Q8: Where do SOFT graduates work?

Graduates work as Fashion Designers, Textile Stylists, Visual Merchandisers, Costume Designers for film/TV, and Fashion Buyers for retail brands.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The SOFT (School of Fashion Technology) in Pune is an esteemed design institute dedicated to women's education in fashion, textiles, and design communication. Through its partnership with SNDT Women's University, industry-aligned studio facilities, and focus on creative entrepreneurship, SOFT prepares graduates to excel across global fashion and apparel industries.
