How Much Does a Boob Job Cost in Tennessee?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 15, 2026

Understanding how much does a boob job cost in tennessee requires analyzing regional cosmetic surgery markets, surgeon board credentials, and implant technology. In Tennessee—anchored by major medical hubs in Nashville, Memphis, Knoxville, and Chattanooga—a primary cosmetic breast augmentation typically costs between $6,200 and $10,500 all-inclusive, with the statewide average sitting between $7,400 and $8,800. Saline implants typically cost $6,200 to $7,400, while modern cohesive silicone gel implants range from $7,500 to $9,500.

Tennessee Regional Pricing: Nashville Luxury vs. Memphis & Knoxville

In Tennessee, the aesthetic surgery market is heavily centered in the Nashville metropolitan area, particularly affluent enclaves like Belle Meade, Green Hills, and Brentwood. Known as a vibrant entertainment and healthcare capital, Nashville attracts high-profile plastic surgeons who operate luxury private surgical pavilions, with turnkey breast augmentation packages averaging $7,800 to $9,800.

In contrast, regional markets such as Memphis, Knoxville, Chattanooga, and the Tri-Cities (Johnson City / Kingsport) offer slightly more economical rates ($6,500 to $8,200). These regional centers feature lower commercial facility overhead while maintaining high medical standards through major regional health systems like Vanderbilt Health, UT Medical Center, and Methodist Le Bonheur.

Cost Components: What's Included in a Nashville Quote?

When comparing cosmetic surgery estimates in Tennessee, patients must ensure they are receiving an all-inclusive 'turnkey' quote rather than an incomplete surgeon fee. In a professional plastic surgery clinic, the total fee covers four primary pillars: the surgeon's professional fee ($4,000 to $6,000), the cost of the sterile FDA-approved implant devices ($1,000 to $2,200), the accredited surgical facility fee ($1,200 to $2,000), and the board-certified anesthesiologist fee ($800 to $1,400).

Reputable Tennessee practices also bundle all preoperative consultations, postoperative medical checkups for 12 months, surgical compression recovery bras, and comprehensive manufacturer implant warranty registrations (such as Mentor Promise or Allergan Natrelle warranties covering up to $3,500 in financial assistance in the rare event of capsular contracture or rupture within 10 years).

The Mommy Makeover Trend in Tennessee

Throughout Tennessee, breast augmentation is frequently paired with other post-pregnancy body contouring procedures as part of a comprehensive 'Mommy Makeover'. Carrying children and breastfeeding causes breast tissue deflation, skin stretching, and abdominal muscle separation (diastasis recti).

By combining a breast augmentation (with or without a breast lift) with a tummy tuck (abdominoplasty) and targeted waist liposuction in a single surgical session, patients undergo anesthesia once and complete a single 3-to-4-week recovery. In Tennessee, combining procedures saves $3,000 to $5,000 in duplicate facility and anesthesia charges, with complete Mommy Makeovers typically ranging from $13,500 to $21,000.

How to Choose a Board-Certified Plastic Surgeon in Tennessee

Follow these five tactical steps to evaluate providers and schedule surgery in Tennessee.

  1. Verify ABPS Certification and Medical Licensure

    Confirm your surgeon is certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery and holds an active Tennessee medical license.

  2. Verify Hospital Operating Privileges

    Ensure your surgeon maintains active hospital privileges at reputable hospitals (like Vanderbilt, Saint Thomas, or Methodist).

  3. Evaluate High-Resolution Before-and-After Case Galleries

    Review photographic galleries of patients with similar height, chest width, and starting breast cup size.

  4. Discuss Incision Placement and Pocket Location

    Decide between inframammary (crease) or periareolar incisions and submuscular (under the chest muscle) implant placement.

  5. Confirm Financing and Low-Interest Payment Plans

    Review financing options through CareCredit, Alphaeon, or PatientFi to establish affordable monthly installment plans.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: How much does a boob job cost on average in Tennessee?

The average all-inclusive cost ranges between $7,400 and $8,800 for silicone implants in Tennessee.

Q2: How much does a boob job cost in Nashville?

In Nashville and Brentwood, breast augmentation typically ranges between $7,800 and $9,800 due to premier boutique clinics.

Q3: Is saline or silicone more popular in Tennessee?

Silicone gel is chosen by over 80% of Tennessee patients due to its softer, more natural appearance and lower rippling risk.

Q4: Does health insurance cover breast augmentation in Tennessee?

No, cosmetic breast enlargement is elective and not covered; cancer reconstructive surgery is mandated by federal law.

Q5: How long is recovery after breast augmentation in Tennessee?

Most patients take 5 to 7 days off work, walk lightly immediately, and return to full workouts after 6 to 8 weeks.

Q6: How much does a breast lift with implants cost in Tennessee?

Combining a breast lift with implants typically costs between $11,000 and $15,500 depending on surgical complexity.

Q7: What is submuscular vs subglandular placement?

Submuscular places the implant under the pectoral chest muscle (best natural padding); subglandular sits on top of the muscle.

Q8: Can breast implants cause back pain?

Excessively large implants can strain back muscles; experienced surgeons recommend proportionate sizes (250cc to 450cc).
