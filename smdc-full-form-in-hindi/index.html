SMDC Full Form in Hindi: विद्यालय प्रबंधन समिति

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 18, 2026

The full form of SMDC in Hindi is School Management and Development Committee, translated and pronounced in Hindi as "Vidyalaya Prabandhan Evam Vikas Samiti" (विद्यालय प्रबंधन एवं विकास समिति). Formally constituted under the Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan (RMSA) and integrated into Samagra Shiksha, the SMDC is the statutory governing committee responsible for managing development, infrastructure, academic improvement, and budgetary expenditures in secondary and senior secondary schools (Classes 9 to 12).

SMDC Ka Full Form Kya Hota Hai? (Understanding SMDC in Hindi)

The acronym SMDC stands for School Management and Development Committee, officially known in Hindi as "Vidyalaya Prabandhan Evam Vikas Samiti" (विद्यालय प्रबंधन एवं विकास समिति). While elementary schools (Classes 1 to 8) operate School Management Committees (SMC) under Section 21 of the Right to Education (RTE) Act, secondary and senior secondary schools (Classes 9 to 12) require specialized oversight tailored to adolescent learning, board examinations, laboratory science infrastructure, and vocational career streaming. To bridge this governance requirement, the Ministry of Education constituted the SMDC under the Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan (now part of the unified Samagra Shiksha scheme).

The SMDC operates as a statutory decentralized governing council embedded within every government and government-aided secondary school. By bringing together school principals, senior teachers, local community parents, panchayat or municipal ward leaders, science experts, and educational administrators, the SMDC ensures that public funds allocated for high school laboratories, libraries, computer rooms, and teacher training are utilized with complete financial integrity and transparency.

Structural Composition and Membership Hierarchy of an SMDC

Guidelines formulated by the central and state governments mandate a balanced, multi-stakeholder composition for the School Management and Development Committee to ensure inclusive democratic participation. An SMDC typically comprises 15 to 20 members headed by the school Principal as Chairperson.

Key membership mandates include active parent representation (including parents of SC, ST, OBC, and minority students), elected local body representatives (Gram Pradhan, Ward Councilor), a local civil society NGO representative, a subject education expert, a woman representative, and the senior-most science/mathematics teacher. This balanced structure ensures that physical construction needs, academic goals, and social equity are given equal priority.

Key Differences Between SMC (Elementary) and SMDC (Secondary)

Educational administrators and teachers must clearly distinguish between an SMC and an SMDC, as both committees fulfill distinct legal mandates within the Indian schooling hierarchy. While the SMC governs elementary schools up to Class 8 under statutory RTE mandates, the SMDC oversees secondary and senior secondary education up to Class 12 under Samagra Shiksha frameworks.

The SMDC operates with broader financial and academic authority. It manages larger composite school development grants, oversees specialized physics, chemistry, biology, and computer laboratories, coordinates vocational skill workshops, and formulates strategic plans to maximize student performance in Class 10 and Class 12 state and central board examinations.

Financial Management and Sub-Committees of SMDC

To execute its mandate effectively, the SMDC establishes two specialized statutory sub-committees: the School Building and Civil Works Sub-Committee, and the Academic and Financial Audit Sub-Committee.

The Civil Works Sub-Committee inspects classroom repairs, laboratory construction, solar panel installations, and boundary fencing, verifying that contractors adhere to quality specifications before funds are disbursed. The Financial Audit Sub-Committee reviews recurring expenditures from the annual Composite School Grant (typically ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000+ depending on student enrollment), ensuring that science chemicals, library books, and sports gear are purchased following transparent procurement rules.

How an SMDC Prepares and Executes an Annual School Budget

A step-by-step practical procedural manual for an SMDC managing annual development funds under Samagra Shiksha.

  1. Convene First Quarter SMDC Planning Meeting

    The Principal issues written agendas convening all parent, teacher, and local panchayat members to evaluate school needs.

  2. Audit Laboratory, IT, and Infrastructure Requirements

    Inspect science laboratory glassware, computer systems, sports equipment, and identify mandatory building plastering and roof repairs.

  3. Draft Itemized Annual Development Plan

    Formulate an itemized expenditure budget allocating funds across science equipment, library books, toilet cleaning, and minor civil works.

  4. Pass Formal Unanimous Committee Resolution

    Review the proposed budget with members, pass a formal resolution in the official SMDC ledger, and obtain all members signatures.

  5. Execute Purchases via Joint Account and Display Vouchers

    Disburse funds through the joint SMDC bank account (Principal + Parent representative) and display bills on the public school notice board.

Frequently Asked Questions (7 Questions Answered)

Q1: SMDC ka full form in Hindi kya hota hai?

SMDC ka full form School Management and Development Committee hota hai, jise Hindi me Vidyalaya Prabandhan Evam Vikas Samiti (विद्यालय प्रबंधन एवं विकास समिति) kehte hain.

Q2: What is the primary difference between SMC and SMDC?

SMC governs elementary schools (Classes 1 to 8), while SMDC governs secondary and senior secondary schools (Classes 9 to 12).

Q3: Who serves as the Chairperson of an SMDC?

The Principal or Headmaster of the secondary school serves as the ex-officio Chairperson of the SMDC.

Q4: How many times must the SMDC convene in an academic year?

The SMDC is mandated to convene at least once every month (or a minimum of once every quarter) to review operations.

Q5: Who operates the official bank account of an SMDC?

The bank account is operated jointly by the Principal (Chairperson) and an elected parent representative to prevent unilateral spending.

Q6: Under which government scheme is the SMDC funded?

SMDC activities and annual school composite grants are funded under the Ministry of Education unified Samagra Shiksha scheme.

Q7: What is the role of parents in the SMDC?

Parents audit school accounts, voice student learning needs, inspect mid-day meals and campus safety, and vote on budget allocations.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The full form of SMDC in Hindi is School Management and Development Committee (विद्यालय प्रबंधन एवं विकास समिति - Vidyalaya Prabandhan Evam Vikas Samiti). By bridging school leadership with community parents, local panchayat representatives, and academic experts, the SMDC democratizes high school governance. Active, transparent SMDCs ensure that secondary schools possess the infrastructure, laboratory tools, and supportive learning environment needed to help students excel in board examinations and build successful careers.
