ASO Full Form in Marathi: Assistant Section Officer

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 18, 2026

The full form of ASO in Marathi is Assistant Section Officer, written and phonetically pronounced in Marathi as 'Assistant Section Officer' (सहाय्यक कक्ष अधिकारी). In government administration, an ASO is a vital Class-II gazetted or non-gazetted executive administrative officer appointed in the state government secretariat (Mantralaya) through the Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) competitive examinations.

Understanding ASO: Assistant Section Officer

State government administration in Maharashtra operates through the apex central secretariat known as Mantralaya in Mumbai. Efficient governance, policy drafting, and statutory file processing require diligent administrative officers capable of analyzing legal documentation and executive directives. The Assistant Section Officer, known in Marathi as 'Sahayyak Kaksh Adhikari' (सहाय्यक कक्ष अधिकारी), represents one of the most prestigious and sought-after government positions recruited through the Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC).

Within the administrative hierarchy of Mantralaya and associated state commissions, the ASO acts as the core analytical anchor of a department section. Whether working in Home, Urban Development, Finance, or Revenue departments, the ASO examines government resolutions, legal court petitions, legislative assembly questions, and public grievances, preparing detailed notes and draft notifications for review by Section Officers and Under Secretaries.

Operational Workflow and Process Stages

The MPSC selection process for the ASO post is notoriously competitive, attracting hundreds of thousands of graduate aspirants across Maharashtra every year. Candidates must demonstrate deep mastery across Marathi language grammar, English comprehension, general studies, Indian polity, and the Constitution of India:

Examination Stage Subject Composition Marks & Question Format
Preliminary Exam (पूर्व परीक्षा) General Studies, Current Affairs, Mental Ability, History, Geography 100 Questions / 100 Marks (Objective MCQ format)
Mains Paper 1 (मुख्य परीक्षा पेपर १) Marathi & English Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension 100 Questions / 200 Marks (Objective format)
Mains Paper 2 (मुख्य परीक्षा पेपर २) Indian Constitution, Political System, RTI Act, Maharashtra Governance 100 Questions / 200 Marks (In-depth polity evaluation)
Final Merit List (अंतिम गुणवत्ता यादी) Aggregated score from Mains Paper 1 and Paper 2 Total 400 Marks (No physical interview for ASO post)

Once appointed, an ASO undergoes specialized administrative training at the Yashwantrao Chavan Academy of Development Administration (YASHADA) in Pune, mastering the Maharashtra Civil Services Rules, budget manual processing, and digital e-Office administrative software.

How to Prepare for and Clear the MPSC ASO Examination in Maharashtra

Follow this systematic preparation strategy to clear the preliminary and mains exams for the Assistant Section Officer post under MPSC.

  1. Master the Official MPSC Syllabus and Exam Pattern

    Download the updated syllabus for the Maharashtra Non-Gazetted Group B Combined Examination from the official MPSC website.

  2. Build Strong Foundations in Indian Polity and Constitution

    Study core political science textbooks and the Constitution of India thoroughly, as polity carries the highest weighting in ASO Mains Paper 2.

  3. Strengthen Marathi and English Language Grammar

    Practice Marathi grammar rules (मराठी व्याकरण) and English vocabulary daily to score high marks on the mandatory language Mains Paper 1.

  4. Solve Past 5 Years MPSC Question Papers

    Analyze previous year question papers to understand question patterns, high-weightage topics, and negative marking dynamics.

  5. Attempt Timed Mock Tests and Practice Speed Accuracy

    Enlist in full-length test series to master time management, aiming to solve 100 questions within the strict 60-minute preliminary limit.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of ASO in Marathi?

The full form of ASO in Marathi is Assistant Section Officer, known as सहाय्यक कक्ष अधिकारी (Sahayyak Kaksh Adhikari).

Q2: Which exam recruits ASO officers in Maharashtra?

Recruitment is conducted through the MPSC Maharashtra Non-Gazetted Group B Subordinate Services Combined Examination.

Q3: Where is an ASO posted in Maharashtra?

An ASO is predominantly posted in the state government secretariat (Mantralaya) in Mumbai or the MPSC office.

Q4: Is there an interview for the MPSC ASO post?

No, selection for the ASO post is determined strictly by the aggregate marks scored in the 400-mark Mains examination.

Q5: What is the starting salary of an ASO in Mantralaya?

Under 7th Pay Matrix S-14, the starting in-hand salary ranges approximately between Rs. 55,000 and Rs. 65,000 per month including allowances.

Q6: What is the minimum educational qualification for ASO?

A candidate must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized statutory university.

Q7: What is the promotional ladder after becoming an ASO?

An ASO is promoted to Section Officer (कक्ष अधिकारी), then Under Secretary (अवर सचिव), and Deputy Secretary (उप सचिव).

Q8: Where do newly recruited ASOs receive administrative training?

They receive specialized induction training at YASHADA (Yashwantrao Chavan Academy of Development Administration) in Pune.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

The Assistant Section Officer (ASO / सहाय्यक कक्ष अधिकारी) is a foundational executive pillar of Maharashtra's state administrative governance. Operating at the heart of state policymaking in Mantralaya, an ASO enjoys immense respect, job stability, and clear promotional paths toward senior executive leadership in the state civil services.
