Asbestos Trust Fund List

Informational
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 14, 2026

The asbestos trust fund list represents a comprehensive catalog of federally approved bankruptcy trust entities established under Section 524(g) of the United States Bankruptcy Code. When dozens of major American manufacturing, insulation, and chemical corporations faced billions of dollars in catastrophic asbestos liabilities, federal courts allowed them to reorganize under Chapter 11 on the condition that they fund dedicated, irrevocable trusts to compensate future and present victims. Today, more than sixty active asbestos bankruptcy trusts hold over thirty billion dollars in assets, providing vital financial relief to individuals diagnosed with mesothelioma, lung cancer, and asbestosis without requiring a traditional jury trial.

Statutory Origin: Section 524(g) and Trust Distribution Procedures

The legal framework governing modern asbestos bankruptcy trusts originated with the landmark reorganization of the Johns-Manville Corporation in 1988, which was codified into federal statutory law under Section 524(g) of the Bankruptcy Reform Act of 1994. Under Section 524(g), a bankrupt debtor involved in asbestos litigation can channel all current and future personal injury claims to an independent trust funded with corporate cash, equity, and insurance proceeds. In return, the court issues a permanent channeling injunction that shields the reorganized corporate entity from any further direct tort litigation.

Each asbestos trust operates under court-approved Trust Distribution Procedures (TDP). The TDP establishes standardized disease levels, medical diagnostic criteria, occupational exposure standards, and scheduled settlement values for eight distinct disease classifications (ranging from bilateral pleural disease up to malignant mesothelioma). Because trusts must ensure that sufficient funds remain available to pay future claimants decades into the future, each trust sets a specific 'Payment Percentage' that determines what fraction of a claim full scheduled value is paid in cash.

Major Active Asbestos Trusts and Corporate Histories

The master asbestos trust fund list encompasses the corporate giants that dominated twentieth-century manufacturing, construction, and naval shipbuilding. The Johns-Manville Personal Injury Settlement Trust, established in 1988, was the first and remains one of the largest, having resolved hundreds of thousands of claims. Other massive funds include the Owens Corning / Fibreboard Asbestos Personal Injury Trust, which manages billions from two merged insulation powerhouses, and the Babcock & Wilcox Company Asbestos PI Settlement Trust, which compensated tens of thousands of boilermakers and power plant workers.

Additional prominent trusts include the Armstrong World Industries Asbestos Trust (covering vinyl asbestos tile and ceiling products), the W.R. Grace & Co. Asbestos Trust (covering Zonolite attic insulation and fireproofing), the Celotex Asbestos Settlement Trust, the USG Asbestos Personal Injury Trust, and the Garlock Sealing Technologies LLC Trust. Each trust maintains its own independent trustees, claims processing facility, and specific product identification matrix cross-referencing thousands of industrial, commercial, and military worksites across the nation.

Filing Procedures: Expedited vs. Individual Review and Maximizing Recovery

When filing claims against the asbestos trust fund list, claimants can choose between two primary evaluation tracks: Expedited Review and Individual Review. Expedited Review provides a fast-track, fixed-dollar payout based on standardized criteria. If a claimant submits certified pathology confirming mesothelioma and verified employment records matching the trust approved exposure sites, the claim is approved quickly without subjective negotiation. This route delivers rapid liquidity, typically paying within three to six months.

Alternatively, Individual Review allows claimants with extraordinary circumstances—such as very young age at diagnosis, high economic wage losses, severe dependency obligations, or unique exposure histories—to seek higher financial compensation than the standard scheduled amount. Skilled asbestos litigation attorneys routinely file across fifteen to thirty separate trusts simultaneously on behalf of a single injured client, aggregating trust payouts to secure hundreds of thousands, or even millions, of dollars in combined compensation.

How to File Claims Against the Asbestos Trust Fund List

Follow these five strategic steps to identify qualifying trusts, prepare documentation, and submit successful asbestos trust claims.

  1. Obtain Certified Medical Pathology and Radiology

    Secure official pathology reports, biopsy slides, and B-reader chest X-rays establishing an eligible asbestos-related disease.

  2. Reconstruct Detailed Occupational and Military History

    Compile complete employment records, union dispatch slips, DD-214 military records, and co-worker witness affidavits.

  3. Cross-Reference Job Sites with Trust Product Matrices

    Match your historic worksites, equipment models, and dates of work against each trust approved site and product database.

  4. Select Optimal Review Track (Expedited vs Individual)

    Evaluate with your legal counsel whether to pursue rapid Expedited Review or higher-value Individual Review for each trust.

  5. Submit Electronic Claims and Monitor Distributions

    Have your attorney submit verified claim packets through electronic trust portals and manage cash distributions.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is an asbestos bankruptcy trust fund?

An asbestos trust fund is an irrevocable trust established under Section 524(g) of the US Bankruptcy Code by a bankrupt company to pay present and future asbestos personal injury claims.

Q2: How much money is available in asbestos trust funds?

There are more than sixty active asbestos bankruptcy trusts holding an estimated thirty billion dollars in collective assets to pay qualified victims.

Q3: What is an asbestos trust payment percentage?

Because trusts must preserve funds to compensate future claimants for decades, they pay a set percentage (ranging from 1% to over 50%) of each claim scheduled value.

Q4: Can I file claims with multiple asbestos trusts at the same time?

Yes, most asbestos victims worked around products made by dozens of different companies and routinely file and collect from fifteen to thirty separate trusts simultaneously.

Q5: Do I have to go to court to receive asbestos trust fund money?

No, asbestos trust claims are completely administrative and handled out of court without judge, jury, or courtroom appearances.

Q6: How long does it take to receive payment from an asbestos trust?

Claims filed under Expedited Review with complete medical and exposure records are typically processed and paid within three to six months.

Q7: Can family members claim from asbestos trusts after a loved one has died?

Yes, surviving spouses and eligible heirs can file wrongful death claims with asbestos trusts on behalf of a deceased family member.

Q8: Can I file a lawsuit and trust claims at the same time?

Yes, you can file administrative claims with bankruptcy trusts while concurrently pursuing civil lawsuits against solvent, non-bankrupt companies in court.
