How to Get a Tint Exemption in NY?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

Navigating New York State's strict automotive window tint laws can be challenging, especially for drivers with severe medical conditions requiring shielding from sunlight. Under Section 375(12-a) of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law, vehicles registered in the Empire State must permit at least seventy percent of visible light to pass through the windshield and front side windows, a metric strictly tested during annual state safety inspections. However, the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles provides a formal medical exemption process via Form MV-80W for individuals suffering from diagnosed photosensitive and photophobic health conditions.

New York State Tint Regulations and Statutory Medical Exemptions

In 2017, New York enacted aggressive updates to its Vehicle and Traffic Law, mandating that state safety inspection stations test window light transmittance using calibrated optical tint meters. Under standard regulations, both the windshield and front side windows (driver and front passenger) must transmit a minimum of seventy percent of exterior visible light (70% VLT). Rear side windows on sedans must also meet the seventy percent threshold, whereas passenger SUVs, vans, and pickup trucks are permitted darker rear glass provided they are equipped with dual exterior side mirrors.

To accommodate motorists who experience severe adverse physical reactions to ultraviolet radiation and ambient sunlight, New York Public Health Law Section 375(12-a)(c) authorizes the Commissioner of Motor Vehicles to grant official medical exemptions. An approved exemption allows the registered owner to apply darker aftermarket window films to the front side windows and windshield without failing state safety inspections or receiving traffic citations from law enforcement officers.

Qualifying Conditions and DMV Form MV-80W Protocol

The New York Department of Motor Vehicles does not grant tint exemptions for cosmetic preferences, cabin cooling, or mild sun sensitivity. Under the guidelines established by the New York Department of Health, medical exemptions are reserved strictly for diagnosed conditions characterized by severe photosensitivity, cutaneous porphyria, or dermatological and ocular disorders exacerbated by direct solar radiation. Applications are submitted using Form MV-80W, officially titled the Application for Tinted Window Exemption.

A licensed physician (MD or DO) or physician assistant must complete the medical certification portion of Form MV-80W. The medical provider must document the patient's specific clinical diagnosis, verify that the condition requires protection from direct sunlight, and certify that darker window tinting is medically necessary for the individual's health and mobility. The form is then mailed directly to the NY DMV Vehicle Safety Services department in Albany for administrative review.

Inspection Compliance, Exemption Stickers, and Renewals

Once the New York DMV approves Form MV-80W, they issue an official Tinted Window Exemption Certificate and a customized medical exemption sticker. The vehicle owner must affix this tamper-evident sticker to the lower corner of the driver's side front window or windshield according to DMV instructions. In addition, the vehicle registration record in the statewide DMV database is updated to reflect the approved medical exemption status.

During your annual New York state safety inspection, the technician will scan the vehicle identification number and examine the sticker. The presence of the official exemption authorizes the inspector to enter an exemption override code into the computerized New York State Vehicle Inspection System (NYVIP), allowing the vehicle to pass inspection despite window readings registering below seventy percent light transmission. It is imperative to retain the physical paper exemption certificate in your vehicle glove compartment alongside your registration card to present to state troopers or local police officers during traffic encounters.

How to Apply for a New York Tint Exemption

Step-by-step instructions for completing Form MV-80W, obtaining physician certification, and receiving an official NY DMV tint exemption sticker.

  1. Download and Print NY DMV Form MV-80W

    Obtain the official Application for Tinted Window Exemption (Form MV-80W) directly from the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles official website.

  2. Schedule a Medical Appointment with Your Treating Physician

    Consult with your dermatologist, rheumatologist, or general physician to evaluate your medical diagnosis against New York Department of Health photosensitivity criteria.

  3. Have Your Physician Complete the Medical Attestation Section

    Ensure your licensed medical provider fills out Section 2, certifying your specific medical condition, medical license number, and clinical necessity for dark window film.

  4. Submit the Completed Form to DMV Vehicle Safety in Albany

    Mail the original completed application along with copies of your current New York vehicle registration to the NY DMV Medical Review Unit in Albany.

  5. Affix the Official Exemption Sticker to Your Vehicle Glass

    Upon receiving your official DMV exemption approval letter and security sticker, mount the sticker to your vehicle window as instructed and keep the certificate in your glovebox.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Does New York allow window tint exemptions for migraines?

Generally no. The New York Department of Health guidelines specifically limit exemptions to severe photosensitive dermatological and genetic disorders rather than chronic migraine headaches.

Q2: How long does it take for NY DMV to approve Form MV-80W?

Processing times typically range from four to eight weeks from the date the DMV Medical Review Unit in Albany receives your completed application.

Q3: Does a NY tint exemption need to be renewed every year?

Medical exemptions for permanent chronic conditions are typically issued for multi-year periods, but must be transferred if you sell the vehicle or change registrations.

Q4: Can an out-of-state driver use their home state tint exemption in New York?

New York law generally recognizes legal window tint from the vehicle state of registration, but commercial or permanent residents must comply with New York titling standards.

Q5: How much does a New York medical tint exemption cost?

The New York DMV does not charge an application fee for processing Form MV-80W, though you will incur standard doctor consultation fees for the medical examination.

Q6: Will my car fail NY state inspection if the tint is dark without an exemption?

Yes. State inspection stations are mandated by law to test windows with calibrated light meters, and failing the 70 percent VLT threshold results in immediate inspection failure.

Q7: Can I tint my front windshield completely dark with an exemption?

Even with an exemption, total blackout tint on the entire windshield is generally restricted for driver visibility; clear UV-blocking or moderate light tint films are typical.

Q8: What should I do if a police officer pulls me over for dark tint in NY?

Politely present your driver license, vehicle registration, and the official DMV Tinted Window Exemption Certificate while pointing out the affixed window decal.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Securing a medical tint exemption in New York State provides essential health protection for individuals suffering from severe photosensitivity disorders while ensuring full compliance with state traffic safety mandates. By partnering with your treating physician, thoroughly completing Form MV-80W, and displaying your state-issued exemption sticker, you safeguard your health and eliminate the risk of failed vehicle inspections and costly municipal traffic citations.
