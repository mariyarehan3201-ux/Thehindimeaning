CA Full Form in Marathi: Chartered Accountant Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 17, 2026

The full form of CA in Marathi is चार्टर्ड अकाउंटंट (सनदी लेखापाल). In English, CA stands for Chartered Accountant, which is an internationally esteemed professional qualification awarded by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Established under the statutory provisions of the Chartered Accountants Act of 1949, ICAI regulates the chartered accountancy profession nationwide. A Chartered Accountant possesses exclusive legal authority to audit financial statements of corporate enterprises, certify financial health, ensure statutory compliance with goods and services tax (GST) laws, oversee corporate taxation strategies, and provide strategic advisory services to corporate conglomerates and public institutions alike.

The integrity of modern industrial commerce, corporate reporting, and statutory taxation depends directly on the expertise and ethical rigor of Chartered Accountants. Regulated by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)—the second largest professional accounting body in the world—the Chartered Accountancy designation represents one of the most intellectually challenging and prestigious professional qualifications in India. Across commercial metropolises like Mumbai and Pune in Maharashtra, CAs serve as trusted business advisors, auditing corporate balance sheets, ensuring tax compliance, and steering corporate restructuring.

Unlike conventional university degrees that focus primarily on classroom lectures, the CA qualification model uniquely blends academic testing with intensive real-world apprenticeship. The mandatory practical articleship training equips students with hands-on experience in auditing manufacturing plants, evaluating internal financial controls, resolving corporate tax disputes, and preparing complex tax filings. This rigorous exposure ensures that by the time a candidate clears the CA Final exams, they possess polished executive competencies, high ethical standards, and mature business acumen.

Navigating the CA journey requires passing three distinct qualifying stages established under ICAI's New Scheme of Education and Training. The table below outlines these examination tiers, study subjects, and passing criteria.

A defining privilege of a qualified Chartered Accountant is the exclusive statutory mandate granted under the Companies Act of 2013 to perform statutory audits of private and public limited companies. Without an audit report signed by an active ICAI member holding a Certificate of Practice (CoP), corporate financial disclosures cannot be filed with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). This legal responsibility positions CAs as the ultimate guardians of shareholder interests, financial transparency, and public economic trust.

With India rapidly expanding its digital economy, goods and services tax (GST) framework, and international business footprint, the demand for Chartered Accountants continues to accelerate. By maintaining unyielding ethical standards and staying abreast of evolving accounting frameworks like Ind AS and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), CAs remain indispensable architects of sustainable corporate growth.

How to Become a Chartered Accountant (CA) Through ICAI in India

  1. Register for and Clear CA Foundation Examination

    Enroll with ICAI after passing Class 10 and appear for the CA Foundation exam after completing your Class 12 (HSC) board examination, passing all four core papers.

  2. Clear CA Intermediate Examination (Both Groups)

    Undergo eight months of intensive study and pass both groups of the CA Intermediate exam covering financial reporting, corporate law, taxation, and advanced accounting.

  3. Complete Mandatory Articleship Training and ICITSS

    Complete practical ICITSS computer and soft-skill courses followed by two years of mandatory practical articleship training under a practicing Chartered Accountant.

  4. Pass CA Final Examination and Obtain ICAI Membership

    Appear for and pass the rigorous CA Final examinations across both groups, submit your membership application, and receive your designated Fellow/Associate ICAI credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is the full form of CA in Marathi?

The full form of CA in Marathi is चार्टर्ड अकाउंटंट (सनदी लेखापाल), signifying a certified accounting and auditing professional.

Q2: Which institute awards the CA qualification in India?

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), a statutory body established by an Act of Parliament in 1949, regulates and awards the CA qualification.

Q3: Can students from arts or science streams pursue CA?

Yes, students from any academic stream (Arts, Science, or Commerce) who have passed Class 12 can register for the CA Foundation exam.

Q4: What is the direct entry route for CA Intermediate?

Commerce graduates with at least 55% marks, or non-commerce graduates with at least 60% marks, can bypass CA Foundation and enroll directly into CA Intermediate.

Q5: What is the duration of practical articleship training?

Under the revised ICAI New Scheme of Education and Training, practical articleship training is completed over a focused duration of two years.

Q6: What exclusive authority does a Chartered Accountant hold?

Under the Companies Act of 2013, only a qualified Chartered Accountant holding an active Certificate of Practice (CoP) can conduct statutory audits of Indian companies.

Q7: Is there an age limit to register for the CA course?

No, ICAI sets no upper age limit for enrolling in or attempting the Chartered Accountancy examinations.

Q8: What are the core career options for a qualified CA?

CAs can set up an independent auditing and tax consulting firm, join Big Four audit networks, or serve as Chief Financial Officers (CFOs) and financial controllers in corporations.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Becoming a Chartered Accountant is a transformative journey that demands intellectual discipline, perseverance, and ethical dedication. With exclusive statutory audit rights and versatile career opportunities spanning corporate finance, taxation, and executive consulting, the CA qualification remains one of the most prestigious and empowering professional milestones in India.
