GKP Ka Full Form: Gorakhpur Station & City Guide

Full Form
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 18, 2026

The Hindi meaning and regional designation of GKP ka full form stands for 'गोरखपुर' (Gorakhpur), most prominently recognized as the official Indian Railways alphanumeric station code for Gorakhpur Junction (GKP). Situated in eastern Uttar Pradesh, Gorakhpur Junction serves as the operational headquarters of the North Eastern Railway (NER) zone and is globally famous for housing the world's second-longest railway platform (measuring approximately 1,366.33 meters).

The Heritage and Global Stature of Gorakhpur (GKP)

In Indian railway scheduling, travel booking systems, and regional geography, three-letter station codes serve as universal navigational anchors. Among the most widely searched transportation identifiers across northern and eastern India is GKP. In public awareness and Indian Railways terminology, GKP ka full form stands for Gorakhpur (गोरखपुर)—specifically representing Gorakhpur Junction railway station, the operational headquarters of the North Eastern Railway (NER) zone.

Named after the revered ancient yogic saint Guru Gorakhnath, the city of Gorakhpur is a profound cultural, spiritual, educational, and transportation nexus in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Historically celebrated as the cradle of the ancient Nath monastic tradition and home to Gita Press—the world's largest publisher of sacred Indian classical texts—Gorakhpur today serves as a bustling industrial metropolis and the prime railway gateway connecting northern India with the Himalayan nation of Nepal.

The Engineering Feat: The World-Class Long Railway Platform

For over a decade, Gorakhpur Junction held the undisputed Guinness World Record for the longest railway platform on the planet, before the recent inauguration of the Hubballi platform in Karnataka. The continuous passenger platform spanning Platforms 1 and 2 at Gorakhpur measures an astounding 1,366.33 meters (4,482.7 feet).

Strategic Significance: The International Gateway to Nepal and Buddhism

Gorakhpur's geographic location confers exceptional strategic and touristic importance. It functions as the ultimate railhead for travelers, pilgrims, and commercial traders entering the neighboring nation of Nepal.

Modern Passenger Amenities and Smart Station Upgrades

Under the national Amrit Bharat Station Scheme, Gorakhpur Junction is undergoing extensive modernization. The historic heritage facade is being enhanced with multi-tier concourses, airport-style segregated arrival and departure lounges, multi-level vehicle parking facilities, and automated escalators connecting every platform face.

With high-speed Vande Bharat Express services operating directly between Gorakhpur, Lucknow, and Prayagraj, GKP continues to reinforce its stature as a premier transportation and economic hub of eastern India.

How Travelers Navigate Gorakhpur Junction (GKP) in 5 Practical Steps

  1. Verify Station Code (GKP) on IRCTC or Reservation Portals

    When booking train journeys, enter 'GKP' to select Gorakhpur Junction, verifying your scheduled train arrival and departure times.

  2. Access the Station via North or South Terminal Gates

    Enter through the primary heritage facade or the modernized southern satellite gate, utilizing automated escalator bridges to reach overhead walkways.

  3. Locate Your Train on the Record-Long Platform Grid

    Check digital passenger display boards to confirm whether your train is berthed along Platform 1 or 2 (which share the iconic 1,366-meter continuous platform span).

  4. Access Passenger Waiting Lounges and Food Courts

    Utilize air-conditioned executive lounges, IRCTC Jan Aahaar cafeterias, clean cloakrooms, and free high-speed railway Wi-Fi networks.

  5. Connect to Local Transit and Nepal Border Roadways

    Exit toward the pre-paid auto stand, city bus terminus, or private taxi stands connecting directly to Gorakhnath Temple, Kushinagar, and the Sonauli Nepal border.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: GKP ka full form kya hai?

GKP ka full form 'Gorakhpur' (गोरखपुर) hota hai, jo Indian Railways ka official railway station code hai.

Q2: Where is Gorakhpur Junction (GKP) located?

It is located in the city of Gorakhpur in eastern Uttar Pradesh, India, serving as the headquarters of the North Eastern Railway (NER).

Q3: Why is Gorakhpur Junction internationally famous?

It is renowned for having one of the longest railway platforms in the world, measuring 1,366.33 meters (4,482.7 feet) in length.

Q4: What major tourist circuits are connected via GKP railway station?

GKP is the primary gateway to the international Buddhist Circuit (Kushinagar, Lumbini) and the main transit route to Nepal via Sonauli.

Q5: What spiritual and publishing institutions are in Gorakhpur?

The world-renowned Gorakhnath Temple (seat of the Nath monastic tradition) and Gita Press (largest publisher of Hindu religious texts).

Q6: How many platforms does Gorakhpur Junction have?

Gorakhpur Junction features 10 operational passenger platforms accommodating hundreds of express, superfast, and passenger trains daily.

Q7: Is Gorakhpur Junction fully electrified?

Yes, the entire rail network radiating from Gorakhpur Junction is 100% energized with 25kV AC overhead electrification (OHE).

Q8: What major trains originate from GKP station?

Major trains include the Gorakhpur - New Delhi Vande Bharat Express, Gorakhdham Superfast Express, Humsafar Express, and Raptisagar Express.
