How Much Is a Driving Test in Texas?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 16, 2026

If you are preparing to secure your Texas driver license, understanding how much is a driving test in Texas depends on whether you test through a state Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) Mega Center or an authorized third-party driving school. Taking your behind-the-wheel road test directly at a Texas DPS driver license office is included in the standard $33 driver license application fee ($16 for applicants under 18), allowing up to three examination attempts within a 90-day window. However, because DPS appointments routinely suffer from backlogs of two to three months, many applicants choose state-certified Third Party Skills Testing (TPST) schools, which charge between $75 and $150 for same-week road testing.

Texas DPS Office Testing Versus Third-Party TPST Providers

The Texas Department of Public Safety oversees all non-commercial Class C driving assessments statewide. When you schedule an appointment directly with a DPS driver license office, you pay no standalone road examination fee beyond the statutory application processing charge. For adults aged 18 to 84, this license fee is $33 and covers your vision screening, written knowledge evaluation, driving skills assessment, and the issuance of your physical plastic license valid for up to eight years. For teens aged 16 and 17, the application fee is $16. If you fail your initial DPS test, your application fee grants you two additional testing opportunities within 90 days before you must repay and reapply.

The primary drawback of testing directly through Texas DPS is appointment scarcity, with major metropolitan centers like Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, and San Antonio booking out eight to twelve weeks in advance. To alleviate this bottleneck, the state created the Third Party Skills Testing (TPST) program, enabling licensed commercial driving schools to administer official state road tests. TPST providers charge private service fees ranging from $75 to $150 for adult and teen road tests, frequently providing same-day or weekend testing slots. Upon passing at an authorized TPST facility, the school provides a sealed envelope with your passing score sheet to submit to DPS for rapid license issuance.

Vehicle Inspection, Insurance, and Testing Prerequisites

Regardless of whether you test at a state DPS office or a private TPST driving school, your testing vehicle must satisfy rigorous mechanical safety standards. Before the examiner enters the passenger cabin, they will inspect the vehicle to verify working headlights, turn signals, brake lights, horn, windshield wipers, tires with adequate tread depth, functional seatbelts, and unobstructed view through all windows. Most crucially, the vehicle must display two valid license plates (front and rear) and present proof of current Texas financial responsibility (auto liability insurance listing the vehicle identification number).

Administrative prerequisites also carry minor financial obligations. Prior to testing, all applicants aged 18 through 24 must complete an approved six-hour Adult Driver Education course, which typically costs $30 to $60 online. Furthermore, every applicant must watch the state-mandated Impact Texas Drivers (ITD) video course—either Impact Texas Teen Drivers (ITTD) or Impact Texas Adult Drivers (ITAD)—which is free on the DPS website but produces a mandatory completion certificate that expires after 90 days. Testing candidates must also furnish proof of Texas residency and lawful presence.

Retest Policies and Common Texas Examination Pitfalls

Failing the Texas driving test requires careful attention to retest guidelines to avoid forfeiting application fees. If you test at a DPS facility and score below the 70 percent passing threshold, the examiner will review your critical deduction areas, such as failing to signal 100 feet prior to a turn, rolling through a stop sign, or improper parallel parking. You can rebook a second or third attempt without paying another $33 fee, but booking subsequent slots often incurs long calendar delays. If all three attempts are exhausted or the 90-day window expires, you must pay a fresh $33 fee.

Third-party TPST driving schools offer much faster retesting turnaround. While private schools charge an administrative retest fee typically ranging between $35 and $60, many schools allow re-examination within 24 to 48 hours. Many TPST locations also offer a 30-minute pre-test warm-up drive where an instructor coaches you on the exact neighborhood maneuvers, parallel parking dimensions, and reversing drills tested on the official route, significantly increasing first-time pass rates.

How to Book and Pass Your Texas Driving Road Test

Follow these five steps to prepare your paperwork, pass your vehicle inspection, and clear the Texas road exam.

  1. Complete Required Educational Courses

    Finish your teen or adult driver education course and view the mandatory Impact Texas Drivers (ITD) video within 90 days of testing.

  2. Choose Between DPS and TPST Testing

    Decide whether to wait for a free appointment slot at a Texas DPS Mega Center or pay $75 to $150 for rapid testing at a TPST driving school.

  3. Inspect the Test Vehicle Thoroughly

    Check front and rear license plates, functional exterior lighting, tire tread, horn, side mirrors, and active proof of Texas auto insurance.

  4. Execute Critical Behind-the-Wheel Maneuvers

    Demonstrate smooth parallel parking between upright stanchions, straight-line 30-foot backing, full stops at red octagons, and constant mirror checks.

  5. Submit Sealed Results to Texas DPS

    Take your sealed TPST passing envelope or DPS exam sheet to the license counter for thumbprint scanning, vision check, and temporary paper license.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Is the driving test free at Texas DPS offices?

The road test itself has no separate charge at DPS offices because it is included in the standard $33 driver license application fee ($16 for teens).

Q2: How much does a third-party driving test cost in Texas?

Authorized TPST driving schools charge between $75 and $150 for an official Texas road skills examination.

Q3: How many times can you take the driving test at Texas DPS for $33?

Your $33 application fee covers up to three attempts within a 90-day window before you must reapply and repay the fee.

Q4: Can I rent a car from a driving school for the Texas road test?

Yes, most third-party TPST driving schools rent certified testing vehicles equipped with dual brakes for $30 to $65 per exam.

Q5: What is parallel parking spacing for the Texas driving test?

The standard parallel parking space in Texas is approximately 25 feet long and 7 feet wide, bounded by upright flag stanchions.

Q6: How long is the Impact Texas Drivers (ITD) certificate valid?

The Impact Texas Drivers completion certificate is valid for exactly 90 days from the date you complete the free online video modules.

Q7: What score do you need to pass the Texas driving test?

You must achieve a minimum passing score of 70 points out of 100 on the driving skills evaluation rubric.

Q8: Can you take the Texas driving test in your own car?

Yes, provided the vehicle has working signals, valid registration, two license plates, and active Texas liability insurance coverage.
