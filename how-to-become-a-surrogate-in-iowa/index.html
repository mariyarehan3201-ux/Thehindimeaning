Q6: Are refurbished automated external defibrillators (AEDs) safe and reliable?

FDA-compliant refurbished AEDs undergo rigorous biomedical bench testing, software upgrades, new internal battery installations, and factory recertification, providing full life-saving reliability at lower cost.

Semaglutide is clinically formulated and FDA-approved as a once-weekly subcutaneous injection due to its seven-day half-life. Patients should never alter dosing schedules without direct supervision from their prescribing physician.
