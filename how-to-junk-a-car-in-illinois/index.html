How to Junk a Car in Illinois?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 26, 2026

Disposing of an end-of-life, damaged, or inoperable vehicle in Illinois requires navigating specific statutory procedures mandated by the Illinois Secretary of State (IL SOS). Whether your car has suffered catastrophic engine failure, severe structural rust, or total collision damage, you cannot simply abandon the vehicle or sell it for scrap without completing official title documentation. Legally junking a vehicle protects you from continuing tax liabilities, parking citations, and civil liabilities if the vehicle identification number (VIN) is later misused. By understanding title requirements, applying for a Junking Certificate when necessary, removing license plates, and obtaining quotes from licensed automotive recyclers, you can convert your scrap car into cash safely and legally.

Illinois Secretary of State Legal Framework and Title Rules

The state of Illinois maintains stringent automotive salvage regulations governed under the Illinois Vehicle Code (625 ILCS 5/). When a vehicle is disposed of for scrap metal, parts dismantling, or crushing, the owner must formally transfer vehicle ownership and notify the Vehicle Services Department. Under Illinois law, an automotive recycler or salvage yard cannot legally purchase or dismantle an automobile without obtaining an official Certificate of Title properly assigned to the buyer, or a state-issued Junking Certificate.

A Junking Certificate (Form VSD-167) is an official title document issued by the Illinois Secretary of State that permanently retires the vehicle title record. Once a vehicle receives an Illinois Junking Certificate, it can never again be titled, registered, or legally driven on any public roadway in the state of Illinois; it is legally classified exclusively for scrap, dismantling, or recycling. If you hold a clear, original Certificate of Title in your name free of outstanding bank liens, you can assign that title directly to a licensed automotive scrap buyer without applying for a separate Junking Certificate beforehand, as the buyer will file the junking transfer with the state.

What to Do if You Lost the Vehicle Title in Illinois

One of the most frequent hurdles vehicle owners encounter when attempting to junk an older vehicle is a missing, damaged, or lost Certificate of Title. In Illinois, you cannot legally transfer an automobile to a scrapyard simply by signing a generic bill of sale. If you have misplaced your title, you have two primary administrative solutions through the Illinois Secretary of State Vehicle Services Department. The standard route is applying for an Application for Duplicate Title (Form VSD-190) accompanied by the state statutory duplicate title filing fee of ninety-five dollars.

However, if your sole intention is to scrap the vehicle and it will never be driven again, you can apply directly for an Illinois Junking Certificate using Form VSD-167. Crucially, applying for a Junking Certificate through the Secretary of State is completely free of charge, allowing you to bypass the ninety-five-dollar duplicate title fee. You must complete the application, provide the vehicle identification number (VIN), year, make, and model, and submit documentation proving ownership (such as an expired registration card or insurance policy). Once issued, you provide this certificate directly to the salvage buyer.

Step-by-Step Execution: Preparing the Car, Plates, and Payout

Before the tow truck arrives to haul your junk car away, take thorough physical preparation steps. Conduct an exhaustive search through the cabin, glove compartment, trunk, under seats, and inside door pockets for personal belongings, tollway transponders (such as I-PASS), parking gate cards, and personal paperwork containing sensitive identity data. Remove all aftermarket electronics, custom stereos, or newly installed batteries and high-tread tires if you plan to sell them separately to maximize financial return.

Next, physically remove both front and rear Illinois license plates from the vehicle. In Illinois, license plates belong to the registered owner, not the automobile. Never allow a junkyard tow driver to haul away a vehicle with your license plates attached. You can transfer those plates to another vehicle you own for a nominal twenty-five-dollar state transfer fee, or dispose of them safely. Complete the Seller Report of Sale (Form VSD-703) online via the CyberDriveIllinois website or by mailing the tear-off stub from the bottom of your title to ensure you are legally released from all future liability.

How to Legally Scrap and Junk a Vehicle in the State of Illinois

A step-by-step procedure for preparing paperwork, removing plates, and selling an inoperable car to an Illinois salvage yard.

  1. Locate and Verify Vehicle Title and Lien Clearances

    Ensure you possess a clean Illinois Certificate of Title in your name, or apply for a free Illinois Junking Certificate (VSD-167) if the title is lost.

  2. Compare Scrap Quotes from Licensed Automotive Recyclers

    Contact at least three local licensed salvage yards, provide vehicle details and catalytic converter status, and confirm free towing.

  3. Clear Personal Items and Remove Illinois License Plates

    Empty all interior compartments, remove I-PASS transponders, and remove both front and rear license plates before the tow truck arrives.

  4. Execute Title Assignment and Collect Cash Payment

    Sign the seller section on the title, record odometer mileage, hand over the title to the licensed driver, and receive your payment receipt.

  5. File Seller Report of Sale and Cancel Auto Insurance

    Submit Form VSD-703 online with the Illinois Secretary of State to release legal liability, and notify your insurance carrier to cancel coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: Can you junk a car in Illinois without a title?

You cannot junk a car without proof of ownership; however, you can apply for a free Illinois Junking Certificate (Form VSD-167) from the Secretary of State.

Q2: How much is an Illinois Junking Certificate?

The Illinois Secretary of State issues a Junking Certificate completely free of charge, avoiding the standard duplicate title fee.

Q3: Do I keep my license plates when I junk a car in Illinois?

Yes, in Illinois, license plates stay with the owner and must be removed prior to towing; they can be transferred to another vehicle or surrendered.

Q4: What is Form VSD-703 in Illinois?

Form VSD-703 is the Seller Report of Sale, which notifies the Secretary of State that you transferred ownership, protecting you from future parking tickets.

Q5: How much money can you get for junking a car in Illinois?

Payouts typically range from 200 to 700 dollars depending on current scrap metal prices, vehicle weight, and the presence of an intact original catalytic converter.

Q6: Can you turn a car with an Illinois Junking Certificate back into a street-legal car?

No, once an Illinois Junking Certificate is issued, the vehicle title is permanently canceled and the vehicle can never again be registered or driven on public roads.

Q7: Will the junkyard pick up my vehicle for free in Illinois?

Most reputable licensed salvage yards and scrap companies offer free flatbed towing within their local operating radius as part of their purchase offer.

Q8: Should I cancel my car insurance before or after junking the car?

Cancel your insurance immediately after the vehicle has been picked up, title signed over, and plates physically removed from the car.

Final Thoughts & Key Takeaways

Junking an unwanted vehicle in Illinois is a straightforward, profitable process when you adhere to Secretary of State legal requirements. Whether you possess an original title or take advantage of the state free Junking Certificate program, securing proper documentation ensures complete legal protection against future liabilities. By removing your I-PASS transponder and license plates, filing your Seller Report of Sale, and comparing scrap quotes from licensed auto recyclers, you can easily clear your driveway and receive top dollar for your scrap vehicle.
