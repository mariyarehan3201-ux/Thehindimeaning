Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What is a specialized municipal asbestos docket?

A dedicated court division within a metropolitan trial court specifically established to manage and resolve complex asbestos personal injury lawsuits under uniform rules.

Q2: What is an accelerated living trial docket?

A fast-track court calendar that prioritizes living mesothelioma patients, granting expedited discovery and setting trial dates within 6 to 12 months.

Q3: Can I file my asbestos lawsuit in a different state than where I live?

Yes. If your exposure occurred in another state, or if the defendant corporation is headquartered there, your attorney can often file in that jurisdiction if laws are more favorable.

Q4: What is a Case Management Order (CMO)?

A master procedural order established by the supervising judge that dictates all deadlines, discovery protocols, and trial guidelines for asbestos cases on that docket.

Q5: How does joint and several liability affect my settlement?

In joint-and-several liability jurisdictions, any defendant found partially liable can be required to pay the entire verdict if other defendants are bankrupt, maximizing recovery.

Q6: What happens if a plaintiff passes away before trial?

The lawsuit transitions into a wrongful death claim brought by the surviving spouse or estate representative, and previous depositions are used as evidence at trial.

Q7: Why are so many asbestos lawsuits filed in Madison County or NYCAL?

These jurisdictions have established streamlined dockets, experienced judges, favorable discovery rules, and proven records of substantial plaintiff jury verdicts.

Q8: How long does an asbestos trial usually last?

If a case proceeds to trial rather than settling, jury selection and arguments typically take between two and four weeks.
