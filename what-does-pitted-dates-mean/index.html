What Does Pitted Dates Mean?

Botanical Structure and Industrial Pitting of Date Fruit

Dates are the sweet, edible drupe fruits produced by the date palm tree (Phoenix dactylifera), cultivated for millennia across the Middle East, North Africa, and the Coachella Valley of California. Botanically classified as a single-seeded stone fruit or drupe, a whole fresh or dried date naturally houses an elongated, rock-hard inner seed known as the pit, stone, or kernel.

When a package is labeled pitted dates, it signifies that the inedible central wooden seed has been intentionally removed during commercial food processing. While artisan growers remove pits manually with specialized hand knives, large-scale commercial packing houses utilize automated high-speed mechanical destoning machines that secure each date, plunge an industrial coring pin through the longitudinal center, and push the hard stone out through the base.

Culinary Applications, Natural Sweetening, and Nutritional Profile

Pitted dates—predominantly Medjool and Deglet Noor cultivars—have surged in popularity as unrefined, whole-food natural sweeteners. In plant-based baking, raw vegan desserts, and athletic nutrition, pureed pitted dates serve as nutrient-dense alternatives to refined cane sugar and high-fructose corn syrup.

Nutritionally, pitted dates are rich in dietary soluble fiber, potassium, magnesium, copper, and polyphenolic antioxidants. Because their natural sugars (fructose and glucose) are structurally bound within intact fruit dietary fiber, pitted dates possess a low to moderate glycemic index (GI), providing sustained physical energy without triggering sharp postprandial glucose spikes.

How to Inspect and Use Pitted Dates Safely in the Kitchen

A four-step culinary guide to prepping and inspecting commercially pitted dates before consuming or blending.

  1. Inspect and Squeeze for Residual Pit Fragments

    Before dropping pitted dates into a high-speed blender or chewing quickly, gently squeeze each date between your fingers to verify no stone fragments remain.

  2. Inspect the Internal Hollow Cavity

    Occasionally pull open the date seam to check the inner cavity for black powdery organic dust (natural Aspergillus fungus) and discard any affected fruit.

  3. Rehydrate Firm Dates in Warm Water

    If dried Deglet Noor dates have become stiff, soak them in hot water for 10 minutes before pureeing into smooth, lump-free date paste.

  4. Store in Airtight Glass Jars in the Refrigerator

    Keep pitted dates stored in sealed airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 6 months or freeze for up to a full year to maintain optimal softness.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: What does pitted dates mean in simple terms?

Pitted dates means the hard, inedible wooden seed or stone inside the center of the date fruit has been completely removed.

Q2: Can pitted dates still contain a pit or seed?

Yes. Automated commercial pitting machines are not 100% perfect; packaging labels routinely warn that occasional pits or pit fragments may remain.

Q3: Can you eat the pit of a date?

No. Date pits are rock-hard, non-digestible, and pose a severe choking hazard and dental fracture risk if bitten.

Q4: What is the difference between Medjool and Deglet Noor pitted dates?

Medjool dates are larger, softer, and richer in caramel flavor; Deglet Noor dates are firmer, drier, and easier to dice for baking.

Q5: Do pitted dates expire or go bad?

Pitted dates have a long shelf-life of 6 to 12 months, but will eventually dry out, develop sugar crystallization, or rarely develop mold if exposed to moisture.

Q6: Why do people use pitted dates instead of sugar?

Pitted dates provide natural sweetness alongside dietary fiber, potassium, and antioxidants, avoiding the empty calories of refined white sugar.

Q7: What is the black powder sometimes found inside a pitted date?

Black powdery dust inside a date is a harmless natural fungus (Aspergillus niger) that occasionally affects fruit on the palm; simply discard affected dates.

Q8: How are dates pitted in commercial factories?

Automated industrial destoning machines align each date and push an automated pneumatic metal rod through the fruit, pushing the seed out cleanly.
