Does a Range Rover Have a Third Row?

Q&A
Published by TheHindiMeaning Admin•Updated: September 12, 2026

For over fifty years, the flagship Land Rover Range Rover has reigned as the ultimate benchmark of ultra-luxury sport utility vehicles, combining aristocratic British craftsmanship with world-class all-terrain capability. Historically, the full-size Range Rover was exclusively a two-row sanctuary for five privileged occupants. However, luxury buyers frequently ask whether the Range Rover offers a third-row seat. The answer is yes: starting with the fifth-generation redesign, the full-size Range Rover offers an optional third-row seat on its Long Wheelbase (LWB) model, seating seven adults in sublime comfort.

The Landmark Fifth-Generation (L460) Redesign

Prior to the 2022 model year, families seeking three rows of seating within the Land Rover brand were forced to step down to the Land Rover Discovery or the smaller Range Rover Sport. That limitation was permanently resolved with the launch of the fifth-generation (L460) Range Rover. Built on Land Rover's flexible Modular Longitudinal Architecture (MLA-Flex), the new platform was engineered from the ground up to accommodate three luxurious rows of seating.

To enjoy third-row seating in the flagship Range Rover, buyers must select the Long Wheelbase (LWB) body style. The standard Short Wheelbase (SWB) model remains strictly a two-row five-passenger configuration. The LWB stretches the wheelbase by 7.9 inches (to 126.0 inches) and extends total vehicle length to 207 inches, providing the expansive cabin footprint necessary to house full-size adult seating in the rear.

Uncompromising Luxury: Third-Row Passenger Amenities

While many mid-size luxury crossovers feature third rows that feel like punishment benches for adults, Land Rover engineered the Range Rover LWB third row to provide true executive-class accommodation. The third row delivers an impressive 34.0 inches of legroom—comparable to the second row of many mid-size sedans—and features deeply cushioned, stadium-style elevated seats that provide genuine under-thigh support.

Third-row passengers in the Range Rover LWB are treated to the same lavish appointments as front occupants. The power-folding third-row seats are wrapped in semi-aniline leather, feature independent heated cushions, and offer dedicated USB-C fast-charging ports, cup holders, and individual air conditioning climate zones controlled through elegant pillar-mounted digital dials.

Third-Row Availability Across the Land Rover Family

If the flagship full-size Range Rover LWB exceeds your budget or garage dimensions, the Land Rover family offers several alternative models equipped with third-row seating. The dynamic Range Rover Sport offers an optional '5+2' seating package on select models, though its third row is compact and intended primarily for children or occasional emergency trips.

For buyers prioritizing maximum family hauling and rugged utility, the Land Rover Defender 130 stretches the iconic off-roader to provide full eight-passenger seating across three full-size rows. Meanwhile, the family-focused Land Rover Discovery provides full seven-passenger stadium seating with intelligent smartphone-controlled seat folding, offering versatile alternatives across different price points.

How to Order a Three-Row Range Rover in 5 Steps

Follow these five steps to specify and order a seven-passenger full-size Range Rover tailored to your family's needs.

  1. Select the Long Wheelbase (LWB) Platform

    Ensure your dealership build specifies the Long Wheelbase body, as the Standard Wheelbase (SWB) cannot be fitted with three rows.

  2. Choose the 7-Seat Seating Option

    Select the '7 Seats' configuration in the Land Rover online configurator, which equips the power-folding heated third row.

  3. Select Your Preferred Powertrain

    Choose between the smooth P400 turbocharged mild-hybrid inline-six or the formidable P530 4.4-liter twin-turbo V8.

  4. Upgrade to 4-Zone or 5-Zone Climate Control

    Option the advanced multi-zone climate package to ensure dedicated digital temperature controls for third-row passengers.

  5. Confirm Garage Dimensions Before Delivery

    Measure your home garage to ensure it can comfortably accommodate the Range Rover LWB's 207-inch overall length.

Frequently Asked Questions (8 Questions Answered)

Q1: When did Range Rover first get a third row?

Land Rover officially introduced the third-row seating option on the full-size Range Rover with the fifth-generation (L460) redesign for the 2022 model year.

Q2: Can adults sit comfortably in the Range Rover third row?

Yes, with 34.0 inches of legroom, generous headroom, and heated cushions, the LWB third row comfortably accommodates adults on long road trips.

Q3: Does the Range Rover Sport have a third row?

The Range Rover Sport offers an optional '5+2' third row, though it is smaller than the full-size LWB and best suited for children.

Q4: How do the third-row seats fold down in a Range Rover?

The third-row seats are fully motorized; pressing buttons in the rear cargo area or on the central Pivi Pro touchscreen folds them flat into the floor.

Q5: Does the SV trim have a third row?

No, the ultra-exclusive Range Rover SV is configured exclusively as a four-seat or five-seat vehicle focusing on maximum executive rear legroom.

Q6: How much cargo room is behind the Range Rover third row?

With all three rows upright, the Range Rover LWB provides roughly 8.7 cubic feet of cargo volume, expanding to 43.1 cubic feet with the third row folded.

Q7: Can child car seats fit in the Range Rover third row?

Yes, the third row features ISOFIX / LATCH anchors and top tethers to securely mount child restraint seats.

Q8: Is the Defender 130 bigger than the Range Rover LWB?

The Defender 130 is slightly longer (211 inches with spare tire) and seats eight, while the Range Rover LWB seats seven with significantly higher luxury.
